What you get

One zip per programme, ready to download as soon as you pay.

Every version

The zip has every version of that programme. Upper Lower, for example, has 15: 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 days a week at each of the three levels.

A PDF for each version

Week by week tables with sets, reps, rest, two swaps for every exercise and a coaching note for each one. Plus your week at a glance, how the blocks run, how to progress and how to warm up.

A spreadsheet for each version

Every week on its own tab. Pick your swap from a drop down, write in your weights and reps, and the Progress tab shows your heaviest weight each week. Opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.

Blocks that change

8 weeks at Beginner and Advanced, 10 at Intermediate, in 2 or 3 blocks with a lighter week at the end. In the second half most exercises change to a different variation, and the staple lifts stay and get heavier.

Volume checked

We checked the weekly sets for each muscle in every version against the research on training volume, and set them for each level, with sessions kept to a sensible length.

Yours to keep

Paid once, no subscription. We also email you a download link that works for 7 days, so you can get it on another device.

See inside

Real pages from the Full Body PDF, 3 days a week at Intermediate.

Questions

How do I get my programme?

Pay with Stripe and you come straight back to a page with your download button. We also email you a link that works for 7 days. Save the zip to your phone, tablet or computer once it finishes.

What is in the zip?

Two folders, PDF and Spreadsheets, with one PDF and one spreadsheet for every version of that programme. The file names say which version is which, for example full-body-3-intermediate is 3 days a week at Intermediate.

Which version should I use?

You get them all, so start with the days you can train most weeks. Beginner suits you if you are new to lifting or coming back after a long break. You can move to another version later without buying again.

How long does each one run?

8 weeks at Beginner and Advanced, and 10 weeks at Intermediate. Each runs in 2 or 3 blocks and finishes with a lighter week, so you recover before your next programme.

Do I need a gym?

Each card says the kit. The home programmes need dumbbells and a bench, or nothing at all. Every exercise also has two swaps, often with less kit, so you can work around a busy gym or a missing machine.

Will the spreadsheets work on my phone or Mac?

Yes. They open in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers. On an iPhone, open the zip in the Files app first. The PDFs open on any phone, tablet or computer, and print on A4.

I lost my download link

Reply to the email we sent you, or write to admintotality@gmail.com from the email you paid with, and we will send you a new link.

Can I get a refund?

The download is yours as soon as you pay, so purchases are generally non-refundable. If a file does not work or you were charged twice, email admintotality@gmail.com and we will fix it or refund you. Nothing here limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Who sells them?

Totality 360 Pty Ltd (ABN 58 698 902 975), the team behind Totality Training. Payments are taken by Stripe in Australian dollars.

If you have an injury or a health condition, check with your doctor or physio before you start, and stop any exercise that causes sharp pain.
