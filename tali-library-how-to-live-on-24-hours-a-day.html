The Daily Miracle

"Yes, he's one of those men that don't know how to manage. Good situation. Regular income. Quite enough for luxuries as well as needs. Not really extravagant. And yet the fellow's always in difficulties. Somehow he gets nothing out of his money."

You wake up in the morning, and lo! your purse is magically filled with twenty-four hours of the unmanufactured tissue of the universe of your life! It is yours. It is the most precious of possessions. No one can take it from you. It is unstealable. And no one receives either more or less than you receive.

Talk about an ideal democracy! In the realm of time there is no aristocracy of wealth, and no aristocracy of intellect. Genius is never rewarded by even an extra hour a day.

What Tali kept

45 things from this book went into her brain. Here are five.

Arnold Bennett held that we never get more time, because we have always had all the time there is. Putting off the things that matter until we have more time is the habit to break.
Arnold Bennett held that you simply begin, since waiting for a better moment only makes the start harder, and that a clear time-table on paper will not do the work for you.
Arnold Bennett advised starting small and quietly, because a failed grand plan damages self-respect, which is the root of all purposefulness.
Arnold Bennett noticed that people who have something definite to look forward to in the evening find more energy and vitality through the whole day, even when they are tired.
Arnold Bennett held that the way to gain time is to prepare the night before, so the morning starts with no obstacles.
