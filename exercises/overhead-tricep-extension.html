Overhead Tricep Extension: how to do it and muscles worked

Extending weight overhead from behind head stretching triceps

WorksTriceps, Long Head
EquipmentDumbbell
LevelIntermediate
Good forMuscle growth

Why it works

Overhead position maximally stretches the long head of the tricep, which makes up the bulk of tricep size. The most effective long head tricep exercise.

How to do it

  1. Standing/seated upright
  2. Weight behind head
  3. Full extension overhead
  4. Control eccentric

Coaching cues

  • Standing or seated position
  • Weight behind head
  • Explosive extension overhead
  • Full lockout
  • Control eccentric
  • Single or bilateral
  • Feel tricep contract
  • Light to moderate weight

Sets and reps

A starting point. Adjust to the person in front of you.

GoalReps
Muscle growth10-15, Accessory: 12-20, Volume: 15-25, Isolation: 15-20

Questions people ask

What muscles does the overhead tricep extension work?

Mainly the triceps and long head.

How many sets and reps should I do?

Muscle growth: 10-15, Accessory: 12-20, Volume: 15-25, Isolation: 15-20.

What equipment do I need?

Dumbbell.

Is it good for beginners?

It’s rated intermediate. Learn the basic version of the movement first, and start light.
