Overhead Barbell Tricep Extension: how to do it and muscles worked

Overhead Barbell Tricep Extension: a targeted exercise for developing the Triceps and Long Head Tricep.

WorksTriceps, Long Head Tricep
EquipmentBarbell
LevelIntermediate
Good forMuscle growth

Why it works

Overhead position maximally stretches the long head of the tricep, which makes up the bulk of tricep size. The most effective long head tricep exercise.

Coaching cues

  • Focus on feeling the target muscle, not just moving the weight
  • Control the negative: slow eccentric builds more muscle
  • Full range of motion unless mobility is limiting
  • Breathe out on the effort phase
  • Leave 1-2 reps in reserve to maintain form

Sets and reps

A starting point. Adjust to the person in front of you.

GoalReps
Strength4-6 reps
Muscle growth8-15 reps
Endurance15-25 reps

3-4 sets.

Questions people ask

What muscles does the overhead barbell tricep extension work?

Mainly the triceps and long head tricep.

How many sets and reps should I do?

Strength: 4-6 reps; Muscle growth: 8-15 reps; Endurance: 15-25 reps. 3-4 sets

What equipment do I need?

Barbell.

Is it good for beginners?

It’s rated intermediate. Learn the basic version of the movement first, and start light.
