Dumbbell Russian Twist: how to do it and muscles worked

Rotational twist holding weight side to side It works the obliques and core.

WorksObliques, Core
EquipmentDumbbell
LevelIntermediate
Good forStrength, Muscle growth, Endurance

Why it works

Trains the obliques through rotation or lateral flexion, developing core rotational power and waist definition.

How to do it

  1. Seated position
  2. Rotation side to side
  3. Weight at chest
  4. Full ROM

Coaching cues

  • Seated position
  • Weight held at chest
  • Explosive rotation side to side
  • Full ROM
  • Control tempo
  • Light to moderate weight
  • Great rotational and oblique builder
  • Dynamic movement

Sets and reps

A starting point. Adjust to the person in front of you.

GoalReps
Muscle growth12-20, Rotation: 15-25, Volume: 20-30, Endurance: 30-50

Questions people ask

What muscles does the dumbbell russian twist work?

Mainly the obliques and core.

How many sets and reps should I do?

Muscle growth: 12-20, Rotation: 15-25, Volume: 20-30, Endurance: 30-50.

What equipment do I need?

Dumbbell.

Is it good for beginners?

It’s rated intermediate. Learn the basic version of the movement first, and start light.
