Lever Hip Abduction: how to do it and muscles worked

A lever machine where you push your legs out against pads. It isolates the outer hips. It works the glute medius, abductors and glutes.

WorksGlute Medius, Abductors, Glutes
EquipmentMachine
LevelBeginner
Good forMuscle growth

Why it works

It isolates the glute medius, which helps keep the knees aligned.

How to do it

  1. Push out under control
  2. Pause and return slowly

Coaching cues

  • Push your legs out against the pads
  • Pause at the end
  • Return slowly
  • Keep your torso still

Sets and reps

A starting point. Adjust to the person in front of you.

GoalReps
Muscle growth12-20
