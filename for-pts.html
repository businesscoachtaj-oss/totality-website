Totality for personal trainers

For solo PTs who coach in person, online, or both. Write the programme, run the session, answer check-ins and get paid, from one app on your phone or the web.

Start your 7 day free trial See Solo prices

Sound familiar?

Programmes in one app, chat in another, payments in a third

Totality keeps programmes, sessions, check-ins, messages and payments together, so you stop copying things between apps.

Sunday nights lost to check-ins

Check-ins land in one list. Tali drafts a reply from what the client wrote, and you edit and send.

Clients who drift off

When a client has not opened the app for 14 days, their profile tells you, and Tali can draft a message to bring them back.

What you get on every Solo plan

The same tools on every Solo plan. You choose a plan by how many clients you coach.

Today card

Today's booked sessions with a one-tap Start that opens the client's session ready to log.

Programmes and auto-regulation

Build programmes from the exercise library. Totality flags when a client should add load, reduce it or deload.

Check-ins and history

Clients send check-ins and see their own trends, PBs and progress photos, measurements, weight and strength.

Chat, templates and broadcasts

Live chat, saved message templates, and one message to all clients when you need it.

Bookings

Appointments and recurring bookings, so regulars are booked in for the weeks ahead.

Nutrition for clients

Clients log food, track water and see a full nutrient breakdown against Australian recommended intakes.

Get paid POS add-on

Take payments and sell PT packages that add session credits, through your own Stripe account. No commission on your coaching revenue.

Reports

See your revenue and sessions over time.

Tali AI

Drafts check-in replies, win-back messages and programmes. See what Tali does.
