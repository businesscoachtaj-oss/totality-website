Tali HQ · for your life and your work

A mind that grows with you.

Tali is an AI who remembers. She keeps your days, your people and your business in order, learns a little more about you every week, and never sends, spends or changes anything without your yes.

Your time

About four thousand weeks.

That is roughly how long an average life runs. Drawn one square a week, it fits on a single page, and it becomes easy to see how many of those squares go on inboxes, invoices and trying to remember what you meant to do.

Tali takes on that admin. She sorts the email and drafts the replies, keeps track of who owes what, holds your dates and lists, and hands you a short brief in the morning. You decide what goes out. The weeks you get back are yours to spend on the people and the work that matter.

In the app, add your birthday and your own weeks fill in, a new square every week. Based on an average Australian life of about 83 years (ABS life tables, 2021 to 2023).

Her brain

A brain that grows with you.

Tali files what she learns into parts of a brain, a bit like yours does: the people in your life, how you like things done, your plans, and the lessons along the way. Spin it, and tap a part to see what she keeps there.

Drag to spin. Tap a part.

A demo with made up memories. Your own Tali starts with an empty brain and fills it as you talk. You can see and delete anything she keeps.

Living well FREE

Reminders worth keeping.

A few parts of Tali HQ are there to help you notice how much time you have, and to spend it on purpose. They are free on every plan.

  • Your timeYour weeks, summers and mornings left on the averages. Add the people you love and how often you see them, and Tali counts roughly how many more times you'll be together. For a mum of 64 you see once a month, it comes to around 260.
  • Your philosophyFollow Stoicism, Epicureanism, Buddhism, Taoism, Confucianism, Existentialism or Transcendentalism. Each comes with the idea in plain words, three lessons, things to try this week, what to read first from Tali's library, and further reading.
  • A thought for todayEach morning Today shows one quote from the schools you follow. Keep it, or put it away until tomorrow.
  • Shut the day downTwo minutes in the evening: how today went, one thing that went well, and tomorrow's three. Then her night sky, and sleep sounds that fade out.

Both sides of your week

One app for the life and the work.

Tali HQ is its own app. You don't need any other app or subscription, and there's nothing to connect to get started.

  • A morning briefWhat came in overnight, who needs a reply, what's on today, and one nudge on your health or goals.
  • Clients and invoicesYour clients and leads in one place. Tali drafts the follow-ups, replies and invoices. Tali HQ Business adds quotes, bookings, a pipeline and a website.
  • Food, training and goalsLog by telling Tali. She finds gaps in your day to train and checks your goals with you every Friday.
  • Your journalA private journal, with a look back to what you wrote a month or a year ago. Tali reads it, learns what matters to you, and you can see and delete anything she keeps.
  • Your people and datesFamily, friends, clients and leads in one place. Tell her a birthday once and she keeps it, and tells you who you haven't caught up with.
  • Your lists and your own moneyTo do, check ups, renewals and habits with a streak. On the paid plans, what's left to spend this week and the bills that repeat.
  • Her libraryEach night Tali reads a few pages of the classics and keeps what is still worth knowing. Finished books open for you to read too.
  • Night schoolOn Tali HQ Pro, Tali studies what you pick and gets better at the job. She can search the web too, and shows her sources.

Nothing happens without your yes.

Tali drafts and you decide. She never sends a message, an invoice or a booking until you approve it, and she never asks for your passwords.

Questions

Where do I get Tali HQ?

It's on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. It also runs in your browser at talihq.com.au, on a phone or a computer. The same account works on all three.

What does it cost?

Free, Tali HQ at A$19.99 a month, Tali HQ Pro at A$49.99 a month, or Tali HQ Max at A$149.99 a month. The paid plans can also be paid yearly. Tali HQ Business is A$199.99 a month, monthly only. See the plans.

What is Tali HQ Business?

The plan for running a business from one place. You get everything in Tali HQ Max, plus jobs and quotes, a pipeline, bookings with a link your customers book from, projects, a hosted website, On autopilot and a bookkeeping pack for your accountant. It is A$199.99 a month.

Can I have a website on my own domain?

Yes. Tali HQ hosts a simple site for you at talihq.com.au/site/your-name. A personal site comes with any paid plan and a business site comes with Tali HQ Business. Putting it on your own domain is A$79 once.

What are Your time and Your philosophy?

Two free parts of Tali HQ for the life side. Your time counts your weeks on an average Australian life (ABS life tables), plus your summers, your mornings and roughly how many more times you'll see the people you love. Your philosophy lets you follow schools like Stoicism or Taoism, with their key texts, lessons, things to try and a quote each morning. Every number is an average, and the quotes come word for word from public domain translations.

Do I need Totality or Totality Nutrition?

No. Tali HQ is its own app with its own sign-up. You don't need any other app to use it.

Who makes Tali HQ?

Totality 360, the Australian team behind Totality and Totality Nutrition. Tali is the same AI that already works inside both apps.
