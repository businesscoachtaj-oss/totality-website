Questions

Where do I get Tali HQ?

It's live now as a web app at talihq.com.au. It works on iPhone, Android and computer. On your phone, add it to your home screen and it works like any other app. The App Store and Google Play versions are coming soon.

What does it cost?

Free, Tali HQ at A$19.99 a month, Tali HQ Pro at A$49.99 a month, or Tali HQ Max at A$149.99 a month, with 15% off if you pay yearly. See the plans.

Do I need Totality or Totality Nutrition?

No. Tali HQ is its own app with its own sign-up. You don't need any other app to use it.

Who makes Tali HQ?

Totality 360, the Australian team behind Totality and Totality Nutrition. Tali is the same AI that already works inside both apps.
