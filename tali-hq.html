Questions

Where do I get Tali HQ?

It's live now as a web app at talihq.com.au. It works on iPhone, Android and computer. On your phone, add it to your home screen and it works like any other app. The App Store and Google Play versions are coming soon.

What does it cost?

Free, Tali HQ at A$19.99 a month, Tali HQ Pro at A$49.99 a month, or Tali HQ Max at A$149.99 a month, with 15% off if you pay yearly. Tali HQ Business is A$199.99 a month, monthly only. See the plans.

What is Tali HQ Business?

The plan for running a business from one place. You get everything in Tali HQ Max, plus jobs and quotes, a pipeline, bookings with a link your customers book from, projects, a hosted website, On autopilot and a bookkeeping pack for your accountant. It is A$199.99 a month.

Can I have a website on my own domain?

Yes. Tali HQ hosts a simple site for you at talihq.com.au/site/your-name. A personal site comes with any paid plan and a business site comes with Tali HQ Business. Putting it on your own domain is A$79 once.

Do I need Totality or Totality Nutrition?

No. Tali HQ is its own app with its own sign-up. You don't need any other app to use it.

Who makes Tali HQ?

Totality 360, the Australian team behind Totality and Totality Nutrition. Tali is the same AI that already works inside both apps.
