Sign in with Totality

Let your members and clients sign in to your website with the Totality account they already use for their training, bookings and nutrition. No new password for them, and no login system for you to build.

How it works

  1. 1. They tap the buttonOn your website, for example to see their bookings or member area.
  2. 2. They say yes in TotalityTotality shows your business name and exactly what you will see. They tap Allow.
  3. 3. They are signed inBack on your website, signed in with their name and email. Next time it is one tap.

What your website gets

  • Who they are, so they can sign in
  • Their email address
  • Their name and profile photo, if you ask for it

What it never gets

  • Their Totality password
  • Their training, nutrition, health or payment details
  • Anything they have not said yes to. They can remove your website any time in Settings, under Connected apps.

Add it to your website

Sign in with Totality uses OpenID Connect, the same standard as Sign in with Google, so most website builders and login tools can add it in a few minutes. Send us your website address and the page people should come back to, and we will send you your client ID.
