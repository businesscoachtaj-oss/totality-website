Common questions

How do I pick my kind of studio?

At sign up, after solo or studio owner, Totality asks what you do. Pick the closest. You can change it later in Settings, under What kind of business is this?

We run classes and PT. Which do I pick?

Pick Gym, which keeps classes, programmes and session packs all on. Or pick your studio type and switch Programmes and Session packs on in Settings.

Does changing the type delete anything?

No. It changes which tabs and menus show and what they are called. Members, classes, payments and history stay as they are.

Do my members pay for the app?

No. Members get the Totality app free to book classes, see what is coming up and pay.
