What's new

Release notes for Totality and Totality Nutrition. Update the app from the App Store or Google Play to get the latest version. The web apps update by themselves.

22 Oct 2026

Release notes

Totality For clients

  • A new Today section on Home: your workout, a check-in when one is due, your next session and the latest update from your coach.
  • Water tracking against a daily target.
  • A nutrition targets calculator.
  • A full nutrient breakdown, with vitamins and minerals measured against Australian recommended intakes.
  • Workout history with your personal bests marked.
  • Check-in history with trends.
  • Progress now has tabs for photos, measurements, weight and strength.
  • A payments hub for purchases, receipts, PT credits and packages.
  • Cancel your own membership from the app.
  • Join a waitlist when a class is full.
  • If a chat message fails to send, retry it with one tap.

Totality For trainers

  • A Today card with your booked sessions and a one-tap Start.
  • Live chat with clients.
  • Broadcast a message to all your clients.
  • Message templates.
  • Archive clients, and search and sort your client list.
  • PT packages that add session credits when a client buys them.
  • Reports for revenue and sessions.
  • See your gym's member list.
  • Recurring bookings.
  • Tali AI buttons show how many credits they use before you tap.

Totality For gyms

  • A Needs attention dashboard: failed payments, expiring memberships, check-ins waiting, leads to follow up and classes that are full or nearly empty.
  • Front desk check-in.
  • Class rosters with attendance and no-shows, plus waitlists.
  • Membership freezes that pause Stripe billing.
  • Failed payments with retry and an update-card link for the member.
  • Staff rosters.
  • Group announcements by push, email and in-app.
  • Revenue by product and by trainer, with CSV export.
  • Cancelling a class refunds and notifies booked members.
  • The Charge screen now shows the real result of each payment.
  • Payroll with Xero export.
  • Improvements to classes, the leads CRM, compliance and imports from Trainerize, Mindbody and PTminder.

Totality For everyone

  • Notification settings with quiet hours.

Totality Nutrition

  • Australian food data from AFCD, plus Open Food Facts, with the source shown on every food.
  • A full nutrient breakdown with vitamins and minerals.
  • Logging streaks with milestone celebrations. One missed day a week is forgiven, your streak runs to 11:59pm, and an evening reminder helps you keep it.
  • A new grocery list: shopping windows, ingredients combined by aisle, pantry staples, tick off as you shop, and share.
  • Hydration and progress, rebuilt.
  • Referral links.
20 Oct 2026

Tali AI

Tali AI arrives in both apps

  • Totality: Tali drafts check-in replies, win-back messages, lead follow-ups and programmes. Credits come with every plan.
  • Totality Nutrition: describe a meal, import any recipe, meal plans and weekly summaries on the new Nutrition + AI plan.
  • On the web apps now. Totality on Android and iOS from 5 Nov 2026. Totality Nutrition on Android from 5 Nov 2026, iPhone coming soon.
