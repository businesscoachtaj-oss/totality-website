Totality Training

A workout tracker with programmes that adjust as you train

Totality Training is a workout app for people who train on their own. Pick from 375 programmes, have Tali build one, or make your own. Log every set, and the app moves your weights and your sets from how you actually trained. Free to use, or from A$8.99 a month for everything.

Last updated 22 November 2026

Start free See plans

On Google Play and the web now. The iPhone app is with Apple for review, and the web app works in the browser on any phone until then. Your first 7 days are on the full plan, no card.

What does Totality Training do?

The same workout engine trainers use with their clients in Totality, set up for someone training alone.

99 programmes, picked in four questions

Say where you train, how many days, what you want to focus on and how long you have been lifting. You get the programmes that fit: full body, upper lower, push pull legs, glutes, strength, lean and fit, home dumbbell and no equipment.

Weights that move by themselves

Hit the top of the rep range on every set two sessions running and the weight goes up, filled in for your next workout. Miss the range twice at full effort and it eases back. A lift that stalls for four sessions gets a new rep target.

Sets that adjust to how each muscle feels

After a workout, three taps for each muscle group: how sore you got, the pump, and how hard it felt. Next week that muscle gets a set added, kept the same or taken off. Every programme ends with a lighter week.

Your programmes, yours to change

Everything you start or have built is saved under My programmes. Open any workout to swap, add or remove exercises and change sets, reps and rest, for this week only or every week from now. Build your own routines too.

Tali builds and reviews it

On Training + AI, Tali writes a programme around your week, your kit and your goal, gives her take after each workout and writes up your week. On Free you can ask her 3 questions a day.

Works with Totality Nutrition

Same login. Today's food shows on your Training home screen, and your weight, measurements and photos are shared. Pay for one app and the other is 40% off.

Common questions

How much does Totality Training cost?

There is a Free plan at A$0. Training is A$8.99 a month or A$91.70 a year. Training + AI is A$11.99 a month or A$122.30 a year. Prices are in Australian dollars. Every new account gets 7 days of everything without a card.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Free is A$0 for good: log every workout, the full exercise library with demos, 3 routines of your own, the Strength Foundations programme, 3 questions for Tali a day and sleep sounds.

Is Totality Training on iPhone and Android?

It is on the web now and works in the browser on any phone. The iPhone and Android apps were sent to Apple and Google for review on 21 November 2026.

How do the weights change by themselves?

When you hit the top of the rep range on every set two sessions running, the weight goes up and is filled in for your next workout. If you fall under the range twice and it felt like a 10 out of 10, it eases back. If a lift sits at the same weight and reps for four sessions, the rep target changes. This is on Training and Training + AI, not Free.

How do the sets change?

After a workout you rate each muscle group you trained with three taps: how sore you got, the pump, and how hard it felt. From that, next week that muscle gets one set added, kept the same, or one set taken off. Every programme also ends with a lighter week.

Do I need Totality Nutrition as well?

No. They are separate apps on the same login. If you use both, today's food shows on your Training home screen and your weight, measurements and photos are shared. If you pay for one, the other is 40% off.

Can I work with a coach instead?

Yes. Totality is the app trainers and gyms use with their clients. The same weight and set changes go to your trainer to approve there.
