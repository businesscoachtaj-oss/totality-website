{{HEADER:Steps 1 and 2: invite them}}
Step by step

Invite a client

1Send the invite

  1. Tap Invite on the Clients tab, or the Invite Client tile on Home.
  2. Type their Full Name and Email.
  3. Tap Invite Client.
  4. Client Invited! shows their temporary password and "Invite email sent to" their address. Tap Done.
If the email doesn't send: the account is still made. Tap Share Credentials and send the login yourself by text or any app.

At your plan's client limit you'll see a message instead of a new client. Archiving a client you no longer coach frees a spot straight away.

1

2What your client does the first time

About five minutes, once only.

  1. Opens the email from Totality and logs in with the temporary password, then sets their own.
  2. Agrees to the terms (they must be 18 or older).
  3. Answers five setup questions: units, main goal, experience and training days, body stats, and injuries and training style.
  4. Sets nutrition targets, or taps Skip for now so you can set them.
  5. Takes Tali's short tour of the Home screen, or skips it.
You see their answers on their profile: goal, experience, injuries and preferred training style. Read them before you build their programme.

{{HEADER:Steps 3 and 4: sessions and programmes}}

3Bring across the sessions they've paid for

  1. Open the client, go to the Money tab and tap Add sessions on the Sessions card.
  2. Under How many, tap 1, 5, 10 or 20, or use plus and minus.
  3. Under How they paid, pick Cash, Bank transfer, Another app or No charge.
  4. Add the Amount paid if you want it recorded (optional), and how long they have to use them: 3, 6 or 12 months.
  5. Tap Add 10 sessions (the number you picked). The client sees their sessions left on their Home straight away.
Then: each booked session uses one. If they run out, new bookings show as Pending Settlement until they pay again.
3

4Give them their programme

  1. Open My Programmes from your Home shortcuts and tap + New. Name it, set the weeks, tap Create.
  2. Add sessions to each week, then exercises to each session.
  3. Tap Functions, then Assign to Client. Pick a start date (Today, Next Monday or a date), tick the clients and tap Confirm.
Already have programmes written? Build each one once and assign it to everyone who's on it. The Coaching guide covers the builder in detail.
4
