{{HEADER:Steps 1 to 3: account, team, members}}

1Create your account

  1. Get Totality from the App Store or Google Play, or go to totalityapp.com.au. Tap Get Started: It's Free.
  2. Fill in your name, email and a password, then Create Account.
  3. Under Are you solo or a studio owner? pick Studio owner. Under What do you do? pick the closest, for example Pilates. It sets your layout and you can change it in Settings. Type your business name and tap Let's Go.
  4. Add your address, phone and email (or Skip), accept the terms and take Tali's one minute tour.
Your dashboard then shows a setup card with your first jobs, such as adding your timetable, your members and connecting payments. Each ticks itself off when done.
1

2Invite your trainers

  1. Open More then Staff then Trainers and tap + Invite.
  2. Type their Full Name and Email, then Invite Trainer.
  3. They get an email with their login. If they already use Totality, they accept your invite in their app instead.

Trainers see their own clients, the members list, the schedule and their shifts. They can't see your billing, payroll, revenue or settings.

2

3Bring your members across

  1. Export your members from {{from_system}} as CSV or Excel.
  2. Open Members then Import data. Pick Clients, upload the file, check the columns are matched (Full Name and Email are required), then tap Import with your number of records, for example Import 48 Records.
  3. For a few members, use Add client instead: name, email, Invite Client.
Heads up: every new member gets their login email as soon as the import runs. Tell your members it's coming before you import.
3
{{FOOTER:Screens show demo data}}
{{HEADER:Steps 4 to 6: payments, memberships, timetable}}

4Connect Stripe and pick your debit day

  1. Open the Sales tab and tap Connect Stripe to get paid, then Connect Stripe account.
  2. Stripe asks for your business details, ID and the bank account you want paid into. Finish every step, then come back and pull down to refresh.
  3. When it says Stripe connected, pick your Debit day: a weekday for weekly and fortnightly memberships, a date for monthly ones.
Bank payments (PayTo) are on by default and cost you less than cards. Stripe takes a day or two to switch them on. Members can save a card in the meantime.
4

5Create your memberships

  1. Open Sales then Memberships. On Products, tap + New.
  2. Add a Name, Price and Billing frequency (weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly or yearly).
  3. Turn on Includes classes if members on it book classes free. Tap Create product.

Active memberships also show in your studio's shop, so members can buy them themselves.

5

6Build your timetable

  1. Open Timetable (called Schedule if you also run PT) then Classes. Tap + New class: name, length, capacity, trainer, and whether it's included in memberships or has a casual price.
  2. Switch to Sessions and tap + Schedule. Pick the class, trainer, date and start time.
  3. Turn on Recurring schedule, tick the days and pick how many weeks (up to 12). Tap Schedule.
6

{{HEADER:Step 7 and your checklist}}

7Start members on their memberships

  1. The member saves a card or bank account under Payments in their app. If they haven't, tap Ask to add card on their row and they get an email link.
  2. On the Memberships Members tab, tap + New, pick the member and the membership, and tap Create subscription.
  3. The box shows what they pay today and what comes out after that, with the dates.
Joining between debit days: they pay a part payment today for the days up to your debit day, then the full amount every debit day after. Quarterly and yearly start the day you create them.

If a payment fails, the membership shows Past due and you get a notification. Every failed payment also shows on your dashboard.
