Four ways your clients can pay

1Automatic plans Best for regulars

Weekly, fortnightly or monthly, from the client's card or bank account, on the day you choose. Their sessions are added with every payment, so nobody has to remember to pay you.

Use for: clients who train with you every week.

2Session packs

A one-off pack, like 10 sessions. The client buys it in your shop and the sessions land on their profile straight away.

Use for: clients who prefer to pay up front.

3Payment links

Charge any amount for anything. Send the link by text or email and the client pays by card on their phone. No app needed.

Use for: casual sessions, trials, one-offs.

4Keep {{pay_now_short}}

No fees, but you check each payment and record it yourself: on the client's Money tab, tap Add sessions and pick how they paid, so the sessions show as paid for.

Step by step

Set it up once (about 15 minutes)

1Add the POS add-on

  1. Open Settings then Manage Subscription.
  2. Scroll to POS and tap Add POS, $9.99/mo. Pay in Stripe.
Why: the POS add-on is what lets you sell, take payments and use the bookkeeping pack for your accountant.
1

2Connect your bank through Stripe

  1. On the same screen, under Trainer Payouts, tap Set Up Payouts.
  2. Stripe asks for your business details, ID and the bank account you want paid into. Finish every step.
  3. It shows Verification in progress while Stripe checks (usually 1 to 2 business days), then Payouts active.
{{stripe_note}}
The button tells you where you're up to

Set Up Payouts: not started
Continue Setup: Stripe is checking
Fix Issues: Stripe needs something
View Connect Dashboard: payouts are on

3Choose how clients pay, and your debit day

  1. Once payouts are on, How clients can pay shows on the same screen.
  2. Card is always on. Bank account (PayTo) is on too. Leave it on: it's cheaper for you and suits clients without a credit card.
  3. Pick your Debit day from the two drop downs: a weekday for weekly and fortnightly plans, and a date for monthly plans. Everyone's payment then comes out on the same day.
Tip: Monday and the 1st are the defaults. If you change the day later, plans already running move to the new day on their own.
3

4Create what you sell

  1. Open any client's Money tab and tap Sell a pack in the Sessions card. That opens My Products. Tap New.
  2. Pick PT Package, add a name and price.
  3. Under How often: One-off for a pack (like 10 sessions), or Weekly, Fortnightly or Monthly for a plan. Set Sessions each time.
  4. Tap Create product.

Example: "2 sessions a week", A$120, Weekly, 2 sessions each time.

4

5Get each client's card or bank account

  1. The client opens Payments then Payment method in their app and taps Add card or Add bank account.
  2. For a bank account they enter their BSB and account number (or PayID), then approve the agreement in their banking app. Each payment is capped at A$1,500.
  3. Or do it from their Money tab in your app: Request Payment Method sends them a reminder, and Add Card Manually lets you enter a card with them in person.
5

6Put them on a plan

  1. Open the client's Money tab and tap Put on a plan.
  2. Pick the plan. Totality shows what they pay today and what comes out after that.
  3. Tap Start plan. That's it.
Starting between debit days: they pay a part payment today for the days up to your debit day, then the full amount every time after. Sessions are added with each full payment.

The client sees the plan under Payments, Memberships. Either of you can cancel, and it stops at the end of the time already paid for. If a payment fails, Stripe tries again and you get a notification.

6

7One-offs: send a payment link

  1. Open POS from your Home shortcuts.
  2. Enter the amount and a description, like "Casual PT session". Pick the client or leave it on Walk-in.
  3. Tap Generate payment link, then Copy, SMS or Email.
7

Tax and records

Put a share of every payment aside for tax. Keep records for 5 years. A separate bank account for your business makes this much easier, and it's where your Stripe payouts can go.
