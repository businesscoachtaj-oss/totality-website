Totality

Is there a free version of Totality?

Yes. Solo trainers get the Free plan at A$0 forever: programmes, session logging, check-ins, nutrition tracking, messaging and scheduling for up to 3 clients, with no Tali. Every new account starts with 7 days of everything, Tali included, then moves to Free if you don't pick a plan. Gyms and studios need a gym plan.

What is Totality?

Totality is client management software for anyone who works with clients by session, class or appointment: personal trainers, coaches, studios, gyms and allied health clinics, solo or with a team. You take bookings, write programmes, log sessions, answer check-ins and chat, with allied health tools (services, intake forms, treatment notes, funding and referrals) you can switch on. Businesses with a team also get classes, front desk check-in, member billing, payroll and reports. Clients use the same app for their workouts, check-ins, food, progress and payments.

Which plan do I need?

Solo trainers start on Free (3 clients, no Tali). Solo plans are for one trainer and go from 5 to 200 clients. Every Solo plan has the same tools. Gym plans (Studio, Club and Enterprise) add unlimited trainers, member billing and POS, classes, payroll, compliance, the leads CRM and imports. The plan picker suggests one in four questions.

Do my clients need to pay for the app?

No. Your clients count towards your plan's client limit and use the Totality app as part of your plan. Totality Nutrition is a separate app with its own subscription.

Can I move my clients across from another app?

Yes. On gym plans, the import wizard takes a CSV export from Trainerize, Mindbody or PTminder and maps the columns for you.

Which devices does Totality work on?

iPhone, Android and the web app at totalityapp.com.au. Your account works the same on all three.

What is On autopilot?

On autopilot is a set of rules that work out who is due a message and line it up for your yes: welcoming someone new, checking in when a client goes quiet, a check-in reminder, a missed session, a package running low, asking for a review and happy birthday. Studios and gyms also get new lead follow ups and failed payments. Nothing goes out until you approve it, and nobody gets the same nudge twice in 30 days.

Can I have my own website with Totality?

Yes, with the Branding add-on, which is included on Club and Enterprise. Totality builds a simple website from your brand with an enquiry form that creates a lead. A gym page also shows this week's classes and your memberships. You can ask to put it on your own domain at no extra charge.

What is the bookkeeping pack?

A quarter in one place for your accountant: payments by type, invoices paid and owed, refunds, expenses, payroll paid and the fees taken. You can share it as one CSV or email it to your accountant from the app. It comes with the POS add-on on Solo plans and is included on Studio, Club and Enterprise.

What happens if a client disputes a payment with their bank?

You get a notification and an email, and the dispute shows in the app under Disputed payments with the amount, the reason and the bank's deadline. Send us anything that shows the payment was genuine and we answer the bank for you.

Does Totality connect to Apple Health or Health Connect?

Yes. Clients can connect Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android from the app.

Totality Nutrition

What is Totality Nutrition?

A food tracking app built on Australian food data. Log meals, scan barcodes, see a full vitamin and mineral breakdown, keep a logging streak and build a grocery list. You do not need a trainer to use it.

Is there a free version of Totality Nutrition?

Yes. The Free plan is A$0 forever: log food with your full history, macro targets, water, weight and progress, 10 saved meals, 7 barcode scans a week and Tell Tali what you ate 3 times a day. No ads. Every new account starts with 7 days of the full Nutrition plan, then moves to Free if you don't pick a plan.

Is Totality Nutrition on iPhone?

Not yet. It is on Android and the web now, and the iPhone app is with Apple for review. The web app works in Safari on iPhone in the meantime.

What is the difference between Nutrition and Nutrition + AI?

Nutrition + AI adds Tali: describe a meal in plain words, import recipes from any page, get meal plans and a weekly summary. It includes 100 Tali credits a month. Everything else is the same on both plans.

Where does the food data come from?

From the Australian Food Composition Database (AFCD), published by Food Standards Australia New Zealand, and from Open Food Facts for packaged foods. Each food shows its source.

Do Totality members get a discount?

Yes. Anyone paying for their own Totality plan gets 50% off the Nutrition plan. This member price is not for clients of a Totality business. See Nutrition pricing.

Billing, trials and offers

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account gets 7 days of everything, then moves to the Free plan if you don't pick a paid one. Pick a monthly plan and the first 2 months are 50% off, applied automatically, so there is no code to enter.

How much do I save with yearly billing?

Yearly Totality plans are 15% off twelve monthly payments, billed once a year. Totality Nutrition also has yearly prices. See pricing.

Do you take a cut of what my clients pay me?

No commission on your coaching revenue. A 3.5% platform fee applies to payments taken in the app: memberships, PT packages, paid class bookings, one-off charges, payment links and shop sales. It is taken from your payout and never added to what your clients pay. Payments go to your own Stripe account, and on memberships, PT packages, class bookings and one-off charges Stripe's own card fee also comes out of your payout. If you refund a payment, the matching share of the platform fee goes back to you.

Is there a referral discount?

Yes. You get 20% off for each successful referral, stacking up to 100% while your subscription is active.

What currency are prices in?

Australian dollars.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move to a bigger or smaller plan from your account.

Cancelling

How do I cancel?

Cancel any time from your account settings, or email admintotality@gmail.com. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

Do you give refunds?

There are no part refunds for unused time after you cancel. We do refund duplicate charges, charges after a confirmed cancellation, and long outages. The refund policy has the details, and nothing in it limits your rights under Australian Consumer Law.

Can members cancel their own gym membership?

Yes. Members can cancel their membership from the payments hub in the Totality app.

Your data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

Totality and Totality Nutrition store data with Supabase, our database provider, on infrastructure hosted in the United States. The privacy policy lists every provider we use.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Cancelling does not delete your account data. Email admintotality@gmail.com to ask us to delete it. Totality Nutrition users can also delete their account in the app under Settings. How account deletion works.

Who can see my clients' information?

A trainer sees their own clients. Gym admins see the members of their gym. Tali only reads data that the person using it is allowed to see.

Tali AI

What is Tali?

Tali is the AI built into Totality and Totality Nutrition, and she has her own app, Tali HQ. In Totality it drafts check-in replies, win-back messages, lead follow-ups and programmes for trainers. In Totality Nutrition it turns a described meal into a food log, imports recipes, builds meal plans and writes weekly summaries. More about Tali.

Does Tali send anything without me?

No. Everything Tali writes is a draft. You edit it and choose to send it or save it.

How do Tali credits work?

Each Tali action uses credits, shown on the button before you tap it. Most use 1 credit, and programme drafts and meal plans use 5. Credits reset each calendar month (Sydney time). Each Totality plan includes a set number, and Enterprise is unlimited with fair use.

What if I run out of credits?

Solo and Studio plans can add a Tali AI add-on for more credits each month. See add-ons.

When can I use Tali on my phone?

Now. Tali is in Totality on iPhone, Android and the web, and in Totality Nutrition on Android and the web. Nutrition on iPhone gets Tali as soon as Apple approves the app.

Tali HQ

What is Tali HQ?

Tali HQ is a separate app where Tali is your AI manager for business and life. She remembers what you tell her, briefs you each morning, drafts replies, follow-ups and invoices for your yes, and keeps your food, training, money, lists and goals in one place. It is live as a web app at talihq.com.au, with the App Store and Google Play versions coming soon.

What does Tali HQ cost?

There is a Free plan. Tali HQ is A$19.99 a month, Tali HQ Pro is A$49.99 a month and Tali HQ Max is A$149.99 a month, with 15% off if you pay yearly. Tali HQ Business is A$199.99 a month, monthly only. Every new account starts with a 7 day free trial of Tali HQ.

What is Tali HQ Business?

The plan for running a business from one place. It has everything in Tali HQ Max, plus jobs and quotes, a pipeline, bookings with a link your customers book from, projects, a hosted website, On autopilot and a bookkeeping pack for your accountant.

Can I put a Tali HQ website on my own domain?

Yes. A personal site comes with any paid plan and a business site comes with Tali HQ Business. Putting it on your own domain is A$79 once.

Do I need Totality to use Tali HQ?

No. Tali HQ has its own sign-up and its own plans. You do not need Totality or Totality Nutrition to use it.
