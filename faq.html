Totality

What is Totality?

Totality is coaching and gym software for personal trainers, gyms and studios. Trainers write programmes, log sessions, answer check-ins, chat and take bookings. Gyms also get classes, front desk check-in, member billing, payroll and reports. Clients use the same app for their workouts, check-ins, food, progress and payments.

Which plan do I need?

Solo plans are for one trainer and go from 10 to 100 clients. Every Solo plan has the same tools. Gym plans (Studio and Enterprise) add unlimited trainers, member billing and POS, classes, payroll, compliance, the leads CRM and imports. The plan picker suggests one in four questions.

Do my clients need to pay for the app?

No. Your clients count towards your plan's client limit and use the Totality app as part of your plan. Totality Nutrition is a separate app with its own subscription.

Can I move my clients across from another app?

Yes. On gym plans, the import wizard takes a CSV export from Trainerize, Mindbody or PTminder and maps the columns for you.

Which devices does Totality work on?

iPhone, Android and the web app at totalityapp.com.au. Your account works the same on all three.

Does Totality connect to Apple Health or Health Connect?

Yes. Clients can connect Apple Health on iPhone or Health Connect on Android from the app.

Totality Nutrition

What is Totality Nutrition?

A food tracking app built on Australian food data. Log meals, scan barcodes, see a full vitamin and mineral breakdown, keep a logging streak and build a grocery list. You do not need a trainer to use it.

Is Totality Nutrition on iPhone?

Not yet. It is on Android and the web now, and iPhone is coming soon. The web app works in Safari on iPhone in the meantime.

What is the difference between Nutrition and Nutrition + AI?

Nutrition + AI adds Tali: describe a meal in plain words, import recipes from any page, get meal plans and a weekly summary. It includes 100 Tali credits a month. Everything else is the same on both plans.

Where does the food data come from?

From the Australian Food Composition Database (AFCD), published by Food Standards Australia New Zealand, and from Open Food Facts for packaged foods. Each food shows its source.

Do Totality members get a discount?

Yes. Totality members get 50% off the Nutrition plan. See Nutrition pricing.

Billing, trials and offers

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account gets a 7 day free trial, then 50% off the first 2 months. It is applied automatically, so there is no code to enter.

How much do I save with yearly billing?

Yearly Totality plans are 15% off twelve monthly payments, billed once a year. Totality Nutrition also has yearly prices. See pricing.

Do you take a cut of what my clients pay me?

No commission on your coaching revenue. A 3.5% processing fee applies to payments taken in the app. Payments go to your own Stripe account.

Is there a referral discount?

Yes. You get 20% off for each successful referral, stacking up to 100% while your subscription is active.

What currency are prices in?

Australian dollars.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move to a bigger or smaller plan from your account.

Cancelling

How do I cancel?

Cancel any time from your account settings, or email admintotality@gmail.com. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

Do you give refunds?

There are no part refunds for unused time after you cancel. We do refund duplicate charges, charges after a confirmed cancellation, and long outages. The refund policy has the details, and nothing in it limits your rights under Australian Consumer Law.

Can members cancel their own gym membership?

Yes. Members can cancel their membership from the payments hub in the Totality app.

Your data and privacy

Where is my data stored?

Totality and Totality Nutrition store data with Supabase, our database provider, on infrastructure hosted in the United States. The privacy policy lists every provider we use.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Cancelling does not delete your account data. Email admintotality@gmail.com to ask us to delete it. Totality Nutrition users can also delete their account in the app under Settings. How account deletion works.

Who can see my clients' information?

A trainer sees their own clients. Gym admins see the members of their gym. Tali only reads data that the person using it is allowed to see.

Tali AI

What is Tali?

Tali is the AI built into both apps. In Totality it drafts check-in replies, win-back messages, lead follow-ups and programmes for trainers. In Totality Nutrition it turns a described meal into a food log, imports recipes, builds meal plans and writes weekly summaries. More about Tali.

Does Tali send anything without me?

No. Everything Tali writes is a draft. You edit it and choose to send it or save it.

How do Tali credits work?

Each Tali action uses credits, shown on the button before you tap it. Most use 1 credit, and programme drafts and meal plans use 5. Credits reset each calendar month (Sydney time). Each Totality plan includes a set number, and Enterprise is unlimited with fair use.

What if I run out of credits?

Solo and Studio plans can add a Tali AI add-on for more credits each month. See add-ons.

When can I use Tali on my phone?

Tali is on the web apps now. In Totality it comes to Android and iOS on 5 Nov 2026. In Totality Nutrition it comes to Android on 5 Nov 2026, and to iPhone when the Nutrition iPhone app launches.
