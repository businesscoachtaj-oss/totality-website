---
title: Business and Life modes
summary: One switch on Today flips Tali HQ between your work and the rest of your week. Tali sorts things to the right side, and it can switch by the clock.
---

If you told Tali you have a business, Today and Your world have a **Business / Life** switch at the top. Each side shows its own things, and the other side shows a count so you can see when something is waiting there.

!phones[Today in Business](img/today-business.png)[Today in Life](img/today-top.png)

## What each side shows

- **Business**: late invoices, drafts waiting for your yes and new emails in A note from Tali, then your Business home (money, pipeline, Needs you, Due today, goals) and the Tools card, with the work parts of Your world.
- **Life**: birthdays, habits and check ups in A note from Tali, then your Life home (habits, people, money this week, food and training, journal, sleep sounds) and the Yours card, with the life parts of Your world. The lens shows People, Health and Growth.
- Her brain and level stay at the top of Today on both sides, and your people are in both.

Each side has its own home, so you can put different blocks and trackers on each. See [Your HQ](home.html).

If you do not have a business, there is no switch and Today looks the way it always has.

## Tali sorts things for you

Tali gives each reminder, list item and line of your brief a side (a reminder you add yourself goes on the side you are on), so work things show in Business and the rest shows in Life. This costs no credits. On To do, Ideas, Learning, Wins and Someday you can change which side an item belongs to, or filter by Everything.

## Switch by the clock

In **Settings**, turn on **Switch by the clock**. Pick when work starts and ends and which days you work. Tali HQ opens on Business in your work hours and on Life the rest of the time. You can still switch by hand whenever you like, and your choice holds until work next starts or ends.
