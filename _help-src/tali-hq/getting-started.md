Setting up takes about two minutes. You make an account, tap the link in the email Tali sends you, answer a few questions about you, and she shows you around.

## Make your account

!phones[Make your account](img/getting-started-signup.png)[Check your email](img/getting-started-check-email.png)

!steps
1. Open **talihq.com.au** in your browser. On your phone, you can add it to your home screen first (in Safari tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**; in Chrome tap the menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**).
2. Tap **New here? Make an account**.
3. Fill in **First name**, **Email** and **Password**. Your password needs at least 8 characters.
4. Tap **Make my account**.
5. The **Check your email** screen appears. Open the email from Tali HQ and tap the link to confirm your address.
6. Come back to Tali HQ and sign in with your email and password.

Your 7 day free trial of Tali HQ starts the moment the account is made. No card needed. [[Trial]]

:::tip Email not there?
Check your junk or spam folder. Still nothing after a few minutes? Tap **Resend email**. If you typed the wrong address, tap **Wrong email? Start again** and fill the form in again.
:::

## Sign in

!phone[Sign in](img/getting-started-signin.png)

Type your **Email** and **Password** and tap **Sign in**. If you forget your password, type your email in the box first, then tap **Forgot password**. Tali emails you a link to set a new one. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.html) if you get stuck.

## Meet Tali

The first time you sign in, Tali asks you a few things, one question to a screen. It takes about a minute.

!phones[What should I call you?](img/getting-started-onboarding.png)[What would you like a hand with?](img/getting-started-plate.png)

!steps
1. **What should I call you?** Check your **First name**. This is what Tali calls you.
2. **What would you like a hand with?** Tap as many as you like, for example My week and to-dos, Health and habits, The people in my life or Big goals. You can change it later.
3. **Do you run a business?** Tap yes or **No, just me**. If you do, add your **Business name (optional)** and tap the closest match under **What kind of business**: Personal trainer, Gym or studio, Coach or consultant, Trades, Health and beauty, Online business or Something else.
4. **Anything I should know from day one?** Write a line or two in your own words, up to 1,000 characters. For example, "I'm hopeless at follow-ups, I want to be in bed by ten, and Mum's birthday is in December." It's optional, and everything you tell her becomes a memory in her brain.
5. **Can I tap you on the shoulder?** Tali lists the only things she sends: your morning brief, reminders you asked for, and things worth knowing, like when an invoice gets paid. Tap **Turn on notifications** and allow them when your phone or browser asks, or tap **Not now, meet Tali**. This step is skipped on devices that can't show notifications.
6. Tap **Meet Tali**.

Tap **Back** on any screen to change an answer. Tali also saves your device's time zone, so your brief, reminders and shutdown land at the right local time. You can change your name, the business answer and your details later in [Settings](settings.html).

If this isn't your account (a shared device, or you signed up with the wrong email), tap **Not you? Sign out**.

:::note Just you?
Answer **No, just me** and Tali keeps to your life: people, money, health and goals. Invoices, receipts, clients, leads and rules stay out of the way. If you start a business later, switch it on in Settings and they appear.
:::

## The quick tour

Next, Tali shows you around in a few short cards. Each one points at a real spot: her brain on Today, her level, the box where you tell her things, Needs your yes, Due today and At a glance, then the Talk, Her brain, Your world and Me tabs. The second last card explains how every Tali learns together and how to switch that off.

!phones[The tour starts](img/getting-started-tour.png)[Each card points at a part of the screen](img/getting-started-tour-step.png)

Tap **Next** to move on, **Back** to go back, or **Skip tour** to finish straight away. It only shows once. To see it again, open **Me** and tap **Show me around again**.

## What to do next

- Open **Talk** and tell Tali three things: who matters to you, what's on your plate this week, and one thing you keep forgetting. The more she knows, the more useful she gets.
- Pick your morning brief time in **Me**, **Settings**.
- On the trial, send your Gmail to Tali or connect Outlook, and connect your calendar. See [Gmail and Outlook](gmail-outlook.html).
- Have a look around **Your world**. It fills in as you talk. See [Your world](world.html).
- If you use Totality or Totality Nutrition, link it from **Her brain** so Tali already knows your goals. See [Her brain](what-tali-knows.html#use-totality-too).
- Turn on notifications so you hear when your brief lands. See [Settings and account](settings.html).

## Questions

?? How long is the free trial?
7 days of Tali HQ from the moment you make your account. You don't need a card to start it.

?? What happens when my trial ends?
You move to the Free plan automatically. Nothing is deleted. To keep the Tali HQ features, open **Me**, tap **Plans**, then **Keep Tali HQ after my trial**. See [Plans, credits and billing](plans.html).

?? Do I have to fill in "Anything I should know from day one?"
No, but it's worth it. Tali reads it before every chat and brief.

?? I said I run a business but I don't any more. Can I change it?
Yes. Open **Me**, **Settings**, and under **Do you run a business?** tap **No, just me**, then **Save**. Your business details are kept in case you switch back.

?? I skipped notifications. How do I turn them on later?
Open **Me**, **Settings**, and tap **Turn on notifications on this device**. See [Settings and account](settings.html#notifications).

?? Can I change my business type later?
Yes. Open **Me**, then **Settings**, change it under **What kind of business** and tap **Save**.

?? I never got the confirmation email.
Check junk and spam, then tap **Resend email** on the Check your email screen. If you closed that screen, go to the sign in screen and try to sign in; Tali will remind you that a confirmation email is waiting. Still stuck? Email admintotality@gmail.com.

?? Can I skip the tour and see it later?
Yes. Tap **Skip tour**, then any time later open **Me** and tap **Show me around again**.
