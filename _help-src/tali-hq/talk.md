Talk is where you chat with Tali. Write to her like you'd message someone on your team. She answers, and from the same message she can add a client, set a reminder, draft a message, log your lunch or remember something about your business.

## Start a chat

Tap **Talk** at the bottom. A brand new chat shows four starters: **Help me plan my week**, **Write a follow-up for a client who went quiet**, **Give me 3 post ideas for this week** and **What should I focus on to grow?** Tap one to put it in the box, or type your own.

!phones[A new chat](img/talk-empty.png)[A chat in progress](img/talk-chat.png)

!steps
1. Type in the **Message Tali** box. A message can be up to 2,000 characters.
2. Tap **Send**.
3. While Tali works, the line under her name says **Thinking...**.
4. Her reply appears. If she did something as well, a short line above the box says what, for example "Draft waiting on Today, reminder set, saved 1 thing to remember."

!phone[The line under the chat says what Tali did](img/talk-notice.png)

## What Tali can do from a message

Just ask in plain words. She only does these when you ask.

- **Remember things** [[Free]]: facts about you, your business, people and preferences. Up to three new things per message. See [What Tali knows](what-tali-knows.html).
- **Draft messages and posts** [[Free]]: texts, follow-ups, social posts. They wait on Today for you to copy and send. See [Approvals](approvals.html).
- **Add or update clients and leads** [[Free]]: "Zack Donaldson is a new lead, follow up Thursday."
- **Set reminders** [[Free]]: "remind me to ring Lou at 4pm tomorrow". With no time given, she uses 9am on that day.
- **Log a decision** [[Free]]: "I've decided to raise PT to $90 from January because costs are up." See [Decisions](decisions.html).
- **Log food, weight and training, and write you a programme** [[Free]]: see [Food and training](food-training.html).
- **Draft an invoice** [[Tali HQ]]: "invoice Priya for 4 PT sessions at $80." See [Invoices](invoices.html).
- **Save and update goals** [[Tali HQ]]: "my goal is 5 more regular clients by February." See [Goals and weekly review](goals-review.html).
- **Draft emails from your Gmail or Outlook, and calendar bookings** [[Tali HQ]]: needs Google or Microsoft connected. See [Gmail and Outlook](gmail-outlook.html).
- **Search the web** [[Tali HQ]]: for things that change, like prices, rules, news and local businesses. She names her sources. Up to 3 searches a message.
- **Read photos** [[Free]] **and PDFs** [[Tali HQ]]: see [How Tali's replies work](replies.html#send-tali-a-photo-or-pdf).

She also knows your clients, follow-ups, reminders, unpaid invoices, business health, goals, this week's commitments and your last shutdown, plus your calendar and recent emails when they're connected. Ask "what's on this week?" or "who owes me money?" and she answers from your real data.

:::warn Tali never sends anything herself
Every message, email, invoice and booking she prepares waits on Today until you tap it. See [Approvals](approvals.html).
:::

## Your chats

Tali remembers you across every chat, so starting a new one never loses anything she knows about you. Each chat still keeps its own history.

- **New chat**: tap it at the top right to start fresh. It only shows once the current chat has a message in it.
- **Chats**: opens **Your chats**, with pinned chats first, then the rest, newest first.

!phones[Your chats](img/talk-history.png)[Rename, pin or delete a chat](img/talk-history-menu.png)

In **Your chats**:

- Tap a chat to open it.
- Tap the three dots (or press and hold a chat) for **Rename**, **Pin** or **Unpin**, and **Delete**.
- **Rename**: type the new name and press enter, or tap away.
- **Delete** asks "Delete this chat for good?". Tap **Delete** to confirm or **Keep** to stop. Deleting removes the chat and any files you sent in it. What Tali has already remembered from it stays until you forget it on the Me screen.

## If a message doesn't send

If Tali can't be reached, your message shows **Not sent. Try again** and your words (and any files) go back in the box so nothing is lost. Tap **Try again** to resend it.

!phone[A message that didn't send](img/talk-failed.png)

Other messages you might see:

- "That's your 250 free messages for this month. Upgrade to Tali HQ to keep chatting." [[Free]]
- "You've used this month's Tali credits. They top up on the 1st, or you can move up a plan." [[Tali HQ]]
- "Easy, tiger. Give me a minute and try again." You sent more than 20 things to Tali in 5 minutes. Wait a minute.

## Ask Tali from other screens

Some screens have an **Ask Tali** button: a client's page, a goal, a decision. It opens Talk with a ready question in the box. Check it, change it if you like, and tap **Send**.

## Share things to Tali

When Tali HQ is added to your home screen on Android, it shows up in your phone's share menu. Share a text, a link or a caption to Tali HQ and it lands in the Talk box as "Someone sent me this. What should I do with it?" with what you shared. Nothing sends until you tap **Send**.

## How much a message costs

- [[Free]] Each message counts as one of your 250 messages a month.
- [[Tali HQ]] [[Pro]] Each message uses 1 credit. A message with photos or a PDF uses 1 extra credit. Each web search uses 2 extra credits.

If something goes wrong on Tali's side, the credit is given back. See [Plans, credits and billing](plans.html).

## Questions

?? Does Tali remember what I said in another chat?
Yes. What she learns about you goes into her memory of you, which she uses in every chat. Inside a chat she also reads the last 20 messages.

?? Can I edit a message after I send it?
No. Send a follow-up message with the correction and Tali will use it.

?? Why didn't Tali search the web?
Web search is part of Tali HQ and Pro. Tali only searches for things that change or that she doesn't know, and never for personal details about private people.

?? Can Tali send a text or email for me?
She drafts it and it waits on Today. With Gmail or Outlook connected, you can send her email drafts with one tap from Today. Texts and social posts you copy and send yourself.

?? What happens to the words I typed if sending fails?
They go back in the box, with any files, and the failed message shows **Try again**.
