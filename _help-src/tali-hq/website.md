Tali HQ can host a simple website for you at talihq.com.au/site/your-name.

- **Just me**: a personal site, on any paid plan.
- **My business**: a business site with your services, a Book a time button and "Ask Tali to write it", on Tali HQ Business.

In Business mode, open **Website** from the tools grid on Today. In Life mode, open it from the **Yours** card on Today or **My website** in Your world.

!phones[The Website screen](img/website-edit.png)[What visitors see](img/website-public.png)

## Set it up

!steps
1. Choose **My business** or **Just me**.
2. Pick your address, the name at the top and one line about what you do.
3. Write an **About** and add up to 8 extra sections, each with a heading and some words.
4. Pick a colour.
5. Under **What shows**, switch on your services and prices, a **Book a time** button and a contact form.
6. Have a look, then save and put it live.

## Ask Tali to write it

On a business site, type anything she should mention and tap the button. Tali drafts the name, the one line, the About and up to 3 sections from what she knows about you. It fills the boxes only. Nothing is saved or live until you save. You get 5 drafts a day and it uses no credits. She is told not to make up prices, awards, years or places.

## Messages from your site

The contact form sends each message to the Website screen. On a business site, a message also becomes a new lead with a nudge on your To do.

## Your own domain

Putting your site on a domain you own is A$79 once, on any paid plan.

!phone[Your own domain, being connected](img/website-domain.png)

!steps
1. On the Website screen, unlock **Use my own domain**.
2. Type your domain and send the request.
3. We connect it by hand. The card then shows two records to add where you bought the domain.
4. Add them. The card shows **Live** once the domain answers, which can take a few hours.

?? Do I need a domain to have a site?
No. Your site works at talihq.com.au/site/your-name without one.
