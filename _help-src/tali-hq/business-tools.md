[[Business]]

**Tali HQ Business** adds the tools a small business runs on. You reach them from the tools grid on Today in Business mode, and from Your world under Work. On other plans each tool shows what it does and a button to [Plans](plans.html).

## Jobs and quotes

A quote becomes a job, and a job becomes an invoice. Each card shows what is next.

!phones[Jobs and quotes](img/business-jobs.png)[Writing a quote](img/business-quote.png)

!steps
1. Tap **New quote**, or **Tell Tali about a job** and let her start it.
2. Add what the job is, who it is for and the lines that make it up, each with how many and a price. Switch on **Add GST** to add 10% on top.
3. Share the quote as a PDF. It matches your invoices.
4. Move it along as it happens: sent, accepted, on now and done.
5. When the job is done, one tap turns it into a draft invoice.

## Pipeline

Everyone who might become a job, from first hello to won. Leads come from Your people and quotes come from Jobs, stacked by stage with the value of each. A lead can carry what it could be worth, and Tali tells you who to chase first.

## Bookings

Your week, your services and one link your customers book from.

!phones[Your bookings](img/business-bookings.png)[The page your customers see](img/business-book-link.png)

- Add your **services**, each with a length and a price.
- Share your booking link, which looks like talihq.com.au/book/your-name. Nobody needs to sign in to use it.
- A request from the link waits under **Waiting on your yes**. Tap **Yes, book it** or **No, turn it down**.
- The public page only shows your business name, your services and the times already taken.

:::note
Let the customer know yourself for now. Tali confirming bookings by text is coming soon.
:::
