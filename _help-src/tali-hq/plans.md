Tali HQ has three plans: Free, Tali HQ and Tali HQ Pro. Every new account starts with a 7 day free trial of Tali HQ. Prices are in Australian dollars, and you can pay monthly or yearly (15% less).

**Free** is for getting to know Tali: chat (250 messages a month), drafts, reminders, clients and leads, decisions, your journal and notes, food and training, the end of day shutdown, Sleep and a Friday check-in on your goals. Photos in chat work too.

**Tali HQ** is for running the business. Everything in Free, plus:

- a morning brief every day at the time you pick
- Gmail or Outlook sorted into Needs you, FYI and Junk, with replies drafted for one tap sending
- Google or Outlook calendar in your brief, with bookings on your yes
- business health checks, with a nudge twice a day when something needs a look
- invoices, sent through your own Stripe on your yes, and the Money screen
- goals, the weekly review and three promises for the week
- what Tali knows about each client, and a nudge before you meet them
- web search with sources, PDFs in chat, and draft rework (shorter, warmer, more formal)
- Tali's own journal every Sunday
- 1,000 credits a month

**Tali HQ Pro** is for handing Tali the front desk. Everything in Tali HQ, plus:

- 3,000 credits a month
- Tali's own email address for enquiries, with one tap replies
- Night school: you pick what Tali studies each night
- Rules: jobs Tali handles on her own inside Tali HQ, like spotting quiet clients and drafting overdue invoice reminders
- Patterns: what Tali noticed in your last 60 days, once a month
- first in line for new features

## The free trial [[Trial]]

Every new account gets 7 days of Tali HQ, with 1,000 credits. No card needed. Today shows how many days are left, and **Me** shows "Trial ends" and the date.

When the trial ends, you move to Free automatically. Nothing is deleted. Pro features are not part of the trial.

## What a credit is

On Tali HQ and Pro, everything Tali does with AI uses credits from your monthly allowance. On Free you have 250 messages instead.

| What Tali does | Credits |
|---|---|
| A message in Talk | 1 |
| A message with photos or a PDF | 1 extra (per message) |
| Each web search during a message | 2 extra |
| Reworking a draft (shorter, warmer, more formal, something else) | 1 |
| Morning brief | 1 a day |
| Your shutdown | 1 |
| Weekly review | 3 |
| Tali's Sunday journal | 1 a week |
| Each new email Tali sorts from Gmail or Outlook | 1 |
| Each email to Tali's own address [[Pro]] | 1 |
| A night of Night school [[Pro]] | 1, plus 2 per web search (usually 3 to 7) |
| Patterns [[Pro]] | 10 a month |

These use no credits: reminders, clients, invoices, Money, goals, decisions, your journal, food and training, Sleep, Rules, forgetting memories, and sending or booking from Today.

If something goes wrong on Tali's side, the credit is given back.

On **Free**, each message in Talk counts as one of your 250, and so does your shutdown.

## When you run out

Credits and messages reset on the 1st of each month, in your time zone. That's true on yearly plans too. Unused credits don't carry over.

- **Free**: when your 250 messages are used, Talk says "That's your 250 free messages for this month. Upgrade to Tali HQ to keep chatting."
- **Tali HQ and Pro**: Talk says "You've used this month's Tali credits. They top up on the 1st, or you can move up a plan." Scheduled jobs (brief, Night school and so on) skip until the 1st. New emails still come in, without a summary or draft.

There are no credit top ups to buy. To get more, move up from Tali HQ to Pro (three times the credits), or wait for the 1st.

## Choose a plan

!steps
1. Open **Me** and tap **See plans** under Your plan.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly, save 15%** at the top.
3. On the trial, tap **Keep Tali HQ after my trial** on Tali HQ, or **Choose Tali HQ Pro**. On Free, tap **Choose Tali HQ** or **Choose Tali HQ Pro**.
4. Stripe's secure checkout opens. Enter your card (and a promotion code if you have one) and pay.
5. You come back to Tali HQ. Your new plan shows on **Me**.

Your plan starts straight away and your card is charged at checkout. If you're on a phone app, checkout opens in your browser and brings you back to the web app; your plan shows in the app the next time you open Plans.

## Change or cancel

!phone[Plans when you're paying](img/plans-paying.png)

Once you're paying, tap **Manage billing** on Plans. It opens Stripe's billing page, where you can:

- update your card
- see and download your invoices
- switch plans. On Tali HQ, the Pro card says "Switching to Pro happens in Manage billing, where your card already is." Tap **Switch to Pro in Manage billing**.
- cancel

When a paid plan ends, you drop back to Free. Your chats, journal, clients and everything Tali knows stay.

## Founding members

If you're a founding member, Plans says "You are a founding member: 42.5% off Tali HQ and Pro for as long as you stay", shows your price with the full price crossed out, and the discount is applied at checkout.

## Questions

?? Do I need a card for the trial?
No. The 7 day trial starts when you make your account, with no card.

?? What happens to my data if I go back to Free?
It all stays. Features that need Tali HQ or Pro are locked until you upgrade again.

?? Can I buy extra credits?
No. Credits reset on the 1st of each month. If you keep running out on Tali HQ, Pro has three times as many.

?? Do unused credits roll over?
No. Each month starts fresh on the 1st.

?? If I pay yearly, do I get all my credits up front?
No. Credits reset each month on yearly plans too.

?? How do I cancel?
**Me**, **See plans**, **Manage billing**, then cancel in Stripe.

?? I paid but Me still shows my old plan.
Close the Plans screen and open **Me** again, or pull down to refresh. It can take a moment for Stripe to tell Tali HQ. Still wrong after a few minutes? Email admintotality@gmail.com.
