Totality keeps track of the sessions each client has paid for. One session (a credit) pays for one PT session. When you book a session, one is used. When there isn't one, the session waits as **Pending Settlement** until it's paid for.

Both of you can see the balance: you on the client's page and in your client list, and the client on their Home.

## The credit system switch

- **Independent trainers** choose. In **Settings > POS & BILLING**, the **Credit System** switch decides. When it's on, bookings use credits. When it's off, bookings don't touch credits and nothing is ever pending. Turning it off asks **Disable credit system?** first.
- **Trainers at a gym** don't have a switch. The gym manages credits, so bookings for gym clients always use them.

## Sessions left

Open the client's page and go to the **Money** tab. The **Sessions** card shows:

- How many sessions they have **left**, how many they've **used**, and the date to **use them by**.
- Every lot they came from, newest first: sessions you added by hand (with how they paid and the amount), packs bought in Totality, and weekly packages. Each lot shows how many are left in it.
- A note when they're running low, or have none left (new bookings then show as **Pending Settlement**). The client sees their sessions left on their Home too.

Your client list shows each client's sessions left as well.

## Add sessions

Use **Add sessions** when a client paid you outside Totality: cash, a bank transfer or PayID, another app, or sessions you're giving for free. You can add any number at once.

!steps
1. On the client's **Money** tab, tap **Add sessions** on the Sessions card.
2. Under **How many**, use the plus and minus, or tap 1, 5, 10 or 20.
3. Under **How they paid**, pick **Cash**, **Bank transfer**, **Another app** or **No charge**.
4. Put in the **Amount paid** if you want it recorded. It's optional, and it shows the price per session.
5. Pick how long they have to use them: **3 months**, **6 months** or **12 months**.
6. Add a note if you like, for example "Paid at the gym on Monday".
7. Tap **Add 10 sessions** (the number you picked).

!phone[Add sessions](img/credits-add-sessions.png)

The client sees them straight away. Every lot is recorded with the date, how they paid and who added it. At a gym, the gym owner can see sessions added by hand and who added them.

:::tip The bookkeeping pack counts it
Put in the amount and the money shows in your [Bookkeeping pack](bookkeeping-pack.html) under **Taken outside the app**, so cash and bank transfers aren't missed at tax time.
:::

## Other ways sessions arrive

- **PT packs from your shop** (independent trainers): create a **PT Package** product in [My Products](marketplace.html). A one-off pack adds its sessions when it's paid. A pack that repeats adds them with every full payment. See [Plans for clients](client-plans.html).
- **Weekly PT packages** (at a gym): a client on a weekly package (for example "3 x week PT") gets that many each week. Gym packages are set up by the gym admin. **Generate this week** on the Sessions card creates this week's sessions if they haven't been made yet.

Sessions have a use by date. Sessions past it don't count as left.

## What happens to credits

| What you do | Credit |
|---|---|
| Book a session and the client has a credit | One credit is used |
| Book when they have none | Session is **Pending Settlement**, no credit used |
| Cancel a session that used a credit | The credit goes back to the client |
| Mark a no-show | The credit stays used |

## Settle a pending session

!steps
1. Tap the pending session in Schedule (or in **Sessions to settle** on [Waiting on you](waiting-on-you.html)).
2. **Session details** says "Pending Settlement: no credit was available when this session was booked."
3. Tap **Cash Off (use 1 credit)** once the client has a credit, or **Mark complete (no credit)** if you're letting it go.

If the client still has no credit, Cash Off says **No credits available**. Add sessions first (**Add sessions**, a pack or a plan), then cash off.

!phone[A pending session](img/credits-pending.png)

## Cash off week

Settle every pending session in the week you're looking at in one go.

!steps
1. On Schedule, go to the week.
2. Tap **Cash off week (N)**. N is how many sessions are pending that week.
3. The button changes to **Sure? Cash off N**. Tap it again within 3 seconds to confirm.
4. A summary tells you how many were cashed off and lists anyone with no credits, for example "No credits: Zack Donaldson (Thu, 5 Nov 7:00 AM). These stay pending."

Sessions for clients with credits are settled. The rest stay pending until you add sessions for them.

## Questions

?? A client paid me cash. How do I record it?
Open their page, go to **Money** and tap **Add sessions**. Pick **Cash**, put in the amount, and add them. Then cash off any pending session.

?? Do unused credits roll over?
Unused credits stay available until their expiry date, if they have one.

?? Why is a session pending when the client has credits?
Credits are checked when you book. If the credit arrived after you booked, cash the session off now.

?? I turned the credit system off. What happens to existing credits?
Nothing is deleted. New bookings just stop using credits. Turn it back on any time.
