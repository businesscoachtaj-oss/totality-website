Group classes are a gym feature. If you work at a gym on Totality, you can see the gym's whole class timetable, pick up classes and run them. [[Gym trainer]]

## The class timetable

Open **Classes** from the Home shortcuts, or tap **Classes** at the top of Schedule.

- Use **‹** and **›** to move between weeks.
- Each class shows its name, time, how many are booked out of its capacity, and who's teaching.
- **You're teaching** marks your own classes.
- **Claim** shows on classes with no trainer. Tap it to take the class. You see "You're now teaching Mobility Flow on ...". The gym admin gets a notification.

!phone[The class timetable](img/classes.png)

## Schedule a new class

!steps
1. Tap **New**.
2. Pick the **Class type** (the gym's classes, set up by the gym admin).
3. Pick the **Date** and **Start time**.
4. Tap **Schedule session**.

The class is added to the timetable with you as the trainer, and the gym admin is notified.

## Run your classes

The classes you teach show in your **Schedule** next to your PT sessions, and in **Today** on Home.

- Tap a class to see **Confirmed Bookings**.
- **Add Client**, then **Book Client**, books a client into the class.
- **Mark Complete** when the class is done. It then shows **Completed ✓**.

Clients who book a class get a reminder by push and email about an hour before it starts.

!phone[A class in your Schedule](img/classes-schedule.png)

## Questions

?? I'm an independent trainer. Can I run classes?
Classes come with the gym plans. Independent trainers see "Classes are a gym feature".

?? Someone claimed a class at the same time as me.
You see **Already claimed**. Pull down to refresh and see who has it.

?? How do I hand a class back?
Ask your gym admin to reassign it.
