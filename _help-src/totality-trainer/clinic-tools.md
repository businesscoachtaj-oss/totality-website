If you see clients by appointment, Totality has the tools allied health practitioners use: services with a length and price, your own intake forms, treatment notes, funding and referrals, and itemised invoices.

## Switch them on

Open **Settings**, then **Allied health tools**. Switch on what you use:

- **Funding and referrals**: Medicare, DVA, Workcover, NDIS, Aged Care, private health or your own, with billing codes.
- **Services**: your own appointment types, each with a length and a price.
- **Intake forms**: your own questions, filled in before a first appointment.
- **Treatment notes**: a note after each appointment, kept on the client.

Then tap **Set up services and intake forms**. Switching one off hides it. Nothing already saved is deleted.

At a gym or clinic, services, intake forms and treatment notes are set by the business. They show on your clients when the business has them switched on, and you use them the same way.

## Services and intake forms

Add a service with its **NAME**, **LENGTH** in minutes and **PRICE ($)**, and pick the **INTAKE FORM** to fill in the first time a client books it. Write intake forms with **Short answer**, **Long answer**, **Yes or no** and **Pick one** questions. The steps are the same as for a clinic. See the [owner guide's clinic page](../totality-owner/clinic-tools.html).

When you book a single appointment for a priced service that no credit covers, Totality asks **Booked. Take payment?**: **Charge their saved card**, **Send a payment link**, **Write an invoice** or **Not now**.

## Treatment notes

On a client's profile, tap **Add note** under **Treatment notes**. Fill in any of **What the client told you**, **What you found**, **Your assessment** and **Plan**, pick the date and service, and tap **Save note**. Clients can't see treatment notes.

## Invoices

Independent trainers have an **Invoices** tile on Home: write, send and track. At a gym, you invoice from the client's profile, and the invoice uses the gym's details.

!steps
1. On a client's profile, tap **New invoice** under **Invoices**. The first time, fill in **Your invoice details**: business name, ABN, address, email, phone, whether you're **Registered for GST**, and **HOW TO PAY**.
2. Under **WHO IS IT FOR?**, pick the client or a funder from their Funding & Referrals.
3. Add a line for each service: description, quantity, price, date of service, and if needed your provider number and an item code.
4. Tap **Save invoice**, then **Email invoice**.
5. When it's paid, tap how under **MARK AS PAID**. That only updates the invoice. It doesn't charge anyone.

## Questions

?? Can my clients see my treatment notes?
No. Only you, and at a gym or clinic the admin and practitioners assigned to that client.

?? Do invoices take the money?
No. Invoices are for billing and records. To take money, charge their saved card or send a payment link.
