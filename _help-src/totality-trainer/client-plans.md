[[POS add-on]]

If you train clients independently, you can put a client on a repeating plan yourself, straight from their page. It comes out of the card or bank account they saved with you, on your debit day, until one of you cancels it. A PT package that repeats adds its sessions every time it is paid.

You need three things first:

1. **Payouts active**, under Trainer Payouts. See [Getting paid](getting-paid.html).
2. **A repeating plan in your shop**: a membership, or a PT package that repeats.
3. **A card or bank account the client saved with you.** They add it under **Payments** in their app.

## Make a PT package that repeats

!steps
1. Open your shop and tap **New** on My Products.
2. Pick **PT Package** as the product type and give it a name and price.
3. Under **How often**, pick **Weekly**, **Fortnightly** or **Monthly**. **One-off** is a pack paid once, as before.
4. Under **Sessions each time**, put how many sessions each payment adds.
5. Save it.

!phone[A PT package that repeats monthly](img/client-plans-repeating-pack.png)

Clients can buy it from your shop too. Either way it comes out on your debit day.

## Put a client on a plan

!steps
1. Open the client's page and go to the **Money** tab.
2. Under **Payment**, tap **Put on a plan**.
3. Pick the plan from the drop down.
4. Check **Pays with**. It shows the card or bank account they saved with you, the one set as their default.
5. Check what comes out today and what comes out after that, then tap **Start plan**.

!phone[Putting a client on a plan](img/client-plans-start.png)

If today is not your debit day, the client pays a **part payment** today for the days up to it, then the full amount on your debit day every time after. The sheet shows both amounts and the dates before you start.

If they have nothing saved with you yet, the button says **Ask (their name) to add one** instead of Start plan. They add a card or bank account under **Payments** in their app, then you start the plan.

:::tip Bank accounts
A bank payment takes about a minute to confirm, so the plan starts straight away and the money follows. A client's bank agreement allows up to A$1,500 a payment, so a bigger plan needs a card.
:::

## Sessions with a repeating PT package

Every full payment adds the package's sessions to the client's sessions left, once per payment. A part payment at the start adds none, so the first sessions arrive with the first full payment on your debit day. The sheet says when.

## When a payment fails

You get a notification: the client's plan payment did not go through. Stripe tries again over the next few days. On the client's page the plan says **Last payment didn't go through. Stripe is trying again.**

## Cancel a plan

Under **Plans** on the client's page, tap **Cancel** next to the plan. It stops at the end of the period already paid for, so nobody loses days they paid for. Sessions already added stay.

The client sees the plan under **Payments**, Memberships, **With your trainer**, and can cancel it there too. Whoever cancels, the other one gets a notification.

## Questions

?? Can I put a client on a plan if I train at a gym?
No. At a gym, the gym sells memberships and packs to its members. This is for independent trainers.

?? Where does the money go?
Into your own Stripe account, then to your bank. Totality's 1.25% platform fee and Stripe's fee come out of each payment. See [Getting paid](getting-paid.html).

?? Can I change the plan's price later?
Edit the product in your shop for new sign ups. A plan already running keeps the price it started on. To change it, cancel it and start the new one.

?? What happens if I change my debit day?
Plans already running move to the new day at their next payment. The days in between are added to that payment, so nobody pays twice or misses a day.

?? Do sessions from a plan expire?
Yes, a year after they are added, the same as sessions from a pack.
