The **Schedule** tab is your week of PT sessions. At a gym it also shows the classes you teach and your rostered shifts.

!phones[Your week](img/schedule-week.png)[Book Appointment](img/schedule-book.png)

## Find your way around

- The week strip shows Monday to Sunday. Use **‹** and **›** to change weeks. Dots mark days with sessions.
- Tap a day to see its sessions, sorted by time.
- Each session shows the time, length, client, and any note. **Completed** sessions are marked. Sessions still to do have a green **Complete** button.
- **Cash off week** settles every pending session in the week at once. See [Credits](credits.html#cash-off-week).
- **Classes** at the top opens the gym's class timetable (gym trainers). See [Classes](classes.html).
- Pull down to refresh.

## Book a session

!steps
1. Tap **Book**.
2. Pick the **Client**. The number next to each name is how many credits they have.
3. Pick the **Date**.
4. Pick a **Start time** from the Morning, Afternoon and Evening slots (every 30 minutes, 6am to 9:30pm).
5. Pick a **Duration**: 30, 45, 60, 75 or 90 minutes.
6. Add **Notes (optional)**, like "Bring lifting shoes".
7. For a regular slot, switch on **Recurring (weekly)** and pick the **Number of weeks**: 4, 6, 8, 12 or 16.
8. Tap **Book session**.

If you've [connected Google Calendar](calendar-sync.html), the times you're busy in Google show under the slots. Picking a busy time asks **Busy in Google Calendar** first, with **Book anyway**.

With the credit system on, booking uses one of the client's credits. If they have none, the session is still booked and marked **Pending Settlement**. You see a message telling you which happened. See [Credits and settling sessions](credits.html).

## Session details

Tap a session to open **Session details**: client, date, time, status, whether it's part of a weekly series, whether a credit was used, and notes.

!phone[Session details](img/schedule-detail-detail.png)

From here:

- **Start session** opens the live session for the client. See [Running a session](running-a-session.html).
- **Mark complete** marks it done.
- **Mark no-show (credit kept)** records a no-show. The credit stays used.
- **Cancel session** cancels it. If a credit was used, it goes back to the client. For a recurring series you choose **This session only** or **All future sessions**.

## Shifts (gym trainers)

If your gym rosters you, **My shifts** shows your shifts for the next 14 days at the top of Schedule, with times and your role. Shifts are set by the gym admin.

## Questions

?? Can my clients book themselves?
No. You book PT sessions. Clients see them in their app as read only, with a link to message you if they need a change.

?? Can I see my sessions in my phone's calendar?
Yes. Connect Google Calendar, or add the calendar link to Google or Apple Calendar. See [Calendar sync](calendar-sync.html).

?? I booked the wrong time.
Open the session, tap **Cancel session**, and book it again at the right time. Any credit goes back to the client.
