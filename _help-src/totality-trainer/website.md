[[Branding add-on]]

With the [Branding add-on](branding.html) you get a simple website for your coaching, built from your logo, your colour and a few words. Branding is A$4.99 a month on Solo plans.

On Home, open **My website** under My business.

!phones[The My website screen](img/website-edit.png)[What visitors see](img/website-public.png)

## Set it up

!steps
1. Save your [branding](branding.html) with a link name first. Your website uses the same link name, logo and colour.
2. Open **My website**. Your address is shown at the top, for example totalityapp.com.au/site/samreid.
3. Write **One line about what you do** and an **About**. Or type a few things under **Ask Tali to write it** and tap **Write my page**. Tali drafts both for 1 Tali credit and you edit them.
4. Under **What shows**, switch on **A question box** so people can message you.
5. Add an email and phone number to show if you want them.
6. Tap **Save changes**. Nothing is live until you save.

Use **Share my link** to send the address to someone, and **Take it offline** to stop the page showing.

## Your own domain

Already own a domain? Ask for it from the **Your own domain** card. It is part of Branding at no extra charge. We connect it for you, then the card shows two records to add where you bought the domain.

?? Where do messages from my website go?
They land on the My website screen for you to reply to.
