**My documents** keeps your certificates and checks in one place, with their expiry dates, so nothing lapses without you knowing. Open **Settings** and, under **MY DOCUMENTS**, tap **Certificates and checks**.

At a gym, your gym's owner can see these too, in their compliance hub. Otherwise only you can see them.

## What you can keep

First Aid Certificate, CPR Certificate, Police Check, Working With Children Check, Fitness Qualification/Certification, Public Liability Insurance, Professional Indemnity Insurance, Contract/Agreement and COVID Vaccination Record.

Each one shows where it's up to: **ON FILE**, **EXPIRING SOON**, **EXPIRED** or **NOT ADDED**.

## Add or renew a document

!steps
1. Tap the document and upload a PDF or a photo.
2. Tap **Add expiry date**, pick the date and tap **Save**. If it never runs out, tap **It doesn't expire**.
3. When you renew, upload the new one with **Replace** and tap **Change expiry date**.

Tap **View** to open what's on file.

## Questions

?? Do I have to buy insurance through BizCover?
No. Any insurer is fine. Upload your certificate either way so the reminders work.

?? Who sees my documents?
You. At a gym, your gym's owner or admin as well.

?? Will I get a reminder before something expires?
Yes. Add the expiry date and you're reminded 30 and 7 days before.
