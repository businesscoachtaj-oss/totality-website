## Plans and billing

?? How much does Totality cost for a personal trainer?
Free for up to 3 clients. Solo plans run from A$19.99 a month (Solo 5) to A$249.99 a month (Solo 200). New accounts get a 7 day trial of everything and 50% off the first 2 months on a monthly plan. See [Plans and prices](plans.html).

?? Do I need a credit card to start?
No. The trial and Free plan need no card.

?? What happens after the 7 day trial?
You move to Free automatically: same 3 clients, no Tali, no deadline. Nothing locks.

?? Is there a yearly price?
Yes. Every Solo plan has a yearly option at 15% off twelve monthly payments.

?? Can I change plans any time?
Yes, in **Settings > Manage Subscription > Change Plan**. It applies straight away and the difference is prorated on your next invoice.

?? How do I cancel?
**Settings > Manage Subscription > Manage Billing**, then cancel in Stripe. You drop to Free and keep your data. See [Your subscription](subscription.html#cancel-your-plan).

?? What's included on every Solo plan?
Programme builder, session logging, check-ins, nutrition tracking, messaging, appointments and scheduling, auto-regulation, push notifications and Tali credits. POS and Branding are add-ons.

?? How does referring another trainer work?
They get 70% off their first 2 months, and you get 20% off your subscription for each one who subscribes, stacking up to 5. See [Settings](settings.html#refer-and-earn).

## Clients

?? How many clients can I have?
3 on the trial and Free, then 5, 10, 20, 30, 50, 100 or 200 on the Solo plans. At a gym, the gym's plan sets a shared limit.

?? Do archived clients count?
No. Archive clients you no longer coach to free up a spot.

?? Do my clients pay for the app?
No. They use Totality free as your clients.

?? What do my clients see?
Their programme and sessions, their food log and targets, their weekly check-in and your replies, their bookings, messages with you, and your shop if you have one. There's a separate guide for clients.

## Coaching

?? Can I build a programme once and give it to lots of clients?
Yes. Assign it to as many clients as you like.

?? Can I copy last week's session into this week?
Yes. **Copy from Week N** in the session, or **Copy week** in the programme builder.

?? Do I have to run sessions with my clients?
No. Clients can log their own sessions from their app. You see everything they log.

?? What's RPE and why does it matter?
Rate of perceived exertion, how hard a set felt out of 10. Auto-regulation uses it to suggest when to change a client's load.

?? Can clients see my coach notes?
Yes. Coach notes on an exercise show to the client as "Note from your coach". Private notes on their profile don't.

## Check-ins and Tali

?? When do clients do check-ins?
Weekly, with a reminder on Sunday morning. They can also skip a week.

?? How much does Tali cost?
Tali credits come with every Solo plan (50 to 600 a month). Check-in replies and nudges use 1 credit, a programme draft uses 5, and her daily brief and meal logging are free.

?? Is Tali on the Free plan?
No. She's in the trial and every Solo plan.

?? Will Tali message my clients without asking?
No. She drafts, you edit and you send.

## Schedule and payments

?? Can clients book their own PT sessions?
No. You book PT sessions. At a gym, clients can book classes.

?? What's "Pending settlement"?
A session booked when the client had no credit. Cash it off once they have one. See [Credits](credits.html).

?? How do I take payments?
With the POS add-on (A$9.99 a month on a Solo plan) and Stripe payouts set up. See [Getting paid](getting-paid.html).

?? What fees are there?
A 3.5% platform fee on POS and marketplace payments, taken from your payout. It's never added to what your client pays.

?? Does my calendar sync?
Yes. Google Calendar two-way, or a one-way link for Google and Apple Calendar. See [Calendar sync](calendar-sync.html).

## Gyms

?? I work at a gym. Do I pay?
No. The gym's plan covers you.

?? What changes when I join a gym?
The gym can see your clients, your own billing ends, and you get Gym Members, Classes and shifts. See [Joining a gym](joining-a-gym.html).

?? Where do payments go at a gym?
To the gym's Stripe account.

## Account and devices

?? Does Totality work on iPhone, Android and computers?
Yes. The phone apps, and the web at totalityapp.com.au.

?? Can I use kg and kJ, or pounds and calories?
Yes. Set your units in **Settings > Units and region**.

?? How do I change my email address?
Email admintotality@gmail.com. It can't be changed in the app.

?? How do I delete my account?
**Settings > Account > Delete Account**. It deletes everything and can't be undone.

?? Is my data safe?
Your data stays in your account. Clients can only see their own data, and a gym can only see your data after you accept its invite.
