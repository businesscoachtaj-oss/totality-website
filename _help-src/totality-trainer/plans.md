Every new independent trainer starts with 7 days of everything. When the trial ends you drop to Free automatically, with no card needed and no lock. You only pay when you pick a Solo plan. All prices are in Australian dollars.

If you work at a gym on Totality, your gym pays and none of this applies to you. Skip to [Trainers at a gym](#trainers-at-a-gym).

## The 7 day trial

[[Trial]]
- Everything is open: programmes, sessions, check-ins, nutrition, messaging, scheduling, auto-regulation.
- Up to **3 clients**.
- **50 Tali AI credits** to try Tali.
- No card needed.

When the trial has 2 days or fewer left, a banner on Home counts down. On the last day it reads "Last day of your trial. Tomorrow you move to Free, or pick a plan to keep Tali". You also get a reminder email 3 days before it ends.

## The Free plan

[[Free]]
Free is yours for as long as you like.

- Up to **3 clients**.
- Every Solo tool: programme builder, session logging, client check-ins, nutrition tracking, messaging, appointments and scheduling, the auto-regulation engine and push notifications.
- **No Tali AI**. Her spot on Home shows what she does and a **See plans** button.
- Add-ons (POS, Tali AI, Branding) come with a Solo plan, so they are not offered on Free.

## Solo plans

[[Paid]]
Every Solo plan has the same tools. They differ by how many clients you can coach and how many Tali credits you get each month.

| Plan | Clients | Tali credits a month | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 3 | none | A$0 | A$0 |
| Solo 5 | 5 | 50 | A$19.99 | A$203.90 |
| Solo 10 | 10 | 100 | A$29.99 | A$305.89 |
| Solo 20 | 20 | 150 | A$39.99 | A$407.90 |
| Solo 30 | 30 | 200 | A$64.99 | A$662.90 |
| Solo 50 | 50 | 300 | A$94.99 | A$968.90 |
| Solo 100 | 100 | 400 | A$149.99 | A$1,529.90 |
| Solo 200 | 200 | 600 | A$249.99 | A$2,549.90 |

- **Yearly** is 15% off twelve monthly payments, billed once a year.
- **New subscribers get 50% off the first 2 months** on a monthly plan. It is applied at checkout automatically, no code needed. It does not apply to yearly plans.
- If another trainer or gym referred you, the first 2 months are 70% off instead (the 50% welcome offer plus 20% from the referral). See [Refer and earn](settings.html#refer-and-earn).

Only active clients count towards your limit. Archived clients don't. See [Your client list](client-list.html).

## Add-ons

[[Paid]]
Add-ons sit on top of a Solo plan. Add them from **Settings > Manage Subscription**. To change or cancel one later, tap **Manage Billing** on the same screen.

| Add-on | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| POS add-on | A$9.99/mo | Take payments and sell products through your own Stripe account. See [Getting paid](getting-paid.html) |
| Tali AI add-on | A$9.99/mo | +1,000 Tali credits a month on top of your plan |
| POS + AI bundle | A$16.99/mo | Both of the above, A$2.99 less than buying both |
| Branding add-on | A$4.99/mo | Your logo, name and colour in the app, emails and push. See [Branding](branding.html) |
| POS + Tali + Branding | A$19.99/mo | All three, A$4.98 less than buying each |

A 3.5% platform fee applies to payments your clients make in the app: PT packages, one-off charges, payment links and shop sales. It comes out of your payout and is never added to what your client pays. It is not a cut of your coaching income. On PT packages and one-off charges, Stripe's own card fee also comes out of your payout.

## Trainers at a gym

If you work at a gym on Totality, the gym's plan covers you. You don't pay anything and there's nothing to choose. Your Subscription screen shows **Gym Partner**, the gym's plan and how many of the gym's member spots are used.

- Your client limit is the gym's limit, shared across every trainer there.
- Tali credits are shared across the gym. Your gym admin can add more.
- POS payments go to the gym's Stripe account, not yours.

## Questions

?? What happens to my clients when the trial ends?
Nothing. You keep your 3 clients, their programmes and all their history on Free. Tali switches off. Pick a Solo plan any time to get her back and add more clients.

?? I had more than 3 clients and moved to Free. Do I lose them?
No one is deleted. You just can't add a new client while you're at or over 3. Archive clients you no longer coach to get under the limit, or pick a Solo plan.

?? Can I switch between monthly and yearly?
Yes. Open **Settings > Manage Subscription > Change Plan**, pick **Monthly** or **Yearly · Save 15%**, then **Switch to this plan**.

?? Do I need a card for the trial?
No. You only enter a card when you subscribe to a Solo plan.

?? What does "Up to 5 clients" count?
Active clients on your list. Archived clients are not counted.
