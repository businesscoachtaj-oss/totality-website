Tali (said "tally") is the assistant inside Totality. She reads your day, spots who's gone quiet and drafts the replies, programmes and nudges. You check everything and decide what gets sent or saved. Tali never sends anything on her own.

## What Tali does

| Job | Where | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Tali's brief, your day in two lines | Top of Home | Free |
| Draft a check-in reply | Check-ins > Your response > **Draft a reply with Tali** | 1 |
| Draft a nudge for a quiet client | Waiting on you, or the client's profile | 1 |
| Draft a programme from a brief | My Programmes > + New > **Draft it with Tali** | 5 |
| Describe a meal to log it | Me > My nutrition > add food > **Describe your meal to Tali** | Free, with a daily limit |

Every draft is marked "Drafted by Tali". Edit it, write another (**Again** or **Regenerate**, which uses another credit), or throw it away.

## Credits

Each plan comes with a monthly allowance that resets each month.

| Plan | Tali credits |
|---|---|
| 7 day trial | 50 for the trial |
| Free | none |
| Solo 5 | 50 a month |
| Solo 10 | 100 a month |
| Solo 20 | 150 a month |
| Solo 30 | 200 a month |
| Solo 50 | 300 a month |
| Solo 100 | 400 a month |

**Settings > Tali AI** shows your plan, credits left this month and the reset date. Buttons that use credits show how many (for example "Uses 1 credit") and what you have left.

## Trainers at a gym

At a gym you share the gym's Tali credits with everyone else there. "Tali credits are shared across Iron Works." There's nothing for you to buy. Your gym admin can add more.

## On the Free plan

Tali isn't included on Free. Her place on Home explains what she does with a **See plans** button, and Tali buttons read **Add Tali** and take you to your plans. Every Solo plan includes her, from 50 credits a month on Solo 5.

## Questions

?? What happens when I run out of credits?
Tali buttons tell you you're out and offer to get more. Everything else in Totality keeps working. Credits reset at the start of your next month.

?? Do unused credits carry over?
No. The allowance resets each month.

?? Does Tali see my clients' data?
Only what she needs for the job you ask: the check-in for a reply, the client's activity for a nudge, onboarding answers for a programme draft when you pick a client. She uses exercises from your library only.

?? Does Tali send messages for me?
Never on her own. You tap Send.
