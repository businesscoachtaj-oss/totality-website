A programme is a block of training: a number of weeks, each with sessions on set days, each session with its exercises. You build it once and assign it to as many clients as you like.

Open **My Programmes** from the Home shortcuts.

!phones[My Programmes](img/programmes-list.png)[New Programme](img/programmes-new.png)

## Your programmes

Each card shows the programme's name, description, length in weeks, and a pill with who it's assigned to (a client's name, or "2 clients"). Tap a card to open it in the [programme builder](programme-builder.html).

The **⋮** on each card opens a menu:

- **Duplicate** makes a full copy, sessions and exercises included, with "(copy)" added to the name. Handy for a new block based on an old one.
- **Share to Collection** (at a gym) lets other trainers at your gym see and copy it. **Remove from Collection** stops sharing.

## Create a programme

!steps
1. Tap **+ New**.
2. Type a **Programme Name**, for example "4-Week Strength Block".
3. Add a **Description** if you want, and the **Duration (weeks)**.
4. Under **Assign to Client (optional)** pick a client, or leave it as "No client selected".
5. Tap **Create**. The programme opens in the builder so you can add sessions.

## Templates

The **Templates** tab on Programmes has ready-made programmes you can set for a client or use as a starting point: the original Totality templates (**Totality classics**) and the full Totality programme library, 420 programmes. The library has every style at 2 to 6 days a week, at Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced. The level changes the exercises as well as the sets, reps and rest.

| Everyday training | Sports and styles |
|---|---|
| Whole body | Powerlifting, Street Lifting, Olympic Lifting |
| Glutes and legs | Classic Bodybuilding, New School Bodybuilding, Powerbuilding |
| Glutes and arms | Strongman, Circus Strongman |
| Strength | Gymnastics, Calisthenics |
| Lean and fit | Kettlebell, Athletic Performance, Hybrid (lifting and running) |
| Health Guidelines | |

!steps
1. Open **My Programmes** from the Home shortcuts and tap the **Templates** tab, then **Programmes**.
2. Pick a **STYLE** from the drop down, then the **DAYS A WEEK** and **LEVEL**.
3. Tap a programme to see every workout, exercise, set and rep, and how it progresses.
4. Tap **Use this template**. Totality makes your own copy, named like "Strongman, 3 days, Intermediate", and opens it in the [programme builder](programme-builder.html).
5. Change anything you like, then tap **Functions**, then **Assign to Client**.

**Ask Tali to tailor it** on the same screen has Tali adjust the template for a client's goal, level, equipment and injuries.

A few styles need kit most gyms don't have. Strongman days say to use the Beginner version of any event you can't do (sandbags, farmer's carries and the trap bar). Gymnastics and Calisthenics need a pull-up bar, and rings help. Every exercise is from the exercise library and has a demo video.

**Health Guidelines** is built on the World Health Organization's physical activity guidelines for adults: strength for every major muscle on 2 or more days and 150 minutes of moderate cardio a week, with mobility and balance work. The 2 and 3 day versions add brisk walks on other days to reach the 150 minutes. It comes for a full gym, dumbbells or no equipment, and isn't made or endorsed by the WHO. **Classic Bodybuilding** trains one or two areas a day. **New School Bodybuilding** trains every muscle at least twice a week. Whole body and Lean and fit also have dumbbell and bodyweight programmes, and Glutes and legs has a dumbbell one, for clients who train at home.

### The second half of a programme

Every template changes halfway through, so your client isn't doing the same exercises for 8 or 12 weeks. The first lifts each day get heavier for fewer reps (no equipment programmes move to a harder version instead), and most other exercises swap for a close variation with the same kit. In the preview, tap the **Weeks** chips to see each half. When you use the template, the swaps are already in the copy from that week.

### Set types

Some exercises on Intermediate and Advanced templates use a set type where it helps: drop set (**DS**), myo reps (**MYO**), rest-pause (**RP**) or **AMRAP**. They show as coloured badges in the preview, carry into the builder, and your client sees how to do each one in their workout.

## Collection (gym trainers)

The **Collection** tab shows programmes other trainers at your gym have shared. Filter by tag. Open one to view it, then tap **Copy to my programmes** to get your own editable copy.

!phone[The Collection at Iron Works](img/programmes-gym-collection.png)

Independent trainers see an empty Collection, because it's shared within a gym.

## Questions

?? Can I use one programme for several clients?
Yes. Assign it to as many clients as you like. Each client's progress is tracked separately.

?? If I edit a programme, do my clients see the change?
Yes. Clients on that programme see the updated sessions next time they open it. Duplicate it first if you want a separate version for one client.

?? Can I delete a programme?
Yes. Open it, tap **Functions**, then **Delete**. Every session and exercise inside it is deleted too, and it can't be undone. If you only want it off one client, use **Remove from client** on their profile instead, which keeps the programme in your library and their past workouts.

?? Can I share a programme with a trainer outside my gym?
No. The Collection is only within your gym. You can sell a programme on the marketplace instead. See [Marketplace](marketplace.html).
