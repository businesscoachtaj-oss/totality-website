## What's in the list

- **Check-ins to reply to**: every check-in you haven't answered, with when it was sent. Tap one to open it in Check-ins, ready for [a reply](check-ins.html#draft-a-reply-with-tali).
- **Gone quiet**: clients who haven't been active in the app for 14 days or more, with "Last active N days ago" (or "Not active in the app yet"). Tap the name to open their profile, or tap **Draft a nudge**.
- **Sessions to settle**: sessions marked Pending settlement. Tap one to jump to that day in Schedule with the session open. See [Credits and settling sessions](credits.html).

When there's nothing left, Tali says so.

## Nudges

A nudge is a short, friendly message to bring a quiet client back. Tali writes it, you send it.

!steps
1. Tap **Draft a nudge** on a client in **Gone quiet** (or **Draft a nudge with Tali** on their profile).
2. Tali looks at their activity and writes a message in your voice. It uses 1 credit.
3. Edit anything in the box. **Regenerate** writes a new one (1 more credit).
4. Tap **Send message**. It arrives as an in-app message from you, with a notification. Or tap **Copy** (**Share** on a phone) to send it another way.

Once you've messaged a client, they drop off **Gone quiet** for 7 days so you're not nudging twice.

!phone[Gone quiet on a client's profile](img/client-profile-zack.png)

## The weekly inactive email

Separately from Tali, Totality emails you a weekly digest listing clients with no activity (sessions, food or check-ins) in the last 14 days. New clients get a 14 day grace period. To stop it, switch off **Tips and reports** in [Settings](settings.html#notifications), or use the Unsubscribe link in the email.

## Questions

?? I'm on Free. Can I still see who's gone quiet?
Yes. The **At risk** filter on the Clients tab shows clients with no app activity for 14 days, and Home flags inactive clients in **Needs Attention**. Waiting on you and Tali's nudges come with a Solo plan.

?? Does Tali send anything on her own?
No. Nothing is sent until you tap **Send message**.

?? Can I nudge a client without Tali?
Yes. Open **Messages**, tap the client and write to them yourself.
