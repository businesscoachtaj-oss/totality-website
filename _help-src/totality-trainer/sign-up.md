Signing up takes about two minutes. You need an email address you can open, and that's it. No card.

## Create your account

!steps
1. Open Totality on your phone, or go to **totalityapp.com.au**, and tap **Get Started: It's Free**.
2. Fill in **First name**, **Email address**, **Password** and **Confirm password**. Last name is optional.
3. Got a referral code from another trainer or gym? Type it in **Referral code**. If you opened a referral link, the code is already filled in.
4. Tap **Create Account**.
5. Check your email for the confirmation link and tap it, then come back and log in.

!phones[Create your account](img/sign-up-form.png)[The first screen. Log In if you already have an account](img/sign-up-login.png)

Your password needs at least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, and a number or symbol.

## Tell Totality how you'll use it

The first time you log in you land on **Welcome to Totality**, which confirms your 7 day free trial has started.

!steps
1. Under **ARE YOU SOLO OR A STUDIO OWNER?** pick **Solo** ("Just me and my clients").
2. Under **WHAT DO YOU DO?** tap the closest match, for example **Personal training**. Health practitioners pick their own, like **Remedial massage** or **Exercise physiology**.
3. Add a **Business name (optional)** if you have one.
4. Tap **Let's Go →**.

| Fitness | Health and wellness |
|---|---|
| Gym | Physio |
| Personal training | Chiro |
| Pilates | Osteo |
| Yoga | Exercise physiology |
| Group training | Remedial massage |
| Dance | Nutrition and dietetics |
| Martial arts and boxing | Recovery |
| Sports and courts | Wellness centre |

The type sets your layout: what your people are called (clients or patients), which tools are switched on, and your Home setup card. A practitioner who writes treatment notes and doesn't use programmes gets a **Notes** tab where Check-Ins would be. Change it any time in **Settings** under **WHAT YOU DO**. Switching a tool off only hides it, nothing is deleted.

:::warn Pick Solo
**Studio owner** is for owners who run a business with a team. It creates a gym and makes you its admin. If you're a trainer who works at someone else's gym, pick Solo and accept the gym's invite when it arrives. At a gym, your layout follows the gym's type, not your own. See [Joining a gym](joining-a-gym.html).
:::

## Add a profile photo

**Let's set up your account** asks for a profile photo. Tap the circle to pick one, or tap **Skip for now**. Clients see your photo in messages. Tap **Let's Go →** and Totality takes you to Home and starts the tour.

## Logging in next time

Open the app and tap **Log In**. Use the same email and password on your phone and on the web.

Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot Password?** on the login screen, enter your email, and Totality emails you a reset link.

## Questions

?? I never got the confirmation email.
Check your spam or promotions folder. The email comes from Totality. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, email admintotality@gmail.com with the address you used.

?? It says an account with this email already exists.
You (or a gym) already made an account with that email. Tap **Log In** instead. If you don't know the password, reset it from the login screen.

?? My gym told me to sign up. Should I pick Studio owner?
No. Pick **Solo**. The gym sends you an invite, and when you accept you become a trainer at that gym.

?? Can I change my business name later?
The name your clients see can be set in **Settings > Branding** with the Branding add-on. See [Branding](branding.html). Your own first and last name are in **Settings > Profile**.
