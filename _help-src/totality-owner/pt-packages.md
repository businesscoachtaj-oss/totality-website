A **PT package** is personal training sold as a subscription, for example "2x PT per week, $170 a week". Each week the member gets that many **credits**. Each PT session booked uses one credit. Trainers are paid for the sessions once they are completed.

Open the **Sales** tab, then **PT packages**.

!phones[Your PT packages](img/pt-packages-list.png)[Members on a package](img/pt-packages-subs.png)

## Create a package

!steps
1. Tap **+ New**.
2. Enter the **Name** (for example 2x PT per week) and **Credits per week**.
3. Enter the **Price ($)** (for example 200.00) and pick the **Billing frequency**: weekly, fortnightly or monthly.
4. Optional: a **Description**.
5. Leave **Active** on and save.

Each package shows its credits and price, for example "2 credits/week · $170.00 AUD/wk". Use the switch to hide a package. Tap it to edit.

## Put a member on a package

The member needs a card saved with your gym first (see [Failed payments and cards](failed-payments.html#ask-a-member-to-add-a-card)).

!steps
1. Under **Client subscriptions**, tap **+ Assign**.
2. Pick the **Client** and the **Package**.
3. Pick their **Trainer (optional)**. Credits are given for this trainer.
4. Save. Their card is charged and billing continues on the package's frequency.

Each member on a package shows the package, trainer, start date and status: **Active**, **Past due** (payment failed, see [Failed payments](failed-payments.html)) or ended.

## How credits work

- Credits are added automatically every **Sunday night** for each member whose package is active and paid up.
- **Generate this week's credits** with the **Generate** button issues this week's batch straight away, for example to start a new member mid-week or catch up after a missed run.
- Credits last a year from when they are issued.
- Booking a PT session uses the member's oldest unused credit with that trainer. Cancelling the session gives it back. A no-show still uses it.
- A session booked with no credit is **pending settlement**. Once the member has a credit, cash it off on the [gym schedule](gym-schedule.html#cash-off-and-cash-off-week).

## End a package

Tap the bin next to the member and tap **End**: "The client keeps their remaining credits until the current billing period ends, no charges after that." Tap **Keep** to leave it.

## Questions

?? A member has no credits this week.
Check their package is **Active** and not past due. Credits are only issued while the package is paid up. Then tap **Generate**.

?? Can I sell a one-off pack of 10 sessions?
You can sell a **PT Package** product with a number of sessions in your [shop](marketplace.html). Weekly credits for booking come from the packages on this screen.

?? Do unused credits roll over?
Yes. Unused credits stay available until they expire, a year after they were issued.

?? Can members see their credits?
Yes, members see their PT sessions and credits in their app.
