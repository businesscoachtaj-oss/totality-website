You can create your gym's Totality account yourself in a few minutes, on your phone or at totalityapp.com.au. Your 7-day free trial starts as soon as the account is made, with no card needed.

## Create your account

!steps
1. Get the Totality app from the App Store or Google Play, or go to **totalityapp.com.au** on a computer.
2. Tap **Get Started: It's Free**.
3. Fill in **First name**, **Last name** (optional), **Email address**, **Password** (at least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, and a number or symbol) and **Confirm password**.
4. If another gym or trainer gave you a referral code, type it in **Referral code**. If you opened their share link, it is filled in for you.
5. Tap **Create Account**.

!phone[The sign up screen](img/sign-up-form.png)

## Tell Totality you run a gym

Next you see **Welcome to Totality** with the line "Your 7-day free trial has started."

!steps
1. Under **HOW WILL YOU USE TOTALITY?** pick **Gym / Studio** ("I manage multiple trainers"). Solo Trainer is for one independent trainer, and does not give you the gym tools.
2. Under **YOUR BUSINESS**, type your **Gym or studio name**, for example Iron Works. You can change it later in Settings.
3. Read the **GETTING STARTED** tips and tap **Let's Go →**.

!phone[Pick Gym / Studio and type your gym's name](img/sign-up-welcome.png)

:::warn Pick Gym / Studio
Choose Gym / Studio here if you run a gym with staff. Each account can create one gym. If you picked the wrong option, email admintotality@gmail.com and we will fix it.
:::

## Set up your gym

The **Set up your gym** screen asks for your gym's **Address**, **Phone** and **Gym email**, so clients and trainers can find you. All three are optional. Fill them in and tap **Let's Go →**, or tap **Skip** and add them later in [Settings](gym-settings.html).

!phone[Set up your gym](img/sign-up-gym.png)

## Accept the terms

The first time you reach the dashboard you are asked to read and accept the Totality Terms & Conditions. Tap **Read the full Terms & Conditions** to read them, then **Accept & Continue**.

## The quick tour

Tali, the assistant inside Totality, then walks you round the dashboard in about a minute. She points out:

- **Your morning brief**: a short summary of today
- **Needs attention today**: the list of things to act on
- **Key numbers**: revenue, members, trainers, sessions and leads
- **Sections**: Members, Sales and billing, Schedule, Staff and Insights
- each tab along the bottom

There are 10 short steps. Tap **Next** to move on, **Back** to go back, or **Skip tour** at the top right to close it. The tour only shows once.

!phone[Tali's tour of the dashboard](img/sign-up-tour.png)

## What to do next

The dashboard shows a **Set up your gym** card with three steps: **Add your trainers**, **Add your members** and **Connect payments**. Each one ticks itself off when it is done. See [Your dashboard](dashboard.html).

## Signing in later

Open the app or totalityapp.com.au, type your email and password and tap **Log In**. Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot Password?**, type your email and tap **Send Reset Link**. The email has a link to set a new one.

## Questions

?? Do I need a credit card to start the trial?
No. The trial starts without a card. You add your card when you pick a plan.

?? Can someone else at the gym also be an admin?
Each gym has one owner account that signed up. Your trainers get their own logins with the trainer tools. If you need a second admin login, email admintotality@gmail.com.

?? I signed up as a Solo Trainer by mistake. What now?
Email admintotality@gmail.com from your account email and we will move you to a gym account.

?? I have been sent an invite to join a gym. Is this guide for me?
No. If a gym invited you as a trainer, see the trainer guide. If you are a member, see the member guide.

?? Can I see the tour again?
Not at the moment. Everything it covers is in this guide, starting with [Your dashboard](dashboard.html).
