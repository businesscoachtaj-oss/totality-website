Tali (say "tally") is the assistant inside Totality. She reads your gym's day, flags what needs you and drafts the messages you would otherwise write yourself. **Nothing is ever sent without you tapping send.** Every draft is yours to read and change first.

Tali comes with every gym plan. Your trainers use the same credits for their own clients.

:::tali
I do the first draft, you do the yes. Saves you a stack of typing, and I never send a thing on my own.
:::

## What Tali does for you

| Where | What Tali does | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | **Tali's brief**: today's classes, this week's sessions and how many things need you | Free |
| A lead | **Draft a text with Tali** or **Draft an email with Tali**: a friendly follow-up | 1 |
| A member who has gone quiet | **Draft a nudge with Tali** on their page, sent as an in-app message from you | 1 |
| A weekly check-in from a client you coach | Drafts your reply | 1 |
| New programme | **Draft it with Tali**: a full programme using only exercises in your library | 5 |
| Your own food log | Describe a meal in your own words and Tali logs it | Free |

Your trainers use Tali the same way in their app: check-in replies, nudges for quiet clients and programme drafts, using your gym's credits.

## How a draft works

!steps
1. Tap the Tali button. Tali shows she is working ("Tali is thinking hard...").
2. The draft appears with a **Drafted by Tali** badge.
3. Edit anything. "You're in charge of what gets sent."
4. Tap **Regenerate** for a different version, or use it: send it, or open it in Messages or Mail.

## Credits on each plan

Credits are shared by everyone at your gym, and reset each calendar month (Sydney time). See how many are left in **Settings**, under **TALI AI**: it shows credits left and the reset date.

## Getting more credits

On Studio and Club you can add **+3,000 credits a month for the whole gym** for A$19.99 a month. On Studio there is also the **Tali + Branding bundle**, +3,000 credits and your gym's branding for A$24.99 a month.

Tap **Get more credits** in Settings, or open **Sales**, then **Your Totality plan**, and find the **TALI AI** card. See [Your Totality plan](your-plan.html).

If credits run out mid-month, Tali's buttons show **Get more credits** until they reset or you add more. Everything else in Totality keeps working.

## Questions

?? Does Tali see my members' private details?
Tali only uses what is already in your gym's Totality account to write a draft, for example a member's recent activity or a lead's notes. She never shares one member's details with another.

?? Can Tali message members by herself?
No. Tali only drafts. You or your trainer always read it and tap send.

?? Do trainers get their own credits?
No. Trainers at your gym share the gym's credits. Only you can add more.

?? Is the brief free?
Yes. Tali's brief on the dashboard costs no credits.
