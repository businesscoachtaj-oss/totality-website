Invoices let you bill a client, a health fund or a funder with an itemised invoice, then email it and keep track of what's paid. Open **Invoices** from your dashboard's **QUICK ACTIONS**, or **Sales and billing**, **Invoices**.

!phones[Invoices](img/invoices.png)[A new invoice](img/invoices-new.png)

## Your invoice details

Do this once. On a client's **Invoices** section, tap **Your invoice details**:

- **BUSINESS NAME**, **ABN**, **ADDRESS**, **EMAIL** and **PHONE**
- **Registered for GST**: on, your invoices say Tax invoice and show the GST included in the total. Your prices are treated as GST inclusive.
- **HOW TO PAY (OPTIONAL)**: for example bank transfer to your BSB and account, with the invoice number as the reference

Tap **Save details**. Changing them later doesn't change invoices you've already written.

## Write an invoice

!steps
1. Open the client and tap **New invoice** under **Invoices**.
2. Under **WHO IS IT FOR?**, pick the client, or a funder or health fund already on their [Funding & Referrals](client-detail.html#funding-referrals). The name and email fill in. Add a **Claim, member or plan number** if there is one.
3. Check the **Invoice date** and when it's due: On receipt, 7 days, 14 days or 30 days.
4. For each line, write what it was (like "Initial consult 60 min"), the quantity and the **Price each ($)**. Add the **Date of service**, and if it's needed, who provided it, their provider number and a billing or item code. Tap **Add a line** for more.
5. Add a **NOTE ON THE INVOICE (OPTIONAL)**.
6. Tap **Save invoice**. Saving gives it a number. Nothing is sent yet.

## Send it and get paid

Open the invoice and tap **Email invoice**. It goes to the email on the invoice. Or tap **I gave it to them another way**.

When it's paid, under **MARK AS PAID**, tap how: Card, Payment link, Bank transfer, Cash or Paid by funder. Marking it paid only updates the invoice. It doesn't charge anyone. Tap **Mark as not paid** to undo it, or **Void this invoice** to cancel it.

## The Invoices screen

**Invoices** lists every invoice with **Unpaid**, **Paid** and **Drafts** tabs, and what's **Waiting to be paid** and **Overdue**. Tap one to open it.

## Questions

?? Does an invoice charge the client's card?
No. Invoices are for billing and record keeping. To take money, use [Charge a member](charges-pos.html) or a payment link.

?? Can I invoice an insurer or NDIS?
Yes. Add the funder on the client's Funding & Referrals first, then pick them under **WHO IS IT FOR?**.

?? Can trainers write invoices?
Yes, from their own app, for their own clients.
