Your trainers do most of the coaching in their own app. As the owner you can do everything they can for your own clients, and you look after the gym's shared programme templates and exercises. The [trainer guide](/help/totality-trainer/) covers the programme builder and session logging in full.

## Programmes

Open the **Schedule** tab, then **Programmes**.

- **My Programmes**: programmes you have built.
- **Collection**: your gym's shared templates, from you and your trainers.

!phones[Programmes](img/programmes-list.png)[New programme, or let Tali draft it](img/programmes-new.png)

### Build a programme

!steps
1. Tap **+ New**.
2. Enter the **Programme Name** (for example 4-Week Strength Block), an optional **Description** and the **Duration (weeks)**.
3. Tap **Create**. The programme editor opens.
4. Tap **+ Add Session** to add each workout (for example Upper Body A), then add exercises, sets and reps to it.
5. Use **Copy week** to repeat a week, **Delete week** to remove one, or **Extend** to add more weeks.

Or tap **Draft it with Tali**: describe the client and goal, pick days a week and weeks, and Tali builds the sessions using only exercises from your library (uses 5 credits). You can then change anything in the editor. See [Tali AI for gyms](tali.html).

### Share a programme with your gym

In the editor, tap the edit button and turn on **Share to Collection**. Add **Tags** (for example strength, beginner, 4-week) to make it easy to find. Your trainers then see it under Collection, and can tap **Copy to my programmes** to make their own version.

### Assign a programme

!steps
1. Open the programme and tap **Assign to Client**.
2. Tick the clients who should get it ("Select which clients receive this programme").
3. Pick the **Start date**: today, next Monday, or a date you choose.
4. Confirm.

**Assign to Me** puts it on your own account, so you can follow it in **My training**.

## Exercise library

Open the **Schedule** tab, then **Exercise library**. It has the Totality library plus any exercises your gym adds.

- Search, or tap the filter button to filter by **Goal** (Strength, Hypertrophy, Endurance, Mobility, Power, Rehabilitation) and **Muscle Group**.
- Tap an exercise for its muscles, best uses, coaching cues and video.
- Tap **+ Add** to add your own: **Name**, **Difficulty**, **Muscle Groups**, **Equipment**, how weight is entered (**External Weight**, **Bodyweight**, **Banded** or **Banded + Weight**), **Description**, **Why It's Effective**, **Coaching Cues**, **Recommended Rep Ranges**, **Goals**, **Use Cases** and an **Exercise Video** (upload a file or paste a YouTube link).

!phone[The exercise library](img/programmes-exercises.png)

Only the Totality team can delete exercises.

## Check-ins from clients you coach

If you coach clients yourself, their weekly check-ins come to you. Your dashboard shows "2 check-ins waiting for your reply". Tap it to open the review screen, read each check-in and reply. Tali can draft the reply for you. Check-ins for your trainers' clients go to those trainers.

## Nutrition

Trainers set calorie and macro targets for their clients and see what they log, in the trainer app. Members log their food in their own app.

## Your own training and food

Under the **More** tab, then **Insights**:

- **My training**: programmes assigned to you. Tap a session to log it.
- **My training history**: your logged workouts.
- **My food log**: track your own meals and macros, with **Add food**, barcode scanning and saved meals.

## Questions

?? Can I edit a programme a trainer made?
You can see your gym's shared templates in Collection and copy one to your own programmes to change it. Each trainer edits their own.

?? Can a member have more than one programme?
Yes. One is marked **PRIMARY**, which is the one they follow by default.

?? Where do I see a member's workouts?
On their page under **TRAINING SUMMARY**, then **View full session history**.
