Tap any member on the **Clients** list to open their page. It has everything about them, top to bottom.

!phones[Profile and membership](img/client-detail-top.png)[Payment and funding](img/client-detail-payment.png)

## Profile

Phone, date of birth (with their age), emergency contact name and phone. Tap **Edit** to change them, then **Save**.

## Membership

Their status (Active, Inactive or Lead), **Member Since** and **Assigned Trainer**.

- **Reassign Trainer**: give them to a different trainer.
- **Membership, freeze and attendance**: opens their [member status](check-in-freezes.html#member-status), where you can freeze their membership and see their visits.

## Intake (PAR-Q and waiver)

The **COMPLIANCE** card shows **Intake form not completed** with a **Complete Intake** button, or **Intake Complete** with the date and a **View Intake** button.

Do the intake with the member, on your phone or tablet, when they join:

!steps
1. Tap **Complete Intake**.
2. **Emergency Contact**: their **Contact Name** and **Contact Phone**. Tap **Next**.
3. **PAR-Q** (Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire): seven yes or no health questions. Each starts on **No**. Tap **Yes** where it applies and add notes. Tap **Next**.
4. **Health History**: **Injuries** and **Medical Conditions**. **Goals**: their **Primary Goal** (Lose Weight, Build Muscle, Improve Fitness, Sport Performance or Rehabilitation) and **Fitness Level** (Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced). Tap **Next**.
5. **Liability Waiver**: the member types their full name, draws their signature with their finger, and ticks **I have read and agree to the above**.
6. Tap **Save Intake**.

!phones[Step 1: emergency contact](img/client-detail-intake.png)[Step 2: PAR-Q](img/client-detail-parq.png)

**View Intake** shows the answers, the PAR-Q, their goals and the signed waiver with who signed and when.

:::warn A Yes on the PAR-Q
If a member answers Yes to any PAR-Q question, ask them to get clearance from their doctor before they start training.
:::

## Payment

Their card on file (for example Visa ending 4242), or **No payment method** with **Request Payment Method** and **Add Card Manually**. See [Failed payments and cards](failed-payments.html#ask-a-member-to-add-a-card).

## Funding & Referrals

For members whose training is funded, for example through Medicare, DVA, Workcover, NDIS or Aged Care.

- **Add Referral**: pick the **Funding Type** and fill in what it needs, such as the referring GP and provider number, the Workcover claim and case manager, or NDIS plan dates and support coordinator. You can upload the referral document.
- **Service Codes**: your gym's list of item codes and rates for funded services. These can also be attached to [PT packages](pt-packages.html).

## Tali's nudge

If the member has not used the app for 14 days or more, a card says "Zack has gone quiet" with **Draft a nudge with Tali** (uses 1 credit). Tali writes a friendly message in your voice, you edit it, and **Send message** sends it to them in the app. See [Tali AI for gyms](tali.html).

## Training

- **TRAINING SUMMARY**: total sets logged, when they last trained, their weight trend and strength progress, and **View full session history**.
- **ASSIGNED PROGRAMME**: their programme (the main one is marked **PRIMARY**), this week's sessions with **Run Session** to coach one live, and **Remove from client**. See [Programmes](programmes.html).

## Admin notes

Private notes about the member, with who wrote them and when. Type in **Add a note...** and tap **Save Note**. Members never see these.

## Deactivate

At the very bottom, **Deactivate Client** makes them inactive (or **Reactivate Client** brings them back). See [Members](members.html#member-statuses).

## Questions

?? Can members fill in their own intake?
Members answer their own onboarding questions when they first set up the app. The intake here is the version you do together at the gym, with the waiver and signature.

?? Can members see admin notes?
No. Members never see them.

?? I need to change a member's email.
Email admintotality@gmail.com with the old and new address.
