Everything about your gym's account lives in **Settings**. To get there, tap the **More** tab, then **Settings**. The screen is titled **Gym details**.

!phones[Gym details and current usage](img/gym-settings-details.png)[Tools, Tali credits and calendar sync](img/gym-settings-tools.png)

## Change your gym's details

!steps
1. Open **More**, then **Settings**.
2. Change any of **Gym name** (required), **Address**, **Phone** and **Contact email**.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Save changes**.

Your gym's name shows at the top of your dashboard, on member emails and in the app your members use.

## Current usage

The **Current usage** card shows how many **Active clients** you have out of your plan's limit (for example 48/500) and how many **Trainers**. Under it, **Subscription & Billing** opens your Totality plan. See [Your Totality plan](your-plan.html).

## What kind of business is this?

Pick **Gym or studio** or **Allied health or clinic**. A clinic gets services, intake forms and treatment notes, each with its own switch, and clinic wording across the app. See [Allied health and clinic tools](clinic-tools.html).

## Tali AI credits

The **TALI AI** card shows how many credits are left this month, a bar of how much you have used and the date they reset. **Get more credits** opens your plan, where you can add more. See [Tali AI for gyms](tali.html).

## Calendar sync

The **CALENDAR SYNC** card puts your bookings into the calendar on your phone or computer. Your own 1:1 sessions, classes and lead appointments assigned to you show up and stay up to date.

- **Add to Google Calendar** or **Add to Apple Calendar** subscribes your calendar to a private link. It is one way: changes in Totality show in your calendar, not the other way round. Apple refreshes roughly every 15 to 60 minutes, Google every few hours.
- **Reset link** makes a new private link. Any calendar using the old link stops updating.

## Refer & Earn

**REFER & EARN** ("Give 20%, get 20%") makes your referral code. Tap **Get my referral code**, then **Share my code**. The card counts how many people you have **Referred**, how many **Converted** and the **Discounts active**. See [Plans for gyms](plans.html#refer-another-gym).

## Questions

?? I changed the gym name but the old one still shows.
Make sure you tapped **Save changes** at the bottom of Settings. Then pull down on the dashboard to refresh.

?? Where do I change my own name or password?
To change your password, sign out and use **Forgot Password?** on the sign in screen. To change the name on your login, email admintotality@gmail.com.

?? Can I have more than one gym on one account?
No. Each account runs one gym. If you run more than one location, email admintotality@gmail.com to talk about Enterprise.
