**Clients** is every member of your gym, across all your trainers. In Totality, members are called clients. Open the **Members** tab, then **Clients**, or tap **Active members** on the dashboard.

!phone[Your members list](img/members-list.png)

## What each row shows

- Name, email and status: **Active**, **Inactive** or **Lead**.
- **Trainer**: who they are assigned to.
- **Last session**: when they last logged a workout.
- Badges:
  - **Intake ✓** or **Intake Pending**: whether their intake form (PAR-Q, health history and waiver) is done.
  - **Card ✓** or **No Card**: whether they have a card saved with your gym.
  - **Expiring**: their membership ends in the next 15 days.

Tap a member to open their [client detail](client-detail.html).

## Search and filter

- **Search clients...** finds members by name or email.
- **All** shows everyone.
- **Expiring** shows members whose membership ends soon.
- **At risk** shows members with no app activity for 14 days or more. Tali can draft a nudge for them from their page. See [Tali AI for gyms](tali.html).
- A chip for each trainer shows just their members.

## Do things in bulk

!steps
1. Tap **Select**.
2. Tap members to tick them, or select all.
3. Pick an action at the bottom:
   - **Message**: send the same message to everyone selected. It arrives in their Messages.
   - **Assign**: give them all to one trainer (**Assign Trainer**).
   - **Status**: set them all to Active, Inactive or Lead (**Change Status**).
4. Tap **Cancel** to leave select mode.

## Assign a trainer

To give one member to a trainer, open the member and tap **Reassign Trainer** under MEMBERSHIP. To move several at once, use **Select** then **Assign** as above. Assigning a member to a trainer lets that trainer coach them: programmes, sessions, check-ins and messages.

## Member statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | A current member. Counts towards your plan's member limit |
| Inactive | Not current. Keeps their history. Does not count towards your limit |
| Lead | Someone interested who has not joined yet |

To change one member, open them and tap **Deactivate Client** or **Reactivate Client** at the bottom of their page.

## Questions

?? Why is a member missing?
Check the search box is empty and **All** is selected. Members appear here once they have joined your gym. If you invited someone who already had a Totality account, they appear after they accept your invite.

?? What is the difference between a member and a client?
Nothing. Totality calls everyone who trains at your gym a client.

?? Does making someone inactive cancel their payments?
No. It only changes their status in Totality. To stop a membership payment, cancel it in [Memberships](memberships.html).

?? Can I delete a member?
You cannot delete a member's account from the gym. Make them Inactive instead, which keeps their history. A member can delete their own account from their Settings.
