This page is about what **you** pay Totality. (What members pay you is in [Memberships](memberships.html).) Open the **Sales** tab, then **Your Totality plan**, or **Settings**, then **Subscription & Billing**.

If you are still on your free trial, it opens **Choose a plan**. Once you have subscribed, it opens your **Subscription**.

## Subscribe

!steps
1. On **Choose a plan**, pick **Monthly** or **Yearly · Save 15%**.
2. Read what each plan includes. While trialling, the top card says **Free trial** and **CURRENT**.
3. Tap **Subscribe** on Studio, Club or Enterprise.
4. Stripe's secure checkout opens with the price. On a monthly plan, your first-months discount is already applied. Enter your card and confirm.
5. Come back to Totality. Your plan shows as **CURRENT** within a minute or so.

!phones[Choosing a plan during the trial](img/your-plan-choose.png)[Your subscription](img/your-plan-subscription.png)

Your paid plan starts when you subscribe, and your card is charged then. See [Plans for gyms](plans.html) for prices and what each plan includes.

## Your subscription

The **Subscription** screen shows:

- **CURRENT PLAN** and its status: **Active**, **Free trial**, **Payment failed** or **Cancelled**.
- **Current period**: the dates you have paid for.
- **Client limit**: your plan's member limit.
- The **TALI AI** and **BRANDING** add-on cards (see below).
- **Manage**: **Change plan**, **Connect Stripe (member billing)**, **Products**, **Member billing**, **Charge a client (one-off)**, **Cancel subscription** or **Reactivate subscription**, and **Manage billing (Stripe)**.

## Change plan

To move to a different plan once you are subscribed:

!steps
1. Open **More**, then **Settings**, then **Subscription & Billing**.
2. Tap **Change Plan**.
3. Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly · Save 15%**.
4. Tap **Switch to this plan** on the plan you want.

You see **Plan change scheduled**: "Your new plan will take effect at your next billing date."

!phone[Change plan from Subscription & Billing](img/your-plan-change.png)

The **Change plan** row on the Sales subscription screen opens the plan picker, which is for starting a new subscription. If you tap **Subscribe** there while you already have a plan, you will see "This gym already has an active subscription", so use the steps above instead.

## Add-ons

On the **TALI AI** card:

- **+3,000 credits a month** for the whole gym, A$19.99 a month (Studio and Club).
- **Tali + Branding bundle**, +3,000 credits plus your logo and colour for the whole gym, A$24.99 a month, A$4.99 less than buying both (Studio).

Enterprise shows "Unlimited Tali AI is included in Enterprise."

The **BRANDING** card opens [Branding](branding.html), where you add (Studio) or set up (Club and Enterprise) your logo, name and colour.

!phone[Add-ons on your subscription](img/your-plan-addons.png)

Anything you already pay for on its own is switched over when you buy a bundle, so you are never charged twice.

## Update your card or see invoices

Tap **Manage billing (Stripe)**. Stripe's billing page opens, where you can change your card, see past invoices and download receipts.

If a payment fails, the screen shows **Payment failed** with **Update payment method**, and your dashboard shows "Your Totality payment failed". Update your card straight away to avoid any interruption.

## Cancel

!steps
1. Tap **Cancel subscription**.
2. Read "You will keep access until the end of the current billing period. You can reactivate before then."
3. Tap **Cancel plan**, or **Keep plan** to stay.

The screen then shows "Cancels on" with the date. Changed your mind? Tap **Reactivate subscription** before that date.

When the period ends, your account becomes read only, like at the end of a trial. Your members, programmes and history are kept safe. Choose a plan any time to switch everything back on.

:::note Members' payments
Cancelling your Totality plan does not cancel your members' memberships with you. Cancel those in [Memberships](memberships.html) first if you are closing your gym.
:::

## Questions

?? Can I switch from monthly to yearly?
Yes. In **Settings**, then **Subscription & Billing**, tap **Change Plan**, pick **Yearly · Save 15%** and tap **Switch to this plan** on your plan. The change takes effect at your next billing date.

?? Where do I get a tax invoice?
Tap **Manage billing (Stripe)** and open the invoice you need.

?? What happens if my card fails?
Your plan shows **Payment failed**. Tap **Update payment method** and add a working card. Stripe tries again.

?? Can I pause my plan?
No. You can cancel at the end of a period and subscribe again later. Your data is kept.
