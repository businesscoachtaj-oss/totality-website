## What is in Settings

- **Your profile.** Tap your initials or **Add a profile picture** to add a photo.
- **Your plan.** Opens Plans. See [Plans and billing](plans.html).
- **Totality Nutrition.** What is shared between the two apps. See [With Totality Nutrition](nutrition.html).
- **Your goal and week.** Change your goal, level, days, time and kit.
- **Tali and your data.** What Tali uses, and turning her on or off. See [Tali](tali.html).
- **Units and region.** kg or lb, cm or ft in, kcal or kJ, and more.
- **Privacy policy**, **Terms** and **Sign out**.
- **Delete my account.**

## Units and region

!phone[Units and region](img/settings-units.png)

Choose your country and language, and the units for body weight, measurements, energy, water and lifting weights. Changing these never changes your saved data.

## Sleep sounds

!phone[Sleep sounds](img/settings-sleep.png)

Open **Sleep sounds** from Quick access on Home. Tap a scene, like Rain on a tin roof or Brown noise, and it fades out so it doesn't wake you. Save a mix you like and it shows under **Your mixes**. Free on every plan.

## Delete your account

**Delete my account** deletes your account and everything in it, in Totality Training, Totality Nutrition and Totality, and cancels any paid plan. It cannot be undone.

## Questions

?? Does changing my goal change my programme?
It changes which programmes are matched to you. To switch, pick one from **Choose from a template**.
