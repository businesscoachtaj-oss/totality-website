The book button on the bar opens the exercise library. It is free on every plan.

!phones[The exercise library](img/library-list.png)[One exercise](img/library-detail.png)

## Find an exercise

- Search by name, like squat or curl.
- Tap the three dots next to the search box to filter by muscle group, equipment or goal.
- Tap an exercise to see its demo, the muscles targeted, the equipment, a description, the recommended rep ranges and coaching cues.

The same details open from **How to** on the workout screen.

## Questions

?? Can I add an exercise from the library to my workout?
Yes, when you edit a workout or build a routine. See [My programmes and editing](my-programmes.html).
