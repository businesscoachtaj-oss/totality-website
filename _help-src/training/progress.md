## Workout history

!phones[Workout history](img/progress-history.png)[Personal bests](img/progress-bests.png)

Open **Workout history** from Quick access on Home. It is free on every plan [[Free]].

- **Sessions** lists every workout by date, with sets, volume, time, average RPE and any new personal best. Tap one to see every set.
- **Personal bests** lists each exercise with your best estimated one rep max, the set it came from and your heaviest weight.
- **Filter by exercise** to find one lift.
- **Log a workout** adds a workout you did without the app.

## Progress

!phones[Strength charts](img/progress-strength.png)[Body weight](img/progress-weight.png)

[[Training]] The chart button on the bar opens Progress, with four tabs:

- **Photos.** Add front, side and back photos to compare over time.
- **Measures.** Body measurements.
- **Weight.** Your daily weight and a 7 day trend. Tap **Log Weight**. Your weight is shared with Totality Nutrition.
- **Strength.** A chart of the estimated one rep max for each lift.

On Free, Progress shows what it includes and a **See plans** button.

## Rep max calculator

Open it from **Rep max calculator** in Quick access on Home, the row at the top of Strength, or the calculator button on an exercise during a workout.

The **Rep max calculator** works out your one rep max from a set you've done, and the right weight for any rep target.

!steps
1. Pick the lift under **From logged training** (it uses your best recent set), or tap **Or type a set** and enter the **Weight** and **Reps**.
2. Set **Reps left in the tank**: how many more you could have done. None means you went to failure.
3. Read your **Estimated one rep max**, then switch views: **Rep maxes** (your best for 1 to 12, 15 and 20 reps), **Percentages** (for programmes written as a percentage of your max), **What to lift** (the plates for each side of the bar, or the dumbbell for each hand, for the reps you want) and **Where you stand**.

**Where you stand** compares your max with your body weight on the squat, bench press, deadlift and overhead press. It's a general guide from body weight multiples, not a ranking against other lifters. **Share where I stand** makes a picture to share. Your body weight is never on it.

The estimate blends seven well known formulas. It's an estimate, not a test: warm up, use a spotter for heavy singles, and stop a set if your form goes. More than 30 reps all up is too many to estimate from, so use a heavier set.



## Questions

?? How is the one rep max worked out?
It is an estimate from your best set, for example 85 kg for 8 reps. It is a guide, not a test.

?? I log my weight in Totality Nutrition. Do I need to log it here too?
No. It is the same weight log in both apps.
