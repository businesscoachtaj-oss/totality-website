A team is a business, club or group that pays for Totality Training for its people. Open **Settings** and tap **Teams**.

!phones[Teams in Settings](img/teams-settings.png)[Join with a code, or set a team up](img/teams-join.png)

## Join a team with a code

!steps
1. Sign up or log in to Totality Training.
2. Open **Settings** and tap **Teams**.
3. Enter the code your team gave you, like IRONWORK-4K7MPQ, and tap **Join the team**.

Your plan is covered straight away and you pay nothing. If you were paying for Training yourself, your own plan stops at the end of what you have paid for, so you are never charged twice.

!phone[A seat covered by your team](img/teams-member.png)

Your team can see that you joined and which seat you have. They cannot see your workouts, weight, photos or progress. To go back to your own plan, tap **Leave this team**. Everything you have logged stays.

## Set up a team

!phones[Name the team and pick the seats](img/teams-setup.png)[The total, then payment](img/teams-total.png)

- **Training seat:** A$6.99 a month or A$71.30 a year, each.
- **Training + AI seat:** A$8.99 a month or A$91.70 a year, each.
- A team starts at 3 seats. You can mix the two seat types.
- A first team paying monthly gets 50% off its first 2 months.

!steps
1. In **Teams**, tap **Set up a team**.
2. Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**, name the team and set how many of each seat you need.
3. Tap **Continue to payment** and pay by card with Stripe.

## Run your team

!phones[Your team: seats, the code and invites](img/teams-admin.png)[People, seats and billing](img/teams-people.png)

- **Team code.** Share it, or tap **Invite by email** and we send the invite for you. **Make a new code** stops the old one working. People already in stay in.
- **People.** Everyone who has joined, with their seat. Tap **Manage** to move someone to the other seat type or remove them. Invites that are still waiting show here too.
- **Take a seat yourself** if you want your own plan covered by the team.
- **Add or change seats.** Extra seats are charged straight away for the rest of the month or year. Seats nobody is using can come off, and your next bill is lower, with no refund in between.
- **Billing and invoices** opens Stripe for your card, invoices and cancelling.

You see who has joined and which seat they have. You never see what anyone lifts, weighs or logs.

## Questions

?? What happens when a team ends, or I am removed?
You keep your account and your workout history. Without a seat or a plan of your own you are on Free.

?? Can I be in two teams?
No. Leave the team you are in first.

?? How do I cancel a team?
Open **Billing and invoices** on your team screen. The team runs to the end of the period you have paid for, then ends.
