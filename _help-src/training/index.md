Totality Training is a workout tracker for people who train on their own. You follow a programme, log every set, and the app adjusts your weights and your sets from how you actually trained. It runs in your web browser at **totality-training.vercel.app**, on a phone or a computer. The iPhone and Android apps were sent to Apple and Google for review on 21 November 2026. Your account is the same everywhere, and it is the same login as Totality and Totality Nutrition.

!phone[Home, with today's workout and Tali's note about a weight going up](img/home-top.png)

## The plans

- [[Free]] A$0. Unlimited workout logging, the full exercise library with demos, 3 of your own routines, the Strength Foundations programme, 3 questions for Tali a day and Sleep sounds.
- [[Training]] A$8.99 a month or A$91.70 a year. All 375 Totality programmes, unlimited routines, weights that go up by themselves when you earn it, sets that adjust to how each muscle feels, Progress (strength charts, personal bests and body stats) and the weekly review.
- [[Training + AI]] A$11.99 a month or A$122.30 a year. Everything in Training, plus Tali builds programmes around your week and kit, gives her take after each workout and writes up your week. 100 Tali credits a month.

Every new account starts with a **7 day free trial** [[Trial]] of everything in Training + AI. No card is needed. When it ends you move to Free unless you pick a plan, and nothing you logged is deleted. See [Plans and billing](plans.html).

## Get going in five minutes

!steps
1. Open **totality-training.vercel.app** and tap **Start free**. Enter your first name, email and a password. See [Create your account](create-account.html).
2. Answer four quick questions: your goal, how long you have been lifting, your week and where you train.
3. Check the programme it matched for you and tap **Start this programme**.
4. On Home, tap **Start workout** when it is a training day. See [Doing a workout](workout.html).
5. After your workout, answer **How did that feel?** so next week's sets fit. See [After a workout](after-workout.html).

:::tali
G'day, I'm Tali. Log every set, even the ugly ones. The more honest your numbers, the better I can move your weights for you.
:::

## Where things live

The bar along the bottom has five buttons:

| Button | What it is |
|---|---|
| Home (house) | Today's workout, this week, today's food from Totality Nutrition and Quick access. See [Home](home.html) |
| Train (list) | Your programme, the templates, Tali's builder, your routines and My programmes. See [Choose a programme](choose-programme.html) |
| Tali (her face, in the middle) | Chat with Tali about your training. See [Tali](tali.html) |
| Progress (chart) | Strength charts, body weight, measurements and photos [[Training]]. See [Progress and history](progress.html) |
| Exercise library (book) | Every exercise with demos and cues. See [Exercise library](library.html) |

**Settings** opens from the gear at the top of Home. The purple **?** button on most screens lets you report a problem.

## Questions

?? Is Totality Training really free?
Yes. The Free plan is A$0 with no time limit. You keep unlimited logging, the exercise library, 3 routines, the Strength Foundations programme and 3 questions for Tali a day.

?? Do I need a card for the trial?
No. The 7 day trial needs no card. If you don't pick a plan, you move to Free when it ends.

?? I already use Totality Nutrition. Do I need a new account?
No. Log in with your Nutrition email and password. Your weight, measurements and photos come with you, and paying for Nutrition makes Training 40% off. See [With Totality Nutrition](nutrition.html).

?? Can I use it on my computer?
Yes. Open totality-training.vercel.app in any browser and log in.
