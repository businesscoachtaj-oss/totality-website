## Choose from a template

!phones[Four quick questions](img/choose-programme-questions.png)[The templates that fit](img/choose-programme-list.png)

!steps
1. On **Train**, tap **Choose from a template**.
2. Answer the four questions: **Where do you train?**, **How many days a week?**, **What do you want to focus on?** and **How long have you been lifting?**. They start on your onboarding answers.
3. The list shows every template that fits, with its days, weeks, level, time and workouts.
4. Tap a template to see every workout and exercise.
5. Tap **Start this programme** (or **Switch to this programme** if you are on one already).

**Where do you train?** has five answers: **Full gym**, **Dumbbells**, **No equipment**, **Bars and rings** (a pull-up bar, dip bars or rings) and **Kettlebells**.

**What do you want to focus on?** has everyday training and sports and styles. Only the ones with a programme for your kit and days are shown.

| Everyday training | Sports and styles |
|---|---|
| Whole body | Powerlifting, Street Lifting, Olympic Lifting |
| Glutes and legs | Classic Bodybuilding, New School Bodybuilding, Powerbuilding |
| Glutes and arms | Strongman, Circus Strongman |
| Strength | Gymnastics, Calisthenics |
| Lean and fit | Kettlebell, Athletic Performance, Hybrid (lifting and running) |
| Health Guidelines | |

Every style comes at 2 to 6 days a week and at Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced. The level changes the exercises too, not just the sets and reps. For example, Strongman beginners use sandbags, farmer's carries and the trap bar, and the log, yoke and stones come in at Intermediate.

**Health Guidelines** is built on the World Health Organization's physical activity guidelines for adults: strength work for every major muscle on 2 or more days, and at least 150 minutes of moderate cardio a week. The 4, 5 and 6 day versions reach 150 minutes in the workouts. The 2 and 3 day versions tell you how much brisk walking to add on other days. Every session starts with a few minutes of mobility, and there's balance work through the week. It comes with a full gym, dumbbells or no equipment. It's our programme, not made or endorsed by the WHO.

**Classic Bodybuilding** trains one or two areas a day. **New School Bodybuilding** trains every muscle at least twice a week.

With **Dumbbells** you can pick Home Dumbbell, Dumbbell Upper Lower, Dumbbell Push Pull Legs, Dumbbell Glutes and Legs or Dumbbell Burn. With **No equipment** you can pick No Equipment, Bodyweight Upper Lower or Bodyweight Burn. Not every one comes at every number of days.

## What a programme looks like

!phone[A programme's details](img/choose-programme-detail.png)

Each programme shows its days a week and which days they land on, how many weeks it runs, the level and the kit, and how it progresses. Powerlifting, for example, adds 2.5 kg to the main lifts when every set is done. Every programme ends with a lighter week. Below are the workouts, day by day, with sets and reps for each exercise.

## Swap an exercise before you start

Missing a piece of kit? On a programme's page, tap **Swap** next to any exercise before you start it.

!steps
1. Tap **Swap** next to the exercise.
2. Pick from the list. It shows exercises for the same muscles that you can do with the kit you picked, closest first. Search if you want something else.
3. The exercise changes in every week of the programme it's in, both halves. It shows **Swapped from** with **Undo**.
4. Tap **Start this programme**.

You can also swap during a workout. See [Your workout](workout.html).

## How a programme moves on

Every programme ends with a lighter week with fewer sets, so you recover. Before that it changes in blocks, and how depends on the style.

- **Most programmes** change halfway through. The first lifts each day get heavier for fewer reps (no equipment programmes move to a harder version instead, like Archer Push-Ups), and most other exercises swap for a close variation with the same kit.
- **Powerlifting, Street Lifting, Strongman and Olympic Lifting** (Intermediate and Advanced) run three blocks: **Volume** (more reps at moderate weights), **Strength** (heavier sets of 3 to 5) and **Peak** (heavy doubles and singles with less accessory work). The lighter last week has a test day if you feel fresh.
- **Athletic Performance** (Intermediate and Advanced) runs **Strength**, **Power** and **Speed** blocks. In the speed block the heavy lifts become speed lifts.
- **Gymnastics Strength and Calisthenics** (Beginner and Intermediate) run **Learn**, **Build** and **Next step** blocks. In the last block your main moves step up to the next harder version.
- **New School Bodybuilding** has a target for each week. Each half starts about 3 reps short of failure and builds to failure on isolation work (Beginners stop 1 rep short).
- **Hybrid** runs get about 10% longer each week, with an easier week every fourth week.
- Beginners on the barbell sports keep two halves while they learn the lifts.

On a programme's page, tap the **Weeks** chips to see each block. When a week has a target, or a new block starts, your workout shows it at the top.

## Set types

Some exercises on Intermediate and Advanced programmes use a set type: a **drop set**, **myo reps**, **rest-pause** or **AMRAP** (as many reps as you can in the time). They're used on the last exercises of a workout, where they help. The workout screen shows a badge on these exercises and how to do each one.

New School Bodybuilding also uses **lengthened partials** on exercises with a big stretch. On the last set, when you can't do another full rep, keep going with half reps in the stretched part. The exercise's note says when.

On Free, **Strength Foundations** and every **Health Guidelines** programme are free to start. The others show a lock and need [[Training]].

## Questions

?? What happens to my history if I switch?
Nothing. Your workouts and personal bests are kept. The new programme starts from week 1.

?? Can I change a template's exercises?
Yes. Once you start it, it is saved under **My programmes**, and every workout is yours to edit. See [My programmes and editing](my-programmes.html).
