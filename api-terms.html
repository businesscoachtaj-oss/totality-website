4. What you must not do

You must not:

  1. Resell, sublicense, share or give the Content, your API key or your site ID to anyone else.

  6. Present the Content as medical advice, or claim it diagnoses, treats or prevents any condition.
  7. Try to get around rate limits, key checks, website locks, signed video links or other security.
  8. Use the API or widget in a way that breaks the law or harms our systems or other customers.

5. Videos and images

5.1 The API serves our animated exercise videos only. Videos are delivered through short-lived links the API returns. Play them inside your app using those links.

5.2 You must not download, store, re-host or put our videos on social media, a CDN or any other platform, or give your users links that let them download them.

5.3 You may pass video through your own server (for example to keep your key off a phone), as long as the video is never saved to disk or a shared cache on your side.

5.4 Licence plan customers who are given video files to host themselves follow their contract instead of 5.1 to 5.3.

6. Caching

6.1 You may cache exercise text and lists for up to 30 days, and images for up to 24 hours, to make your app faster.

6.2 Videos may only be cached for normal playback (a browser or device buffer).

6.3 When your plan ends, delete any cached Content within 30 days.

7. Attribution

7.1 Include this sentence in your terms of service, privacy policy or an about page: "Exercise content provided by Totality (totality360.com.au)."

7.2 An in-app credit or link is welcome but not required.

7.3 Don't suggest that Totality endorses your product, and don't use the Totality name or logo as part of your product name, domain or logo.

8. API keys and limits

8.1 Keep your API key secret. Don't put it in a mobile app or public website code. You are responsible for all calls made with your key.

8.2 Each plan has a monthly call limit. A call is one request to the API, and each video play counts as one call. When you reach your limit, the API returns an error until the next month or until you upgrade.

8.3 Tell us straight away if your key is lost or misused. We can cancel and replace it.

9.1 Paid plans are billed monthly in advance through Stripe, in Australian dollars. Prices include any GST that applies.

9.2 Founders prices stay the same for as long as your plan stays active. For any other price change we give at least 30 days' notice, and the new price applies from your next billing period after the notice ends.

9.3 If a payment fails, we retry it for 7 days. After that, we may pause your key or widget until payment goes through. Pausing does not cancel your plan.

9.4 You can cancel at any time. Your plan runs until the end of the period you've paid for. We don't refund part periods, except where the Australian Consumer Law requires it.

9.5 The Sandbox plan is free, has no service commitment and can be changed or withdrawn at any time.
