Who it’s for

Pick the cover that fits how you work. BizCover helps you compare and buy it online.

Personal trainers and coaches

Cover for you and your clients

  • Allied health combined liability. Bundles professional indemnity and public liability cover into one policy, for 200+ types of allied health, fitness and beauty professions.
  • Public liability. Covers compensation costs and legal fees if you negligently cause injury to a member of the public or damage to property.
  • Professional indemnity. Covers financial losses and legal fees as a result of alleged or actual negligence in your professional services or advice.
  • Personal accident and illness. Covers loss of income if you are unable to work as a result of an injury or illness in or outside of work. Limits apply.
Gyms, studios and clinics

Cover for the whole business

  • Public liability. For slips, trips and property damage.
  • Contents. Covers loss or damage to business items at the insured location, such as equipment, stock, machinery and furniture.
  • Business interruption. Covers loss of income following an interruption to your business following an insurable event.
  • Business insurance package. An insurance bundle that can be customised to include cover for your tools and equipment, stock, owned business buildings, and more.

Questions

Who is BizCover?

BizCover helps Australian small businesses compare quotes from multiple insurers and buy their cover online in minutes, with easy and professional claims support. It covers fitness professionals including personal trainers, gym owners, group exercise, yoga and pilates instructors.

Does the code work if I don’t use Totality?

Yes. Anyone can use code TOTALITY to get $20 off each policy with BizCover.

Does Totality give insurance advice?

No. We point you to BizCover and remind you when your cover is due. BizCover and the insurer you choose can answer questions about the cover itself.

Who handles a claim?

BizCover and your insurer. Your policy documents explain how to make a claim.
