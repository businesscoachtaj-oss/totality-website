What does Totality do for a practice?

The clinic tools come with every plan, solo or team. Switch on the ones you use.

Your own services

Initial consult, follow-up, extended session: each with a length, a price and, on team plans, a different price per practitioner.

Book by service

Pick the service when you book and the length is set for you. Recurring bookings for regulars, and a schedule for the whole team.

Intake forms you write

Short answer, long answer, yes or no, or pick one. Attach a form to a service and fill it in with the client. Answers keep the questions as they were asked.

Treatment notes

What the client told you, what you found, your assessment and the plan. Kept on the client, private to the treating team, never shown to the client.

Funding and referrals

Medicare, DVA, Workcover, NDIS, Aged Care, private health, or a funding type you name yourself such as TAC. Referrer, case manager, plan dates and the referral document.

Provider numbers and billing codes

Keep provider numbers by fund, and your own library of billing codes with a description and a rate, ready to attach to a referral.

Invoices

Itemised invoices for the client, a health fund or a funder, with the service, date, practitioner, provider number and billing code on each line. Email it from the app and mark it paid.

Payments

Through your own Stripe account: a payment link by text or email, a charge to a saved card, or packs and weekly subscriptions. Book a service and Totality offers to take payment with the price filled in.

Exercise programmes and classes

Build a home programme from the exercise library with video, and run group classes with waitlists. Clients follow along in their free app.

Team, payroll and compliance

Invite practitioners, see their client loads, run rosters and a payroll summary with Xero export, and track registration and insurance documents with expiry alerts.

What it does not do yet

So you know before you start.

Claims

Totality does not lodge claims with Medicare, DVA, NDIS or HICAPS. It keeps the details together for when you do.

Card readers

There is no tap to pay terminal. In person, the client pays a link on their phone or you charge their saved card.

Client-filled intake forms

Intake forms are filled in by staff with the client for now. Sending a form for the client to complete at home is on the way.

Treatment notes are health records. Totality limits who can see them, but your own privacy and record keeping obligations still apply. See Security and Privacy.

Common questions

Is Totality only for gyms and personal trainers?

No. Totality is for anyone who sees clients by session, class or appointment. At sign up you choose solo or studio owner, then fitness or allied health. Choosing allied health switches on services, intake forms, treatment notes and funding, and the app uses clinic words like practitioner and appointment.

Which professions does it suit?

Exercise physiologists, physiotherapists, remedial massage therapists, dietitians, podiatrists, occupational therapists, psychologists and similar practices that book by appointment and deal with referrals or funded clients. It suits practices that also run exercise programmes or classes especially well, because those are built in.

Who can see treatment notes?

Only the practitioner who wrote them, the clinic's admin, and practitioners assigned to that client. Clients cannot see treatment notes. A note can only be edited by the person who wrote it.

How do clients pay?

Through your own Stripe account. You can send a payment link by text or email, charge a card the client has saved, or sell packs and weekly subscriptions that are used up as appointments are booked. After you book a service, Totality offers those options with the price already filled in, or you can write an itemised invoice and mark it paid when the money arrives. Totality takes no commission; a 3.5% platform fee applies to payments taken in the app, taken from your payout.

Does Totality lodge Medicare, DVA or health fund claims?

No. Totality records the referral, the funder, provider numbers and your billing codes against the client so the details are in one place. It does not lodge claims with Medicare, DVA, NDIS or HICAPS, and it does not replace your claiming terminal or portal.

Can I switch the clinic tools off?

Yes. Services, intake forms and treatment notes each have their own switch in Settings. Switching one off hides it and nothing already saved is deleted.
