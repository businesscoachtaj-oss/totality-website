What you can use today

Everything listed here is shipped and working in the live apps. If something is still on the way, we say so.

In Totality

  • Program builder with a 676 exercise library
  • RPE based auto-regulation
  • Client check-ins, messaging and progress tracking
  • Billing and payments through Stripe, with no commission taken by Totality
  • Classes, scheduling and a gym admin portal
  • Staff hours, payroll export and Xero integration
  • Nutrition tracking for your clients, built in

In Totality Nutrition

  • Australian-first food database and barcode scanner
  • Custom calorie and macro targets

  • Calorie cycling for training days and rest days

  • Weight trend on a 7 day average, body measurements and progress photos
  • Saved meals you can log again in one tap
