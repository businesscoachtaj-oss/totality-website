What can you use today?

Everything listed here is shipped and working in the live apps. If something is still on the way, we say so.

In Totality

  • Programme builder with a 714 exercise library
  • RPE based auto-regulation
  • Client check-ins, messaging and progress tracking
  • Billing and payments through Stripe, with no commission taken by Totality
  • Classes, scheduling and an admin portal for a team
  • Staff hours, payroll export and Xero integration
  • Your own services, each with a length and a price, and booking by service
  • Intake forms you write, and treatment notes on each client
  • Itemised invoices you can email and mark paid
  • Funding and referrals: Medicare, DVA, Workcover, NDIS, Aged Care, private health or your own, with billing codes
  • Nutrition tracking for your clients, built in

In Totality Nutrition

  • Australian-first food database and barcode scanner
  • Custom calorie and macro targets
  • Adaptive targets: go over today and tomorrow's target adjusts
  • Calorie cycling for training days and rest days
  • Weekly meal planning with a grocery list you can share
  • Weight trend on a 7 day average, body measurements and progress photos
  • Saved meals you can log again in one tap

In Tali HQ

  • A morning brief at the time you pick
  • Replies, follow-ups and invoices drafted for your yes
  • Clients, leads and Stripe invoices in one place
  • Food and training logged by telling her
  • A private journal, goals and a Friday check-in
  • Her brain: see what she remembers and delete any of it
  • Web search with sources
