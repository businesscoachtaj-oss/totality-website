Programme Builder

Build weekly training plans with sessions, exercises, sets, reps and tempo. Assign to any client in one tap. Track resistance bands as their own load type, and mark exercises unilateral or bilateral: the interface adjusts sets and reps per side automatically.

🧠

Auto-Regulation Engine

RPE-based auto-regulation flags underperforming clients automatically so coaches can adjust before problems compound. No other platform offers this natively.

🍎

Nutrition Tracking

Clients log food with barcode scanning. Coaches set macro targets and review trends with a built-in calculator. Included on all plans.

Weekly Check-Ins

Is Totality right for you?

We'd rather tell you now than have you find out after you switch.

Totality is purpose-built for coaching, not facility management. That's a deliberate trade-off, not an oversight. Compared to Mindbody or Trainerize, here's where that actually lands.

Totality is a strong fit if you...

  • Run a solo PT business or a small-to-mid gym and want programming, nutrition, billing and admin in one login

  • A large marketplace of 100+ third-party integrations

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

What is Tali AI and what does it cost? +

Tali is the AI inside Totality. She drafts check-in replies, programmes, win-back messages and lead follow-ups for you to review. Every plan includes monthly AI credits (100 to 400 on Solo Coach, 1,000 on Studio, unlimited with fair use on Enterprise). Need more? The Tali AI add-on is A$9.99/mo for Solo Coach (+1,000 credits) or A$19.99/mo for Studio (+3,000 credits). Solo Coach can also bundle the POS add-on and AI for A$16.98/mo.

Does Tali message my clients on her own? +

No. Tali only drafts. You read it, edit it and send it yourself.

When can I use Tali AI on my phone? +

Tali AI is live on the Totality web app now. The iOS and Android apps get it from 5 November.

Is the client app really free? +

Yes. Clients never pay for Totality. They download the app, accept your invite and they're in. They can follow their programme, log sessions, track food, submit check-ins and message you, all for free. You pay the platform subscription; your clients pay nothing.

What's the difference between Totality and Totality Nutrition? +

Totality is the full coaching platform. It's for gyms and personal trainers who need programme building, client management, billing, check-ins and nutrition tracking in one place. Totality Nutrition is a standalone food tracking app for individuals who want to track their own nutrition without a coach. Both share the same food database, and clients on Totality can use the nutrition features built into their client app for free.

Can I bring my existing clients and data across? +

Yes. Totality supports guided migration from PTminder, Trainerize and Mindbody, and a CSV import wizard for any other platform: map your own columns and bring your client list, payment history and memberships with you. No starting from scratch.

How does billing work for gym members? +

Gym member billing runs through your own Stripe account, money goes directly from your member to your bank account. Totality charges zero commission on any member payments. A small processing fee of 3.5% applies to in-app payments to cover the cost of moving money through the payment system, clearly itemised on every payout. Payments are included from day one on Studio and Enterprise. Solo coaches add them with the POS add-on for A$9.99/mo.

What is the auto-regulation engine? +

Auto-regulation is a training methodology where session intensity adjusts based on how a client is actually performing, not just what was planned. When a client logs a session with their RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion), Totality analyses their performance and flags underperformance to the trainer automatically. It's built as clear, coach-reviewable logic, no AI, no add-ons, and included on all plans.

Is there a free trial? +

Yes. Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial, no credit card required to get started. On top of that, new accounts get 50% off their first 2 months automatically, no code needed, for both gyms and solo coaches. Totality Nutrition also has a 7-day free trial, and Totality members get 50% off Nutrition. You also get 20% off for each successful referral, stacking up to 100% while your subscription is active.

What happens to my data if I cancel? +

Your data is always yours. Export your client list, session history, payment records and programme templates at any time: before, during or after your subscription. We don't hold your data hostage. Cancel anytime from your settings screen, no exit fees, no phone call required.

Do I need separate apps for training and nutrition? +

No. Training, nutrition, check-ins, messaging and progress tracking are all in one app for your clients. As a trainer, you set macro targets, review food logs and see nutrition trends alongside session data, in the same place. One platform, one login for everyone.

I run a large gym with multiple locations. Can Totality handle that? +

Enterprise covers unlimited trainers and up to 5,000 active clients. For more than 5,000 clients, multiple locations or white label: contact us for a custom quote.
