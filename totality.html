What does Totality do?

One platform, set up around how you work. At sign up you say whether you are solo or a studio owner, and whether you do fitness or allied health. The app lays itself out to match, and you can switch any of it on or off later in Settings.

Solo

Just you and your clients. A Today card with one-tap Start, bookings and recurring bookings, programmes with auto-regulation, check-ins, live chat, templates, broadcasts and session packs.

Totality for personal trainers

Studio owners

A business with a team: a studio, a gym or a clinic. A Needs attention dashboard, front desk check-in, classes with waitlists, member billing, failed payment recovery, staff rosters and payroll with Xero export.

Totality for studios and gyms

Allied health and clinics

Your own services with a length and a price, booking by service, intake forms you write, treatment notes on each client, and funding and referrals for Medicare, DVA, Workcover, NDIS, Aged Care and private health.

Totality for allied health

For clients

Today's workout on Home, workout history with PBs, progress photos and measurements, food and water tracking, class booking and a payments hub. Free for your clients.
