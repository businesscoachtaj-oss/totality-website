Everything in Totality

Tools for working with clients, tools for growing the business, tools for allied health, tools for running a team, and the app your clients use. Items marked Studio plans come with Studio, Club and Enterprise. Everything else is on every plan.

Working with clients Growing the business Allied health Running a team For clients For everyone What your plan includes

Working with clients

Plan the work, run the session, keep in touch. On every plan.

New

Today card with one-tap Start

Today's booked sessions and classes in one card. Tap Start and the client's session for today opens, ready to log.

Programme builder

Build programmes from the exercise library and assign them to clients. Tali can draft one from a short brief for you to edit.

Auto-regulation engine

Totality reads each client's logged sessions and flags when to add load, reduce it or deload. You approve or dismiss each flag.

Session logging

Log sets, reps and weights as you coach. Personal bests are picked up for the client's history.

Client check-ins

Clients send check-ins and you reply in one place. Tali can draft a reply you edit and send.

New

Live chat and templates

Chat with clients in real time. Save message templates for the things you say every week.

New

Broadcast to all clients

Send one message to every client at once, like a holiday timetable or a new programme block.

New

Recurring bookings

Book a client into the same time each week in one go. Appointments and scheduling are on every plan.

New

Search, sort and archive clients

Find anyone fast. Archive clients who have finished up so your list stays current.

NewNeeds payments

PT packages

Sell session packs. When a client buys one, credits are added to their account. Payments come with the POS add-on on Solo plans and are included on gym plans.

New

Reports

See your revenue and sessions over time.

New

Tali credits shown up front

Tali buttons show how many credits they use before you tap, and settings show how many you have left this month. About Tali AI.

Growing and running the business

Follow ups that line themselves up, a website from your brand, and the numbers ready for your accountant.

New

On autopilot

Rules that work out who is due a message and line it up for your yes: welcome someone new, check in when a client goes quiet, a check-in reminder, a missed session, a package running low, ask for a review and happy birthday. Studios and gyms also get new lead follow ups and failed payments. Change the number of days on each rule, and ask Tali to write the message for 1 credit. Nobody gets the same nudge twice in 30 days.

New

Send it your way

Each autopilot message goes out as a message in the app, or you can tap Text from my phone to open it in your own Messages app. A lead has no app yet, so their follow up goes by text or email from you, and sending it moves them from New to Contacted.

NewBranding add-on

Your own website

A simple website built from your brand: your logo, your colour, a line about you and an About. A gym page also shows this week's classes and your memberships. The enquiry form creates a lead for you to follow up. Ask Tali to write the words for 1 credit, then edit and save. Included on Club and Enterprise.

NewBranding add-on

Your own domain

Ask for your website to sit on your own domain from the Website screen. It is part of the Branding add-on at no extra charge, and we connect it for you.

NewNeeds payments

Bookkeeping pack

A quarter in one place for your accountant: payments by type, invoices paid and owed, refunds, expenses, payroll paid and the fees taken. Share it as one CSV or email it to your accountant from the app. With the POS add-on on Solo plans, included on Studio plans.

NewNeeds payments

Disputed payments

If someone asks their bank to reverse a card payment, you get a notification and an email, and it shows under Disputed payments with the amount, the reason and the bank's deadline. Send us what shows the payment was genuine and we answer the bank for you.

Studio plansNew

Credit checks

See sessions marked complete with no credit used, PT credits added by hand with who added each one, and credits put back after the session went ahead. Payroll flags the same sessions on each trainer's card.

Studio plansNew

Find a screen and Everything

Search for any screen from the owner home, or open Everything to see every owner screen in five groups: People, Money, Training, Growing and Setting up.

New

Home screens that fold away

Sections on the owner, trainer and member home screens fold away and stay how you left them. The owner home shows the top three things needing attention first.

New

Client page in four tabs

A client's page is now sorted under Overview, Training, Food and Money, with nothing removed.

New

Add-ons in one place

An Add-ons screen shows what Branding and website, Tali AI and POS each include, for gyms and solo trainers.

Coming soon

Front desk and business texts

Tali answering the calls you miss on Club and Enterprise, and texts sent for you from a business number. Neither is available yet.

Allied health and clinic tools

For exercise physiologists, physios, massage therapists, dietitians and anyone who sees clients by appointment. On every plan. Switch on the ones you use in Settings.

New

Your own services

Appointment types with a length and a price. On Studio plans, set a different price per practitioner.

New

Book by service

Pick the service when you book and the length is set for you. The service is saved on the appointment.

New

Intake forms you write

Short answer, long answer, yes or no, or pick one. Attach a form to a service and fill it in with the client.

New

Treatment notes

What the client told you, what you found, your assessment and the plan. Private to the treating team and never shown to the client.

New

Invoices

Itemised invoices for a client or whoever pays for them, with the practitioner, provider number and billing code on each line. Email it from the app, mark it paid, and see what is owed in one list. For trainers and coaches too.

New

Take payment after booking

Book a priced service and choose to charge the saved card, send a payment link or write an invoice, with the amount filled in.

Funding and referrals

Medicare, DVA, Workcover, NDIS, Aged Care and private health, or a funding type you name yourself. Referrer, case manager, plan dates and the referral document.

Provider numbers and billing codes

Provider numbers by fund, and your own library of billing codes with a description and a rate.

New

Clinic wording

Choose allied health and the app says practitioner, appointment and clinic in the menus, dashboard, schedule and tour.

Group announcements

Leads CRM

Track enquiries from first contact to member. Tali can draft a follow-up text or email.

Studio plans

Compliance hub

Store staff compliance documents, see what expires in the next 60 days, and keep incident reports.

Studio plans

Import wizard

Bring clients across from a CSV export out of Trainerize, Mindbody or PTminder.

Studio plansNew

More than one location

Run every site from one login. Step into a location to work in it, and see revenue, members and bookings for each one with a total. Copy products, programme templates, packs, class types and services from one location to the others. Extra locations are A$149.99 a month each.

Studio plansNew

Memberships that work at every location

One switch for chains. Members of one location can book classes at the others, and a class their membership covers at home is covered everywhere.

Studio plans

Unlimited trainers

Add your whole team. Each trainer gets the full set of trainer tools, and can see the gym's member list.

Studio plans

Marketplace

Sell programmes and products to members in the app. Solo trainers can add this with the POS add-on.
