Everything in Totality

Tools for trainers, tools for gyms, and the app your clients use. Items marked Gym plans come with Studio and Enterprise. Everything else is on every plan.

For trainers For gyms For clients For everyone What your plan includes

For trainers

Plan the work, run the session, keep in touch. On every plan.

New

Today card with one-tap Start

Today's booked sessions and classes in one card. Tap Start and the client's session for today opens, ready to log.

Programme builder

Build programmes from the exercise library and assign them to clients. Tali can draft one from a short brief for you to edit.

Auto-regulation engine

Totality reads each client's logged sessions and flags when to add load, reduce it or deload. You approve or dismiss each flag.

Session logging

Log sets, reps and weights as you coach. Personal bests are picked up for the client's history.

Client check-ins

Clients send check-ins and you reply in one place. Tali can draft a reply you edit and send.

New

Live chat and templates

Chat with clients in real time. Save message templates for the things you say every week.

New

Broadcast to all clients

Send one message to every client at once, like a holiday timetable or a new programme block.

New

Recurring bookings

Book a client into the same time each week in one go. Appointments and scheduling are on every plan.

New

Search, sort and archive clients

Find anyone fast. Archive clients who have finished up so your list stays current.

NewNeeds payments

PT packages

Sell session packs. When a client buys one, credits are added to their account. Payments come with the POS add-on on Solo plans and are included on gym plans.

New

Reports

See your revenue and sessions over time.

New

Tali credits shown up front

Tali buttons show how many credits they use before you tap, and settings show how many you have left this month. About Tali AI.

For gyms and studios

Run the front desk, the floor and the books. These come with the Studio and Enterprise plans.

Gym plansNew

Needs attention dashboard

Failed payments, memberships expiring in the next 14 days, check-ins waiting for a reply, leads to follow up and classes that are full or nearly empty, on one screen.

Gym plansNew

Front desk check-in

Check members in as they arrive.

Gym plansNew

Classes, rosters and waitlists

Run classes with rosters, mark attendance and no-shows, and fill spots from the waitlist. Cancel a class and members are refunded and told.

Gym plans

Member billing and POS

Memberships, one-off charges and a Charge screen that shows the real result of each payment, through your own Stripe account.

Gym plansNew

Membership freezes

Freeze a membership and Stripe billing pauses with it.

Gym plansNew

Failed payments

See every failed payment, retry it, or send the member a link to update their card.

Gym plansNew

Staff rosters and payroll

Roster your staff and run a payroll summary, with export to Xero.

Gym plansNew

Group announcements

Send an announcement to members by push, email and in-app at once.

Gym plansNew

Revenue reports

Revenue by product and by trainer, with CSV export for your accountant.

Gym plans

Leads CRM

Track enquiries from first contact to member. Tali can draft a follow-up text or email.

Gym plans

Compliance hub

Store staff compliance documents, see what expires in the next 60 days, and keep incident reports.

Gym plans

Import wizard

Bring clients across from a CSV export out of Trainerize, Mindbody or PTminder.

Gym plans

Unlimited trainers

Add your whole team. Each trainer gets the full set of trainer tools, and can see the gym's member list.

Gym plans

Marketplace

Sell programmes and products to members in the app. Solo trainers can add this with the POS add-on.

For clients

The app your clients and members use. Free for them, included with your plan.

New

Today on the home screen

Today's workout, a check-in when one is due, the next session and the latest update from their coach.

New

Workout history with PBs

Every session they have done, with personal bests marked.

New

Check-in history

Past check-ins with trends over time.

New

Progress tabs

Photos, measurements, weight and strength, each on its own tab.

New

Nutrition targets and breakdown

A targets calculator, food logging, and a full nutrient breakdown with vitamins and minerals measured against Australian recommended intakes.

New

Water tracking

Log water through the day against a daily target.

New

Payments hub

Purchases, receipts, PT credits and packages in one place. Members can cancel their own membership.

New

Class booking and waitlists

Book classes and join the waitlist when one is full.

New

Chat with their coach

If a message fails to send, it can be retried with one tap.

Apple Health

Clients on iPhone can connect Apple Health.

Notification settings

Choose which notifications you get, and set quiet hours so nothing buzzes overnight.
