Totality Training for teams

Cover Totality Training for your clients, members or staff

Buy seats on one bill and hand them out with a team code. Each person gets Totality Training on their own account and pays nothing. Seats are A$6.99 a month each, with a 3 seat minimum.

Start a team See seat prices

Log in or create a free account first, then open Settings and tap Teams. Totality Training runs in the browser now. The iPhone and Android apps were sent to Apple and Google for review on 21 November 2026.

New teams: 50% off your first 2 months on a monthly team. Applied at checkout, no code needed.

Who a team is for

Any business that wants its people training on a proper programme in the same app.

Online communities

Give your members a workout tracker with 420 programmes to pick from, on an account they keep.

Schools and universities

Cover students for a term, with a demo for every exercise in the library.

Gyms and studios

Hand out seats for a challenge, or add them to a membership tier.

Sports clubs

Cover a squad for the season and take seats off when it ends.

Workplaces

Offer seats as a staff health benefit, paid on one invoice.

Just you?

The personal plans start free. See Totality Training

Seat prices

In Australian dollars, per person. Mix the two seat types however you like. A team starts at 3 seats, which is A$20.97 a month on Training seats.

Training seat

A$6.99 a month, per seat

or A$71.30 a year, per seat

  • All 420 Totality programmes and unlimited routines
  • Weights that go up when you earn it
  • Sets that adjust to how each muscle feels
  • Strength charts, personal bests and body stats

The same plan people buy for themselves at A$8.99 a month.

Training + AI seat

A$8.99 a month, per seat

or A$91.70 a year, per seat

  • Everything in the Training seat
  • Tali builds programmes around your week and kit
  • Tali's take after each workout
  • A weekly write-up from Tali, and 100 credits a month

The same plan people buy for themselves at A$11.99 a month.

New teams: 50% off your first 2 months on a monthly team. Yearly teams already get 15% off, so the offer is for monthly teams only.

How a team works

  1. Set up your team

    Name it, pick how many of each seat you need, and choose monthly or yearly.

  2. Pay by card

    Payment is taken by Stripe. Invoices can carry your business name, address and ABN.

  3. Share your team code

    Send the code yourself, or type in email addresses and we send the invite for you.

  4. Your people join

    They sign up or log in, open Settings, tap Teams and enter the code. Their seat is on straight away.

Their training stays private

People log honestly when they know nobody at work or at the gym is reading it.

What you see

  • Who has joined your team
  • Which seat each person has
  • Invites that are still waiting

What you never see

Workouts, weights lifted, body weight, photos and progress stay private to each person. There is no report or export of anyone's log for the team owner.

Common questions

How much does a team cost?

A Training seat is A$6.99 a month or A$71.30 a year. A Training + AI seat is A$8.99 a month or A$91.70 a year. Prices are per seat, in Australian dollars. A team starts at 3 seats.

Can I add or remove seats later?

Yes, from your team screen. An extra seat is charged straight away for the rest of the month or year. You can take off any seat nobody is using, and your next bill is lower. There is no refund for the time left on a seat you take off.

Can I mix seat types or move someone?

Yes. Buy any mix of Training and Training + AI seats, and move a person from one seat type to the other from your team screen.

What if someone already pays for Totality Training?

When they join your team, their own plan stops at the end of what they have paid for, so they are never charged twice.

What happens when someone leaves, or the team ends?

They keep their account and their workout history. Without a seat or a plan of their own they are on the Free plan.

How do I cancel?

Open Billing and invoices on your team screen. The team runs to the end of the period you have paid for, then ends.

Do you need more than 500 seats?

Email admintotality@gmail.com and we will set it up with you.

Start with 3 seats. Add more any time from your team screen.
