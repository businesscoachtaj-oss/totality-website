Find a gym, trainer, studio or clinic near you

Totality Discover is a free directory of gyms, personal trainers, studios and clinics. Search by suburb or use your location, see what each place offers, and send an enquiry or take up an intro offer. No account needed.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Looking for somewhere to train or get treated?

Discover works in any browser, and from inside Totality Training and Totality Nutrition.

Search near you

By name, suburb or postcode, or use your location. Narrow it to gyms, personal trainers, pilates, yoga, group fitness, physio and rehab, massage, recovery or health clinics, or show only places with an intro offer.

See what they offer

What they do, where they are, their website, and their services and prices where they have added them. A trainer working from home can show their suburb only.

Get in touch

Send an enquiry from the listing. It goes to that business only, so they can reply to you. No account and no sign in.

Take up an intro offer

When a business takes card payments, you can pay for their intro offer or a service by card on the listing, and they get in touch to book you in.

Run a gym, studio or clinic? List it free

Anyone with a free Totality account can list. You do not need a paid plan.

Free to list

Make a free account on the listing page, fill in what you do, where you are, a photo and an intro offer, and switch it on. Nobody sees it until you do.

Enquiries to your inbox

Each enquiry is emailed to you with the person's name, email or phone and message. Reply straight from your email. They also sit on your listing page.

Already on Totality?

Your listing is in Settings, under Your Discover listing. It starts with your business name, photo and kind of business filled in.

Card payments for a gym or studio

On a Totality team plan, connect your gym's Stripe account and payments for your intro offer and services go straight to the gym. If you already take payments in Totality, it uses the same account.

Card payments for everyone else

For solo trainers, practitioners and businesses not on a team plan, card payments come with the POS add-on, a month, the same as for trainers on Totality. Then connect your own Stripe account and the money goes straight to you. Listing stays free.

Fees and refunds

Totality's fee is 1.25% plus the card fee (1.65% + 30c) on each payment, taken from your payout. You can give a full refund from your listing.

What it does not do yet

So you know before you list.

Picking a time

People pay or send an enquiry from the listing, and you book them in. Choosing a time slot from Discover is not there yet.

Reviews

Listings do not show ratings or reviews yet. Your website link is on your listing.

Outside Australia

Discover is for Australian businesses, with prices in Australian dollars.

Common questions

Who can list on Discover?

Gyms, personal trainers, studios, clinics and anyone in fitness or health who sees people in Australia. You need a free Totality account, which you can make on the listing page.

Does it cost anything to list?

No. Listing is free. Taking card payments on your listing needs a Totality team plan or the POS add-on, and each payment has Totality's 1.25% fee plus the card fee.

Where does my enquiry go?

Only to the business you sent it to, by email, so they can reply to you. Totality does not use it for anything else.

Can I hide my street address?

Yes. A listing shows your suburb only unless you choose to show your street, which suits trainers who work from home or travel to clients.

How do I take my listing down?

Switch Show my listing on Discover off on your listing page. It disappears straight away and nothing is deleted.
