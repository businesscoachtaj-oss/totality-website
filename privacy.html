Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026  ·  Totality 360 Pty Ltd (ABN 58 698 902 975)

iIntroduction

Totality is a fitness coaching platform operated by Totality 360 Pty Ltd (ABN 58 698 902 975) ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Totality platform, the Totality Nutrition app and the Totality Training app, including the web apps at totalityapp.com.au, totalitynutrition.com.au and totality-training.vercel.app and any associated mobile applications (collectively, the "Platform").

By creating an account or using the Platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.

Australian Privacy Law: Totality 360 Pty Ltd (ABN 58 698 902 975) is based in Australia and complies with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

Account information

  • Name and email address (required to create an account)
  • Password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text)
  • Profile photo (optional, uploaded by you)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Business name (for gym administrators)

Health and fitness data

  • Body measurements, weight, and fitness goals entered by you or your trainer
  • Session logs, exercise records, and programme data
  • Nutrition logs and daily food entries
  • Weekly check-in responses and progress photos (if provided)
  • PAR-Q health screening responses (gym intake forms)

Wearable and health app data (optional)

  • If you choose to connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), we read daily summaries of your steps, active calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, sleep, and exercise sessions
  • This is entirely optional and only happens after you approve each permission on your device. See Section 4 for full details

Billing information

  • Subscription tier and billing status
  • Payment is processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details
  • Stripe may retain a customer reference ID that we use to manage your subscription
  • For marketplace purchases: buyer email address, product purchased, and transaction status
  • For marketplace sellers (trainers and gyms): Stripe Connect account ID and payout status

Marketplace data

  • If you purchase a product through the Totality marketplace, we record your email address, the product purchased, and the transaction status
  • If a purchase includes a coaching relationship (e.g. a PT package), your details may be shared with the trainer or gym whose product you purchased, in order to deliver the service
  • Totality 360 Pty Ltd (ABN 58 698 902 975) charges a platform fee of 1.25% on payments made through Totality, including memberships, PT packages, paid class bookings, one-off charges, marketplace and point of sale (POS) payments. The rest of each payment goes to the seller via Stripe Connect

Usage data

  • Actions you take within the Platform (e.g. sessions logged, programmes created)
  • Device type and browser information for technical support purposes

2How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the Platform and its features
  • Allow trainers to manage client programmes, sessions, and progress
  • Allow gym administrators to manage their business, trainers, and members
  • Process subscription payments via Stripe
  • Process marketplace purchases and route payments to sellers via Stripe Connect
  • Send transactional emails (account confirmation, invitations, billing notices) via Resend
  • Send push notifications to your device via OneSignal (session reminders, check-in alerts, messages)
  • Respond to support requests and resolve technical issues
  • Show you your own activity, sleep, and heart rate summaries if you choose to connect Apple Health or Health Connect
  • Improve the Platform based on aggregated, anonymised usage patterns (this never includes data from Apple Health or Health Connect)
  • Meet our legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

3Sensitive Health Information

Some information you enter into Totality, such as body measurements, fitness goals, health history, and PAR-Q screening responses, may be considered sensitive health information under Australian privacy law.

This data is entered voluntarily by you or, where applicable, by a trainer or gym administrator acting on your behalf with your consent. It is used solely to deliver the coaching and fitness tracking features of the Platform and is never shared with third parties except as described in Sections 4 and 5.

If you are a client whose data is managed by a trainer or gym, that trainer or gym administrator can view your fitness and session data within the Platform. You should contact your gym or trainer directly if you have questions about how they handle your data.

4Apple Health and Health Connect Data

Totality and Totality Nutrition can read activity and health data from Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android. This is optional. Nothing is read unless you choose to connect it and approve each permission on your device.

What we read

  • Steps
  • Active calories burned
  • Heart rate (we keep the daily average)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Sleep (total time asleep)
  • Exercise sessions (the number completed that day)

We only read this data. We never write to, change, or delete anything in Apple Health or Health Connect.

How it is stored and who can see it

We save one summary per day (the totals and averages above), not minute-by-minute readings. It is stored in your account on our database provider, Supabase, with the same access controls as the rest of your data (see Section 6). Only you can see it in the app. It is not shown to your trainer or gym administrator.

How we use it

We use it only to show you your steps, activity, heart rate, sleep, and workouts on your Dashboard and Wearables screen. These figures are general fitness information. They are not medical advice, and Totality is not a medical service.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell this data
  • We do not use it for advertising, marketing, or profiling, and we do not share it with advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers
  • We do not use it for credit, insurance, or employment decisions
  • We do not use it to develop or improve anything other than the features you can see in the app, and it is excluded from our aggregated usage analysis
  • We do not share it with anyone except Supabase (to store it for you), or where you ask us to, or where the law requires it
  • Our staff do not read it unless you ask us to in a support request, it is needed to investigate abuse or a security issue, or the law requires it

Totality's use and transfer of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect requirements in the Google Play Developer Program Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information received from Apple Health is used only to provide the health and fitness features described above, in line with Apple's HealthKit requirements.

Your choices

  • You can turn off access at any time in your phone's Apple Health settings or in Health Connect's app permissions
  • Disconnecting in the app stops further syncing
  • To delete the daily summaries we have already saved without closing your account, email us at admintotality@gmail.com and we will remove them within 30 days
  • Closing your account deletes them (see Section 7)

4aTali in Claude (Claude Connectors)

You can connect your Totality or Totality Nutrition account to Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, using our connectors Tali by Totality and Tali by Totality Nutrition. This is optional. Nothing is shared until you connect in Claude, sign in to Totality and select Allow.

What Claude can access

Only what your own Totality login can already see in the app, and only when you ask something that needs it.

  • Tali by Totality (trainers and gym owners): your clients' names and emails, when they last used the app, their programmes, completed sessions and logged sets, PT appointments and classes, weekly check-ins (including anything a client wrote in them, which can include health information), weigh-ins, and membership payments taken through Totality
  • Tali by Totality Nutrition (members): your own food log, targets, water, weigh-ins, meal planner, grocery list, saved meals and saved recipes

What it can change

  • Tali by Totality can only read. It cannot change your account, message clients or take payments
  • Tali by Totality Nutrition can add entries to your food log, water, weigh-ins, meal planner and recipes, and only after you agree in the chat. It never deletes anything and never changes your targets, plan or payments

How it is shared

When Tali looks something up for you, the result is sent to Anthropic so Claude can answer in your chat. Anthropic handles your Claude conversations under its own privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and your Claude settings. We do not receive or keep your Claude conversations, and we do not use anything from them for advertising or to train AI models.

If you are a trainer or gym owner, asking Tali about a client sends that client's details, which you can already see in Totality, to Anthropic as part of your chat. Only ask about what you need, and only connect if that fits what your clients have agreed to.

What we store and for how long

  • The connection between your Totality account and Claude, kept by our sign-in provider Supabase until you disconnect or it expires
  • Standard server logs of each request (the time, which Tali feature was used, and whether it worked), kept by Supabase for up to 7 days for security and troubleshooting. We do not keep Tali's answers
  • Anything Tali by Totality Nutrition adds to your log with your agreement, kept like the rest of your account (see Section 7)

Your choices

  • Disconnect at any time in Claude: Settings, then Connectors
  • Questions or a request to delete anything a connector added: email admintotality@gmail.com
  • More detail on how the connectors work: totality360.com.au/claude.html

4bFree Skool Tools (Zero to Operator)

Our free tools for Skool owners at totality360.com.au/skool-tools run in your browser. Anything you paste or upload into them stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

To download the Promo Tracker Chrome extension we ask for your email. We keep only your email, which tool you came from and the date, stored with our provider Supabase. If you tick the box to get updates, we also keep the wording you agreed to and may email you about new free tools from Zero to Operator. Every email has an unsubscribe link. If you don't tick the box, we won't send you marketing emails.

To have your email removed, email admintotality@gmail.com.

4cConnecting Your Google Account (Gmail and Google Calendar)

In Tali HQ you can connect your Google account so replies Tali drafts go out from your own Gmail and so she can work with your Google Calendar. From 10 November 2026 Tali no longer asks to read your Gmail. To have her sort your inbox you switch on Gmail's own forwarding to your Tali address, and she only sees the emails Gmail forwards after that. In Totality, trainers can connect Google Calendar only. Both are optional. Nothing is read until you choose Connect Google, sign in with Google and select Allow on Google's own screen.

What we ask Google for, and why

  • Read your Gmail (Tali HQ only, accounts connected before 10 November 2026): Tali no longer asks for this. If you connected before that date, she keeps reading new mail the old way until your first forwarded email arrives or until the end of 24 November 2026, whichever comes first. After that she does not read your Gmail through Google.
  • Send email from your Gmail (Tali HQ only): so a reply Tali drafted can go out from your own address when you tap Send. Tali never sends anything on her own.
  • See your Google Calendar: so Tali can tell you what is on today, find free gaps, and in Totality grey out times you are already busy in the booking screen.
  • Add and change calendar events: so a booking you approve lands in your Google Calendar, and in Totality your PT sessions and classes are mirrored to your calendar.
  • Your email address: to show you which Google account is connected.

What we store

  • A sign-in token for your Google account, so you do not have to sign in every time. It is kept in our database at Supabase, is never shown in the app, and is deleted the moment you disconnect.
  • In Tali HQ: the emails Tali has sorted for you (sender, subject, the text, Tali's summary and category), so you can see them in the app. Only mail that arrives after you set up forwarding (or, for older accounts, after you connected) is kept. Each email is deleted 90 days after it arrived. Mail read the old way through Google is deleted straight away when you disconnect.
  • In Totality: the link between a Totality appointment and its Google Calendar event, so changes stay in step.
  • We do not store your calendar events in Tali HQ. Tali reads today's events live when you open the app and when she writes your morning brief.

How your Google data is used

To sort an email, draft a reply or write your brief, Tali sends the email text or today's calendar to Anthropic's Claude model and gets the summary or draft back. Anthropic processes this under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models, and does not keep it beyond the request. Nobody at Totality 360 reads your mail. Your Google data is used only to provide the Tali features described above. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never shared with any other third party, and never used to train AI models.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect at any time: in Tali HQ under Me, then Connections; in Totality under your Google Calendar card. This deletes the sign-in token and the synced emails straight away.
  • You can also remove access from Google's side at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Questions or a deletion request: email admintotality@gmail.com.

4dThe Tali HQ App

Tali HQ is a separate app from Totality, where you work with Tali, an AI that remembers your life and your business. This section covers what Tali HQ keeps and why. The Google section above also applies if you connect Google.

What Tali HQ stores

  • Your account: email, first name, business name and type, what you tell Tali about yourself, your time zone and settings.
  • Your chats with Tali, including photos and PDFs you send her, and your journal entries and notes. Entries you mark "Keep this from Tali" are never read by Tali.
  • What Tali remembers about you and your business (her memory for your account). You can see every memory under Me, What Tali knows, and delete any of them.
  • Your clients, leads, reminders, invoices, goals, decisions, end of day check-ins and weekly reviews.
  • Food, weight and training you tell Tali about, if you choose to. Food figures are Tali's estimates.
  • Connections you choose to make: Google, Microsoft (Outlook) and Stripe. We keep a sign-in token for each, never your password.
  • On Pro, emails sent to your own Tali address, so Tali can summarise them and draft a reply.
  • Push notification tokens for your devices, if you turn notifications on.

Who helps us run it

  • Supabase stores the data in Sydney, Australia.
  • Anthropic's Claude model writes Tali's replies, briefs and summaries. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Stripe handles payments for Tali HQ plans on the web and Android, and for invoices you send through your own connected Stripe account. In the iPhone app, plans are paid through Apple, and Apple tells us which plan you have and when it renews or ends.
  • Resend delivers emails to and from Tali's addresses. Vercel hosts the web app. Expo and your browser deliver push notifications.

Tali and AI companies

Tali is an AI. To think, write and remember for you, Tali HQ sends some of your information to the AI companies that run her thinking. Nothing goes to them until you say yes on the "Tali and your data" screen, which shows the first time you sign in. You can change your answer any time in Tali HQ under Me, Tali and your data. Without a yes you can still use your lists, notes, journal and the rest of the app, but Tali can't chat, write your brief, sort email or study at night.

What can be sent, only when it is needed for something you asked for or switched on:

  • What you type or say to Tali, and photos or files you send her.
  • Your notes, journal, goals, to do list and what Tali remembers about you. Entries you mark "Keep this from Tali" are never sent.
  • Emails you forward to Tali, or that come in from an inbox you connect.
  • Your first name, business details and the people and money you track in the app.

Your password and card details are never sent. The AI companies are Anthropic (privacy policy), OpenAI (privacy policy), Google (privacy policy) and Voyage AI (privacy policy). They process the information to give Tali her answer, under their own terms.

Lessons shared with every Tali

Tali keeps a shared brain of general business lessons that every user's Tali learns from. Lessons Tali learns while working with you (from night school, and lessons, mistakes and rules that have proven themselves in your chats) may be added to it. Before anything is shared, it is rewritten as a general lesson with no names, clients, staff, suppliers, places, prices, contact details or anything else that identifies you or anyone you deal with, and it is left out if it can't be made general. Your facts, people, events, photos, journal and chats are never shared.

This is on by default. You can switch it off any time in Tali HQ under Me, Settings, Help every Tali learn, and mark any single memory Private in Tali's brain. Lessons already shared contain nothing that identifies you, so they stay in the shared brain if you switch it off or delete your account.

Tali never sends, spends or changes anything without your yes, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in Tali HQ under Me, Settings, Delete account. This removes your profile, chats, journal, Tali's memories of you, clients, invoices and everything else listed above. Questions or requests: admintotality@gmail.com.

4eThe Totality Training App

Totality Training is our workout app for people who train on their own. It uses the same account as Totality and Totality Nutrition, and everything else in this policy applies to it. This section covers what it adds.

What Totality Training stores

  • Your answers when you set up: your goal, how long you have been lifting, the days you train and the equipment you have.
  • Your programmes and routines, and every workout you log: exercises, sets, reps and weights.
  • What you tell the app after a workout about how it felt, which it uses to adjust next week's sets.
  • Body stats you choose to enter, such as your weight.
  • Your questions to Tali and her answers, on the same terms as Tali in our other apps.
  • Your plan and trial dates. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.

With Totality Nutrition

If you also use Totality Nutrition on the same account, each app can show a little of the other: today's food totals in Totality Training, and the day's workout in Totality Nutrition. You can switch each one off in Totality Training. Nothing is copied between the apps and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Teams

If a workplace, club or coach covers your plan with a team seat, the person who runs the team can see your name, your email address and which seat you have. They cannot see your workouts, your weights, your body stats or anything else you log.

Emails

We send a welcome email, a reminder before your free trial ends and one email when it has ended, plus emails about your plan or price when something changes.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in Totality Training under Settings, Delete my account. See Delete your account for what is removed.

5When We Share Your Information

We share personal information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Stripe: payment processing for subscriptions and marketplace purchases. Stripe handles all card data under their own privacy policy (stripe.com/au/privacy). We never see or store your card details. For marketplace transactions, Stripe Connect routes payments directly to the seller's (trainer or gym's) connected Stripe account. Totality 360 Pty Ltd (ABN 58 698 902 975) retains a 1.25% platform fee from payments made through Totality (memberships, PT packages, paid class bookings, one-off charges, marketplace and POS transaction amounts); the remainder is paid to the seller.
  • Supabase: our database and authentication provider. Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in the United States. Supabase complies with applicable data protection standards.
  • Resend: transactional email delivery (account invitations, billing emails, platform announcements). Only your email address and name are shared for this purpose.
  • OneSignal: push notification delivery to mobile and web devices. Your device token (a technical identifier for your device) is shared with OneSignal to enable push notifications. OneSignal's privacy policy is available at onesignal.com/privacy_policy. You can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings.
  • Anthropic: only if you connect Totality to Claude, and only what Tali looks up for you in your Claude chat. See Section 4a.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Business transfer: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before any such transfer.

We do not share your data with any other third parties.

6Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure, which is hosted in the United States. By using the Platform, you consent to your data being transferred to and stored in the United States.

We take the following security measures to protect your information:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Database access is controlled by row-level security policies
  • Payment card data is never transmitted to or stored on our servers
  • Access to production systems is limited to authorised personnel only

While we take reasonable precautions, no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to contact us immediately if you suspect any unauthorised access to your account.

7Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Platform's services.

If you close your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal information within 90 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or accounting purposes (e.g. billing records, which may be retained for up to 7 years under Australian tax law).

Health and fitness data (session logs, nutrition entries, programme records) is deleted along with your account unless your gym or trainer is required to retain it for compliance or legal reasons.

Daily summaries from Apple Health or Health Connect are deleted with your account. Disconnecting in the app stops further syncing but does not remove summaries already saved. You can ask us to delete them at any time (see Section 4).

8Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: request that we delete your personal information (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Portability: request your data in a portable format where technically feasible
  • Complaints: lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au if you believe we have breached the Privacy Act

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 11. We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

9Cookies and Local Storage

The Totality Platform uses browser local storage and session cookies solely for the purpose of maintaining your login session and remembering your in-app preferences (such as onboarding status).

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics tools. No third-party cookies are placed on your device through our Platform.

10Children's Privacy

The Totality Platform is intended for use by adults aged 18 and over, or by minors under the supervision of a parent or guardian and with the explicit consent of a qualified fitness professional.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify you by email or via an in-app notice before the changes take effect.

Your continued use of the Platform after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
