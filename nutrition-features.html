Totality Nutrition features

Food tracking built on Australian food data. Log what you eat, see every nutrient, keep a streak going and shop from a list that writes itself.

On Android and the web now. iPhone is coming soon.

Logging Nutrients and targets Streaks and progress Grocery list Tali AI Plans

Logging food

New

Australian food data first

Search puts foods from the Australian Food Composition Database (AFCD) first, then packaged foods from Open Food Facts. Every result shows where its numbers come from.

Barcode scanner

Scan a packaged food to find it fast.

Saved meals and recipes

Save the meals you eat often and log them in one tap. Keep your own recipes too.

Import recipes from a link

Paste a link from a recipe site and the ingredients are matched to foods for you to check before saving.

Nutrients and targets

New

Full nutrient breakdown

Vitamins and minerals for the day, measured against Australian recommended intakes, not just calories and macros.

Targets calculator

Work out calorie and macro targets from your details and goal, or set your own.

New

Hydration

Log water through the day against your target. Rebuilt in this release.

Trend insights

See how your intake moves week to week.

Streaks and progress

New

Logging streaks

Any day with a food logged counts, right up to 11:59pm. Miss one day in a week and it is forgiven, as long as you logged the days either side.

New

Milestones and reminders

A celebration when you hit a streak milestone. On your phone, a 7:30pm reminder if you have a streak going and have not logged yet that day.

New

Progress

Photos, measurements and a weight trend, each on its own tab. Rebuilt in this release.

New

Referral links

Share your link.

Grocery list

Built from your meal plan, plus anything you add, in amounts you can actually buy.

New

Shopping windows

Shop for this week, the rest of the week, the next 3 days or the next 7 days. The list covers just that window.

New

Combined ingredients

The same food across meals becomes one line, grouped by supermarket aisle, with cooked weights turned into buying weights.

New

Pantry staples

Mark what you always have at home so it stays off the list.

New

Tick off and share

Tick items off as you shop and they drop to the bottom. Ticks are saved. Share the list, grouped by aisle.

Tali AI

On the Nutrition + AI plan. Tali never makes up the numbers: calories and macros always come from the food database or your saved foods.

Describe a meal

Type what you ate. Tali splits it into foods and amounts, and you check it before it is logged.

Import any recipe

Pages the standard importer cannot read, or recipe text you paste in.

Meal plans

Built from your own saved meals, recipes and the foods you eat most, sized to your targets.

Weekly summary

A short read on how your week went, at the top of Trend Insights.
