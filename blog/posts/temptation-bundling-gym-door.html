Lock Your Favorite Show Behind the Gym Door

September 19, 2026  ·  Totality

Willpower is an unreliable way to get someone to the gym. Their own cravings are a much more reliable one, if you point them in the right direction.

Pick one genuinely addictive audiobook, podcast series, or show, and make yourself a rule: you only get to consume it while working out, nowhere else. This borrows motivation from something you already crave instead of relying on discipline alone. Showing up to train becomes the only way to scratch the itch.

What the Research Found

In a field experiment, gym members who could only listen to a tempting audiobook at the gym visited 51% more often than a control group with unrestricted access. A larger follow-up study, tracking nearly 6,800 people, found the same "temptation bundling" approach kept boosting attendance for up to 17 weeks, not just a short novelty spike.

How to Use It

The mechanism only works if the content is genuinely hard to put down and genuinely restricted. A show you're lukewarm on won't pull you through the door on a tired evening, and if you let yourself watch it on the couch too, the restriction, and the pull, disappears. Pick something you're already hooked on, and hold the line: gym-only.

Evidence

  • Milkman KL, Minson JA, Volpp KG (2014), Management Science, 60(2):283-299. Field experiment on temptation bundling and gym attendance. PMID: 25843979
  • Kirgios EL, et al. (2020), a large-scale (n=6,792) field replication finding temptation bundling's effect on gym attendance held for up to 17 weeks.

This is general behavior-change information, not individual coaching or medical advice.
