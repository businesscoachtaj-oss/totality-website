Totality Nutrition in 30 seconds

A food tracking app built in Australia, on Australian food data. For anyone who wants to know what they are eating.

What it does

Calories, macros, vitamins and minerals, barcode scanning, meal planning and a grocery list that writes itself.

What it costs

Free plan forever. Nutrition A$8.99 a month. Nutrition + AI A$11.99 a month.

What your code gives

70% off the first 2 months on a monthly plan: A$2.70 a month, or A$3.60 with AI.

Where it runs

Android and any web browser. The iPhone app is with Apple for review, so iPhone users sign up in Safari.

Things worth mentioning

  • Search puts Australian foods first, from the Australian Food Composition Database, then packaged foods from Open Food Facts. Every result shows where its numbers come from.
  • The barcode scanner works on Australian products.
  • A full vitamin and mineral breakdown, measured against Australian recommended intakes.
  • Plan your meals and the grocery list builds itself, grouped by supermarket aisle.
  • Paste a recipe link and it matches the ingredients to foods for you.
  • Logging streaks, and a forgiven day if you miss one in a week.
  • With Nutrition + AI, type what you ate and Tali splits it into foods and amounts for you to check.
  • Built by a personal trainer in Australia.

The free plan

No card and no ads. Food logging with full history, macro targets, water, weight and progress, 10 saved meals, 7 barcode scans a week and 3 Tell Tali meals a day.

Every new account gets the full Nutrition plan for its first 7 days, no card needed. If they don't pick a plan, they move to Free.

Your code takes 70% off the first 2 months when they choose a monthly paid plan. It does not apply to yearly plans.

Questions people ask

Short answers you can paste into a reply.

Is it free?

There's a free plan forever, with no card and no ads. The paid plans are A$8.99 a month, or A$11.99 with Tali AI. Every new account gets 7 days of the full plan first.

Is there an iPhone app?

It's with Apple for review. Until it's out, iPhone users sign up and use it in Safari at the link, and the code works there.

Does the code work on a yearly plan?

No. It's 70% off the first 2 months of a monthly plan. After that it's the normal monthly price, and they can cancel any time.

How is it different to other trackers?

Talk about what it does: Australian food data first, where every number comes from, vitamins and minerals against Australian intakes, and a free plan with no ads. Please don't make claims about other apps.

Does the AI make up the numbers?

No. Tali splits a meal into foods and amounts, and the calories and macros come from the food database or the person's saved foods. They check it before it's logged.

Can my workplace pay for it?

Yes. Businesses can buy team seats from A$6.99 a person a month. Point them to Totality Nutrition for teams.

Totality in 30 seconds

Client management software built in Australia for anyone who works with clients: personal trainers, coaches, studios, gyms and allied health clinics.

What it does

Programmes, sessions, check-ins, chat, bookings, billing and classes in one app. Clients use it free.

What it costs

Free for up to 3 clients. Solo plans from A$19.99 a month. Studio A$389.99 a month with an unlimited team.

What your code gives

70% off the first 2 months on any monthly plan. Solo 20 comes to A$12.00 a month for those 2 months.

Where it runs

iPhone, Android and the web.

Things worth mentioning

  • One app for programmes, sessions, check-ins, chat and payments, so you stop copying things between apps.
  • A Today card with one-tap Start on each booked session.
  • Programmes from a library of 751 exercises, with auto-regulation that flags when a client should add load, reduce it or deload.
  • Tali drafts check-in replies, win-back messages and programmes. The trainer edits and sends.
  • Clients use the app free: workouts, PBs, progress photos, food and water.
  • No commission on coaching revenue. A 3.5% platform fee on payments taken in the app, from the payout.
  • Studio plans add a front desk, classes with waitlists, member billing, rosters and payroll with Xero export.
  • Allied health tools you switch on: services, intake forms, treatment notes, and funding for Medicare, DVA, Workcover, NDIS and private health.

Plans in short

Free: up to 3 clients, every Solo tool, no Tali AI.

Solo: priced by client count. Solo 5 A$19.99, Solo 10 A$29.99, Solo 20 A$39.99, up to Solo 200 A$249.99 a month.

Studio, Club, Enterprise: for a business with a team, from A$389.99 a month.

Every paid plan starts with a 7 day free trial. Your code takes 70% off the first 2 months on a monthly plan. Yearly plans are 15% off and the code does not apply to them.

Questions people ask

Do my clients pay?

No. Clients use the Totality app free on iPhone, Android and the web.

Is there commission?

No commission on coaching revenue. A 3.5% platform fee applies to payments taken in the app. It comes out of the payout and is never added to what clients pay.

What does it cost?

Free for up to 3 clients. Solo plans by client count, from A$19.99 a month for 5 clients. Studio is A$389.99 a month with an unlimited team. Prices are in AUD on the pricing page.

Does the code work on yearly?

No. It's 70% off the first 2 months of a monthly plan, after the 7 day free trial.

Is it only for gyms and PTs?

No. It's for anyone who works with clients by session, class or appointment, including allied health clinics.

How does it compare to other software?

Point them to the compare page rather than making your own claims about other apps.

How you earn

The same for both apps, counted separately for each.

Your first 5 paying sign-ups

Your first year of the app is free. Each person who pays after signing up with your code takes 20% off your own plan. Five of them make it 100% off, so it stays free after the year, as long as they keep paying.

From your 6th, commission

You earn a share of the standard monthly price, every month each person keeps paying. Add-ons aren't included, and clients of a trainer or gym don't count.

The rules

Short, and there to keep you and us out of trouble.

Please do

  • Label it clearly every time. If you got the app free or earn from your code, say so with #ad, "gifted" or the platform's paid partnership label.
