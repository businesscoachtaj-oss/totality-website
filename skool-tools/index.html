Free for every Skool owner

Tools for running a Skool group

Four free tools for the jobs every Skool owner does: looking after members, fixing your About page, formatting lessons and keeping track of where you promote. No sign up. Nothing you paste leaves your browser.

Member dashboard

Bring in your member list and see who's new, who's gone quiet and who's warming up. Copy a personal message in one tap.

Group health check

Score your group against what Skool says it rewards and penalises in Discovery, then get a fix list and a Claude prompt to rewrite your About page.

Lesson formatter

Paste a lesson from Claude or a doc. Get Skool-ready pages with code blocks, tables and dividers fixed, split one lesson per page.

Promo tracker

Every group you promote in, its rules and when you can post next. Warns you before you repeat yourself. Comes with a Chrome extension.
