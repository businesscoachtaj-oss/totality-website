Member dashboard

See who joined this week, who has gone quiet and who is warming up, then message them with one tap. Bring your member list from Skool and it stays in your browser.

1. Bring in your members

In Skool, open your group, click Members, scroll to the bottom so everyone has loaded, then select all (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A), copy and paste it here. This way includes last active times. Got more than one page of members? Paste each page in, one after the other.

In Skool, open Members and click Export. Drop the CSV file here. The export has join dates, tiers and emails but no last active times, so the "gone quiet" list needs the paste option.
