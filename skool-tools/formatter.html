Free tool

Lesson formatter

Paste a lesson you wrote with Claude or in a doc. It comes back ready to paste into a Skool Classroom page, with the bits Skool handles badly already fixed, and long courses split into one lesson per page.

1. Paste your lesson

2. Choose the fixes

The heading becomes the lesson title. Skool would not save page titles over 50 characters when we tested, so long ones are flagged.

3. Copy into Skool

Open the Classroom page in Skool, click the pencil, click into the body and paste. Copy the title separately into the title box.
