Lose It! alternative in Australia: Totality Nutrition and Lose It! compared

Lose It! is a calorie counter built around weight loss, with a large food database. Here is how it lines up with Totality Nutrition.

Checked Nov 2026. Prices change, so check Lose It!'s site before you decide.

Lose It! and Totality Nutrition side by side
 Lose It!Totality Nutrition
Who it is forPeople who want to lose weight with a calorie budget.People in Australia who want to track food, macros and nutrients, on their own or alongside a coach.
PriceFree basic version. Premium on the Australian App Store is listed from A$7.99 to A$124.99, plus lifetime at A$79.99 or A$99.99. The listing doesn't say which prices are monthly or yearly.Free A$0 forever. Nutrition a month or a year. Nutrition + AI a month or a year.
Free versionFree calorie tracking, a weight loss plan and community support.Free forever, no card and no ads: food logging with full history, macro targets, water, weight and progress, 10 saved meals, 7 barcode scans a week and Tell Tali 3 times a day. New accounts get 7 days of the full Nutrition plan first.
Food database56+ million items and recipes.Foods from the Australian Food Composition Database (AFCD) come first, then packaged foods from Open Food Facts. Every result shows where its numbers come from.
Barcode scanningPremium only.Yes. 7 scans a week on Free, unlimited on Nutrition.
AI loggingPhoto meal logging and AI voice logging on Premium.Type what you ate and Tali splits it into foods and amounts for you to check, with numbers from the food database. 3 a day on Free and Nutrition, more with Tali AI credits on Nutrition + AI. No photo logging.
Vitamins and mineralsVitamins and minerals in Premium's advanced tracking.Vitamins and minerals for the day, measured against Australian recommended intakes. Tap any nutrient for a short note on why it matters.
Pregnancy and breastfeedingThe App Store description doesn't mention a pregnancy or breastfeeding setting.A pregnancy (by trimester) and breastfeeding setting. Targets lead with protein, calories are a minimum to reach, and there is never a deficit while pregnant or breastfeeding. Shaped with feedback from a midwife.
Targets and coachingA weight loss plan on Free. Meal planning and targets, and diet plans on Premium.A targets calculator from your details and goal, or set your own. Trend insights, and a weekly summary from Tali on Nutrition + AI.
Recipes and meal plansMeal planning on Premium.A cookbook of 200 high protein meals to browse. On the Nutrition plan: save recipes, import a recipe from a link, plan your meals by the day and get a grocery list from the plan.
Sleep soundsThe App Store description doesn't mention sleep sounds.Free on every plan: ready made scenes like rain and fire, your own mixes and a timer that fades out.
AppsiPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch on the App Store.Android and the web app now. The iPhone app is coming soon.
CurrencyAustralian dollars on the Australian App Store.Australian dollars. Made by Totality 360 in Australia.

Lose It! sources (checked Nov 2026): https://apps.apple.com/au/app/lose-it-calorie-counter/id297368629 https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lose-it-calorie-counter/id297368629?uo=4

Lose It! may suit you better if you want

  • A database of 56+ million items and recipes.
  • Photo and AI voice logging on Premium.
  • A lifetime Premium option.

Totality Nutrition may suit you better if

  • You want barcode scanning on the free version (7 scans a week, unlimited on Nutrition).
  • You want vitamins and minerals measured against Australian recommended intakes without a premium tier for advanced tracking.
  • You want Australian Food Composition Database foods first.
  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding and want targets that lead with protein and never set a deficit.
  • You want sleep sounds in the same app, free.

Try Totality Nutrition: every new account gets 7 days of the full Nutrition plan, no card. If you don't pick a plan, you move to Free.
