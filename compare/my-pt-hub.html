My PT Hub alternative in Australia: Totality and My PT Hub compared

My PT Hub is coaching software for personal trainers, priced in US dollars, with unlimited clients from its Premium plan. Here is how it lines up with Totality, using each company's own published information.

Checked Oct 2026. Prices change, so check My PT Hub's site before you decide.

My PT Hub may suit you better if you want

  • Unlimited clients on one plan for a flat price.
  • A custom branded app for a one-off fee.
  • Zapier automations.

Totality may suit you better if

  • You want to pay in Australian dollars with Australian food data built in.
  • You may grow into a gym and want classes and member billing later without changing apps.
  • You want an AI assistant, Tali, built into the app you and your clients use.
