Totality and GymMaster compared

GymMaster is gym management software from New Zealand, with Australian dollar pricing by active member count and optional door access. Here is how it lines up with Totality, using each company's own published information.

Checked Oct 2026. Prices change, so check GymMaster's site before you decide.

GymMaster may suit you better if you want

  • 24/7 door access control and key fobs.
  • Stock and inventory.
  • All features on every plan, priced by active members.

Totality may suit you better if

  • Your trainers coach clients through programmes, check-ins and session logs, and you want that in your gym software.
  • You want AI included in the price, without a separate OpenAI account.
  • You want a free trial to set it up with your own members first.
