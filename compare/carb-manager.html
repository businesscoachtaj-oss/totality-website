Carb Manager alternative in Australia: Totality Nutrition and Carb Manager compared

Carb Manager is a keto and low carb tracker with free net carb tracking. Here is how it lines up with Totality Nutrition.

Checked Nov 2026. Prices change, so check Carb Manager's site before you decide.

Carb Manager and Totality Nutrition side by side
 Carb ManagerTotality Nutrition
Who it is forPeople on keto or low carb diets who track net carbs.People in Australia who want to track food, macros and nutrients, on their own or alongside a coach.
PriceFree to use. Premium is A$12.49 a month, A$24.49 for three months, or from A$46.99 a year on the Australian App Store (several yearly prices are listed, up to A$99.99).Free A$0 forever. Nutrition a month or a year. Nutrition + AI a month or a year.
Free versionFree net carb, calorie and macro tracking, unlimited barcode scans, water, exercise and weight.Free forever, no card and no ads: food logging with full history, macro targets, water, weight and progress, 10 saved meals, 7 barcode scans a week and Tell Tali 3 times a day. Barcode scanningFree and unlimited.Yes. 7 scans a week on Free, unlimited on Nutrition.

Carb Manager may suit you better if you want

  • Built around keto and net carbs.
  • Unlimited free barcode scanning.
  • 5,000+ keto recipes on Premium.

Totality Nutrition may suit you better if

  • You want vitamins and minerals measured against Australian recommended intakes on your plan, not a premium add-on.
  • You want Australian Food Composition Database foods first.
  • You want a weekly meal plan with a grocery list grouped by supermarket aisle.
