TrueCoach alternative in Australia: Totality and TrueCoach compared

TrueCoach is programming and coaching software for personal trainers, priced in US dollars by active clients. Here is how it lines up with Totality, using each company's own published information.

Checked Oct 2026. Prices change, so check TrueCoach's site before you decide.

TrueCoach may suit you better if you want

  • A custom branded app from the Standard plan.
  • Zapier automations.
  • Nutrition tracking through MyFitnessPal.

Totality may suit you better if

  • You want nutrition tracking built into the app, with Australian food data.
  • You coach more than 50 clients on your own: Solo plans go up to 100 clients at a published price.
  • You may grow into a gym and want classes and member billing later without changing apps.
