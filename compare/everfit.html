Everfit alternative in Australia: Totality and Everfit compared

Everfit is coaching software for trainers, with a free plan for up to 5 clients and paid plans priced by client count. Here is how it lines up with Totality, using each company's own published information.

Checked Oct 2026. Prices change, so check Everfit's site before you decide.

Everfit and Totality side by side

Who it is forIndependent coaches, online coaching businesses, studios and gyms.Solo personal trainers, gyms and studios. A separate Totality Nutrition app for individuals.
Starting priceFree for up to 5 clients. Pro from $19 a month for 5 clients, $49 for 20 and $95 for 50, up to $290 for 300. Studio from $105 for 50 clients. Currency not stated on the page.Solo plans from a month (5 clients) to (200 clients). Studio a month for up to 500 clients and unlimited trainers. Club for up to 1,500 clients. Everfit may suit you better if you want

  • A free plan while you have 5 clients or fewer.
  • Custom branding on Pro and Studio.
  • Usage based automation with Autoflow.

Totality may suit you better if

  • You want client food logging and macro tracking included on every plan, not as an add-on.
  • You want to pay in Australian dollars with Australian food data built in.
  • You want an AI assistant, Tali, built into the app you and your clients use.
