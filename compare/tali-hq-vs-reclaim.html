Reclaim alternative: Tali HQ and Reclaim compared

Reclaim.ai is a calendar tool that protects time for habits, focus and meetings. Tali HQ is an AI manager that runs your inbox, clients and invoices as well as your week. Here is how they line up.

Checked Nov 2026. Prices and features change, so check Reclaim's site before you decide.

Reclaim may suit you better if you want

  • Deep automatic scheduling of habits, focus time and meetings.
  • Team calendar features like a shared out of office calendar, SSO and SCIM.
  • A free plan built around your calendar.

Tali HQ may suit you better if

  • You want your inbox sorted and replies drafted, not just your calendar.
  • You send invoices and want clients, leads and Stripe invoices with the rest of your week.
  • You want prices in Australian dollars and an AI that remembers you in a brain you can see.

Questions

Is Tali HQ a calendar app like Reclaim?

No. Tali knows your Google Calendar and books into it when you approve, but she doesn't auto-schedule habits and focus blocks the way Reclaim does. She also runs your inbox, clients and invoices.

Where do I get Tali HQ?

It's live now as a web app at talihq.com.au and works on iPhone, Android and computer. On your phone, add it to your home screen and it works like any other app. The App Store and Google Play versions are coming soon.
