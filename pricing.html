Pricing

Totality is for personal trainers, gyms and studios. Totality Nutrition is the food tracking app for anyone. Every price is in Australian dollars.

New accounts:

Help me choose a plan

Solo plans

For one trainer. Every Solo plan has the same tools. You pick the plan by how many clients you coach.

Gym plans

For gyms and studios with a team. Unlimited trainers, member billing, classes, payroll and the front desk tools.

Add-ons

Add these to a plan at any time and remove them when you like.

Yearly plans are 15% off twelve monthly payments, billed once a year.

Totality Nutrition plans

Food tracking with Australian food data. Add Tali AI if you want to describe meals in plain words and get meal plans and weekly summaries.

Totality members get % off the Nutrition plan. The member price is shown on that plan.

Available on Android and the web now. iPhone is coming soon. See every Nutrition feature.
