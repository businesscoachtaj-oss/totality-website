Pricing

Totality is for anyone who works with clients, solo or with a team: trainers, coaches, studios, gyms and allied health clinics. Totality Nutrition is the food tracking app for anyone. Totality Training is the workout tracker for people who train on their own. Tali HQ, out now, is Tali as your AI manager for business and life. Every price is in Australian dollars.

New accounts:

Help me choose a plan

Solo plans

For one person and their clients: a trainer, a coach or a solo practitioner. Start on Free, or pick a Solo plan by how many clients you see. Every Solo plan has the same tools, including the allied health tools, and includes Tali.

Studio plans

For a business with a team: studios, gyms and clinics. Unlimited trainers or practitioners, member billing, classes, payroll and the front desk tools.

Add-ons

Add these to a plan at any time and remove them when you like.

More than one location? On Studio, Club and Enterprise, each extra location is A$149.99 a month (A$1,529.90 a year on yearly plans), up to 5 locations. Each has its own members, staff, schedule and Stripe payouts, with one login and combined numbers across all of them. More than 5 is a custom quote.

Yearly plans are 15% off twelve monthly payments, billed once a year.

Listing on Discover is free. Any business can list on Totality Discover with a free account. To take card payments on your listing you need a studio plan or the POS add-on ( a month), and each payment has Totality's 1.25% fee plus the card fee (1.65% + 30c).

Totality Nutrition plans

Food tracking with Australian food data. Add Tali AI if you want to describe meals in plain words and get meal plans and weekly summaries.

New subscribers get 50% off their first 2 months on a monthly plan. It is applied at checkout, no code needed, and it is the same offer on every Totality app.

Buying for clients, members or staff? Team seats are a month each, from seats. Totality Nutrition for teams

Available on Android and the web now. The iPhone app is with Apple for review. See every Nutrition feature.

Totality Training New

A workout tracker for people who train on their own. Prices in Australian dollars. Every new account gets 7 days of everything, no card. On the web now, with the iPhone and Android apps sent to Apple and Google for review on 21 November 2026.

Free

A$0

Unlimited workout logging, the full exercise library with demos, 3 of your own routines, the Strength Foundations programme and 3 questions for Tali a day.

Training

A$8.99 a month, or A$91.70 a year

All 99 programmes, unlimited routines, weights that go up when you earn it, sets that adjust to how each muscle feels, strength charts and a weekly review.

Training + AI

A$11.99 a month, or A$122.30 a year

Everything in Training, plus Tali builds programmes around your week and kit, gives her take after each workout and writes up your week. 100 Tali credits a month.

Nutrition and Training together: pay for one app and the other is 40% off. A seat on a team counts as paying. With Totality Nutrition, Training is A$5.39 a month, or A$7.19 with AI, so both apps are A$14.38 a month. Paying for Training makes Nutrition 40% off when you join on the web or Android. First time on a monthly plan there is 50% off your first 2 months instead. The two offers do not combine.

Buying for clients, members or staff? Totality Training for teams, seats from A$6.99 a month each.

Tali HQ New

A separate app: Tali as your AI manager for business and life. Not tied to Totality or Totality Nutrition. On the App Store, Google Play and the web.

Free

A$0

Chat with Tali (250 messages a month), log food and training by telling her, a private journal she reads and remembers, a Friday check-in on your goals, 10 web searches and 1 Deep think a month.

Tali HQ

A$19.99 /month

or A$203.90 a year

Tali sorts your Gmail or Outlook and drafts the replies, briefs you each morning, checks the business twice a day for overdue invoices and cold leads, keeps clients, leads and Stripe invoices in one place and books into your Google Calendar. Nothing sent without your yes. 1,000 credits and 30 images a month.

Tali HQ Pro

A$49.99 /month

or A$509.90 a year

Everything in Tali HQ, plus your own Tali email address that customers write to and she answers with one tap, night school where you pick what she studies, and rules where you tell her what she can do on her own, like replying to booking requests. 3,000 credits and 100 images a month.

Tali HQ Max

A$149.99 /month

or A$1,529.90 a year

Everything in Tali HQ Pro, with 10,000 credits and 300 images a month. Deep think, Big brain and second opinions cost half the credits for your first 100 a month, she brings up to twice as many memories to each reply, and her voice is included when it arrives.

Tali HQ Business New

A$199.99 /month

Monthly only

For running a business from one place. Everything in Tali HQ Max, with the same 10,000 credits and 300 images a month, plus the business tools:

  • Jobs and quotes. Quote, job and invoice in one line, with a PDF that matches your invoices.
  • Pipeline. Who to chase next and what it could be worth.
  • Bookings. Your week, and one link your customers book from.
  • Projects. The bigger pieces of work, with their tasks.
  • Website. A simple site hosted for you, written by Tali if you like, with a contact form that lands as a lead.
  • On autopilot. Rules that put a nudge on your To do so nothing slips.
  • Bookkeeping pack. A quarter on one page, ready for your accountant.

Your own domain. Put your hosted site on your own domain for A$79 once, on any paid plan. A personal site comes with any paid plan. A business site, services and the booking button come with Tali HQ Business.

Coming soon, not on sale yet: Tali HQ Business Pro (A$389.99 a month) for a small team, with seats for your staff, text messages to customers and Tali answering your phone, and Tali HQ Business Max (A$649.99 a month) for a bigger team or more than one business. Tali's voice add-on (A$4.99 a month) is also coming soon.
