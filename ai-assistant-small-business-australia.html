An AI assistant for small business in Australia

Tali HQ runs the busy bits of a small business so you can do the work you're good at. Built in Australia by Totality 360, written in Australian English and priced in Australian dollars.

What she handles each day

  • A morning brief at the time you pick: what came in overnight, who needs a reply, what's on today and how the money looks
  • Your inbox sorted. Forward your Gmail to Tali or connect Outlook, and she flags what needs you and drafts the reply
  • Clients, leads and invoices in one place. Connect your own Stripe and she sends the invoice when you tap send, and marks it paid when your client pays
  • Your calendar. She knows your week from Google Calendar and books into it when you approve
  • Follow-ups. Health checks twice a day flag overdue invoices, quiet clients, new leads and drafts waiting on you
  • Web search with sources, and a weekly journal of how the business is going

Made for how Australian businesses work

Prices are in Australian dollars, with no conversion surprises. Tali writes in Australian English, and the people behind her are a small Australian software company you can actually reach.

You stay in charge

Tali drafts, you decide. She never sends a message, an invoice or a booking until you approve it, and she never asks for your passwords. You can see and delete anything she remembers about your business.

Hand her the front desk on Pro

Tali HQ Pro gives Tali her own email address. Give it to customers and she handles the enquiries, with one-tap replies. Pro also adds night school, where she studies your industry, and Rules, where she keeps watch for quiet clients and new leads.

Questions

How much does Tali HQ cost?

Free at A$0, Tali HQ at A$19.99 a month, Tali HQ Pro at A$49.99 a month or Tali HQ Max at A$149.99 a month, with 15% off yearly. Every new account starts with a 7 day free trial of Tali HQ.

Does Tali send emails or invoices on her own?

No. She drafts them and nothing goes out until you tap yes.

Which email does it work with?

Gmail and Outlook on the paid plans.

Where do I get Tali HQ?

It's live now as a web app at talihq.com.au and works on iPhone, Android and computer. On your phone, add it to your home screen and it works like any other app. The App Store and Google Play versions are coming soon.

Do I need Totality or Totality Nutrition?

No. Tali HQ is its own app with its own sign-up. You don't need any other app to use it.

Who makes Tali HQ?

Totality 360, an Australian software company.
