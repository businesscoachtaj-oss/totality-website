Common questions

How much does Totality cost for a gym?

The Studio plan is A$389.99 a month for up to 500 members with unlimited trainers. Club is A$649.99 a month for up to 1,500 members, with 2,500 Tali AI credits a month and your branding included. Enterprise is A$999.99 a month for up to 5,000 members with unlimited Tali AI on fair use. Prices are in Australian dollars and month to month.

Does Totality take a commission on member payments?

No. There is no commission on your revenue and payments go to your own Stripe account. A 1.25% platform fee applies to payments taken in the app, plus Stripe's card fee (1.65% + 30c for Australian cards). It is taken from your payout and never added to what your members pay. POS is included on Studio, Club and Enterprise.

Can I move my members across from Mindbody?

Yes. On gym plans the import wizard takes a CSV export from Mindbody, Trainerize or PTminder and maps the columns for you.

Does Totality do staff rosters and payroll?

Yes. Gym plans include staff rosters, a payroll summary with Xero export, and compliance documents with expiry alerts.

Is there a free trial or a contract?

Every new account gets a 7 day free trial, then 50% off the first 2 months on a monthly plan, applied automatically. There is no contract. Cancel any time from your account settings.
