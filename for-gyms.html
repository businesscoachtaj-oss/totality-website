One system for the desk, another for the trainers

Totality has gym management and PT coaching in the same app, so members, classes, sessions and billing sit together.

Built for running a gym

Needs attention dashboard

Failed payments, memberships expiring in the next 14 days, check-ins waiting for a reply, leads to follow up and classes that are full or nearly empty, on one screen.

Front desk check-in

Classes

Rosters, attendance, no-shows and waitlists. Cancel a class and members are refunded and notified.

Member billing and POS

Reports
