Open the zip

On a computer, double click it. On an iPhone or iPad, open it in the Files app. On Android, open it in Files by Google or your file manager.

Pick your version

The file names say the days a week and the level, for example full-body-3-intermediate. Start with the days you can train most weeks.

Track it

Open the matching spreadsheet in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. Pick your swaps, write in your weights and reps, and watch the Progress tab.
