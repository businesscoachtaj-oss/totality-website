What Totality Nutrition does

Australian food data

Foods from the Australian Food Composition Database come first, then packaged foods from Open Food Facts. Scan barcodes, save meals and import recipes from a link.

Logging features

Every nutrient, not just macros

A full vitamin and mineral breakdown against Australian recommended intakes, a targets calculator, hydration and trend insights.

Nutrients and targets

Streaks that forgive a slip

Your streak runs to 11:59pm, one missed day a week is forgiven, and milestones get a celebration. Progress photos, measurements and weight sit alongside.

Streaks and progress

A grocery list that writes itself

Pick a shopping window, and ingredients from your meal plan are combined by aisle in amounts you can buy. Mark pantry staples, tick off as you go and share.
