Common questions

How much does Totality Nutrition cost?

There is a Free plan at A$0. Nutrition is A$8.99 a month and Nutrition + AI is A$11.99 a month, in Australian dollars. Totality members get 50% off the Nutrition plan.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Every new account gets 7 days of the full Nutrition plan without a card. If you don't pick a plan after that, you move to Free and keep your account and food history.

Is there a free version?

Yes. The Free plan is A$0 forever: log food with your full history, macro targets, water, weight and progress, 10 saved meals, 7 barcode scans a week and Tell Tali what you ate 3 times a day. No ads. Every new account starts with 7 days of the full Nutrition plan, then moves to Free if you don't pick a plan.

Is Totality Nutrition on iPhone?

Not yet. It is on Android and the web now, and the iPhone app is with Apple for review. The web app works in Safari on iPhone in the meantime.

Where does the food data come from?

From the Australian Food Composition Database (AFCD), published by Food Standards Australia New Zealand, and from Open Food Facts for packaged foods. Each food shows its source.

What does Nutrition + AI add?

Tali. Describe a meal in plain words and Tali logs it, import recipes from any page, and get meal plans and a weekly summary. It includes 100 Tali credits a month.
