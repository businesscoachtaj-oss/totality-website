Features

Everything you need to track your nutrition. Nothing you don't.

Every feature included in one flat monthly price. No add-ons, no upgrades required — and there's more inside the app than we can fit on this page.

🇦🇺

Australian food database

Thousands of AU products from Woolies, Coles, IGA and more — plus the full Open Food Facts global database. Search and log in seconds.

📷

Barcode scanner that works

Scan any Australian product barcode and it logs instantly. No "product not found" for the stuff on your shelf.

🎯

Custom macro targets

Set calories and macros manually or use the built-in Mifflin-St Jeor calculator — fill in your stats and your targets are done.

⚖️

Adaptive Nutrition

Went over your calories yesterday? Your target for today automatically adjusts down to compensate — capped at a gentle 500 cal/day, carried forward if needed, with a 1,200 cal safety floor. Protein stays fixed; only carbs and fat trim to fit.

🔁

Calorie Cycling

Pick your training days and eat more on them — rest days automatically dial back so your weekly total stays exactly on target. Set it once and it runs itself.

📅

Weekly meal planning

Plan your whole week by meal slot — breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks. Drag saved meals in. See your macro totals for each day at a glance.

⭐

Saved meals

Save a meal you eat regularly with one tap. Re-log it any time in seconds — no searching, no re-entering serving sizes.

🔗

Recipe importer

Paste a link to any recipe online and Totality Nutrition reads the ingredients, matches them to our food database, and builds a full macro breakdown per serving. Review and tweak on one screen, then save it to My Recipes and log it again with a single tap.

🛒

Grocery list + share

Your weekly meal plan generates a grocery list automatically. Share it straight to your phone, your partner, or whoever's doing the shop.

📈

Weight trend graph

Log your weight daily with 0.1kg precision. A 7-day rolling average smooths out the noise so you can see the actual trend, not just day-to-day fluctuation.

📏

Body measurements

Track waist, hips, chest, arms and more over time. View change graphs to see where your body is actually shifting.

📸

Progress photos

Upload front, side and back photos with date stamps. Compare them side by side over time.

💧

Hydration tracking

Log your daily water intake against a target. Quick-add buttons for common amounts — no friction.

🥦

Fibre & micronutrient tracking

Fibre, sodium, sugar and saturated fat tracked alongside your macros. See the full nutritional picture.

💡

Trend insights

14-day pattern detection surfaces whether you're consistently under calories, missing protein targets or spiking sodium on weekends — actionable, not just data.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is it really one flat price? +

Yes. A$8.99/mo gets you every feature — barcode scanning, adaptive targets, calorie cycling, meal planning, grocery lists, progress photos, all of it. There are no premium tiers and nothing held back for an upsell.

Does it work if I already have a coach or trainer? +

If your coach uses Totality, nutrition tracking is already built into your free client app — you don't need a separate Totality Nutrition subscription. Totality Nutrition is for people tracking on their own, without a coach on the platform.

Why does Totality Nutrition have more features than the nutrition tracking inside Totality? +

Because it's a dedicated app with nutrition as its only job. That singular focus is why it goes deeper — recipe saving, calorie cycling, trend insights and more. That doesn't mean the nutrition tracking built into Totality's client app is lacking — it covers what a coach's clients need day to day — it's just that a purpose-built standalone app for individual tracking will always be able to go further on nutrition specifically than a feature inside a broader coaching platform.

Can I cancel any time? +

Yes. Totality Nutrition is month-to-month with self-serve cancellation — no contracts, no exit fees, no phone call required.

Does barcode scanning really work at Woolworths, Coles and IGA? +

Yes — that's the entire reason the app exists. The food database was built Australian-first, so scanning a product from Woolworths, Coles, IGA or ALDI logs it instantly instead of returning "product not found."

What if a product isn't in the database? +

The Australian database is combined with the full global Open Food Facts database, so coverage is broad. If something genuinely isn't listed, you can log it manually with your own nutrition figures.

Is there a free trial? +

Yes — try it free, no card required. New accounts also get 50% off their first 2 months automatically once they subscribe, no code needed.

Does Adaptive Nutrition mean my targets change every day? +

Only when it needs to. If you go over your calories, Adaptive Nutrition trims tomorrow's target down slightly to compensate — capped at a gentle 500 cal/day, with a 1,200 cal safety floor and protein always left untouched. Stay on target and nothing changes.

What devices does it run on? +

Totality Nutrition is a native app available on iOS and Android.
