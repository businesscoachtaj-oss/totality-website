Your documents and insurance

Keep your first aid, CPR, police check, qualifications and insurance on file with expiry reminders, and get cover through BizCover with $20 off.

2 min read · Updated 25 November 2026

On this page

  1. What you can keep
  2. Add or renew a document
  3. Insurance with BizCover

My documents keeps your certificates and checks in one place, with their expiry dates, so nothing lapses without you knowing. Open Settings and, under MY DOCUMENTS, tap Certificates and checks.

At a gym, your gym's owner can see these too, in their compliance hub. Otherwise only you can see them.

What you can keep#

First Aid Certificate, CPR Certificate, Police Check, Working With Children Check, Fitness Qualification/Certification, Public Liability Insurance, Professional Indemnity Insurance, Contract/Agreement and COVID Vaccination Record.

Each one shows where it's up to: ON FILE, EXPIRING SOON, EXPIRED or NOT ADDED.

Add or renew a document#

  1. Tap the document and upload a PDF or a photo.
  2. Tap Add expiry date, pick the date and tap Save. If it never runs out, tap It doesn't expire.
  3. When you renew, upload the new one with Replace and tap Change expiry date.

Tap View to open what's on file.

Insurance with BizCover#

Totality is a referral partner of BizCover, an Australian online insurance broker. When your public liability or professional indemnity isn't on file, or has expired, you see Get covered with BizCover (or Renew with BizCover) under it. Tap it to open Cover for fitness pros, with BizCover.

  • TOTALITY MEMBER OFFER: $20 off a policy with code TOTALITY. Tap Copy code.
  • WHAT YOU CAN COVER: public liability, professional indemnity, and personal accident and illness.
  • MADE FOR FITNESS WORK: no association membership needed, parks, council land and more than one gym, online classes and nutrition advice can be covered.
  • HOW IT WORKS: compare quotes from several insurers and buy online in minutes. Your certificate of currency comes to your inbox.
  1. Tap Get a quote with BizCover.
  2. Get your quote and use code TOTALITY at checkout.
  3. Back in Totality, tap I've renewed, upload my certificate and add it with its expiry date. You're reminded 30 and 7 days before it runs out.

About the cover

Insurance is arranged by BizCover Pty Limited (ABN 68 127 707 975, AFSL 501769), which acts as agent of its insurance partners and not as your agent. Totality doesn't give insurance advice. Read the policy documents before you buy.

Questions#

Do I have to buy insurance through BizCover?

No. Any insurer is fine. Upload your certificate either way so the reminders work.

Who sees my documents?

You. At a gym, your gym's owner or admin as well.

Will I get a reminder before something expires?

Yes. Add the expiry date and you're reminded 30 and 7 days before.
