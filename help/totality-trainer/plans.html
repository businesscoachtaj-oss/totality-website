Plans and prices

The 7 day trial, the Free plan, every Solo plan with its client limit and price, and the add-ons.

On this page

  • The 7 day trial
  • The Free plan
  • Solo plans
  • Add-ons
  • Trainers at a gym
  • Questions

Every new independent trainer starts with 7 days of everything. When the trial ends you drop to Free automatically, with no card needed and no lock. You only pay when you pick a Solo plan. All prices are in Australian dollars.

If you work at a gym on Totality, your gym pays and none of this applies to you. Skip to Trainers at a gym.

The 7 day trial#

Trial - Everything is open: programmes, sessions, check-ins, nutrition, messaging, scheduling, auto-regulation. - Up to 3 clients. - 50 Tali AI credits to try Tali. - No card needed.

When the trial has 2 days or fewer left, a banner on Home counts down. On the last day it reads "Last day of your trial. Tomorrow you move to Free, or pick a plan to keep Tali". You also get a reminder email 3 days before it ends.

The Free plan#

Free Free is yours for as long as you like.

  • Up to 3 clients.
  • Every Solo tool: programme builder, session logging, client check-ins, nutrition tracking, messaging, appointments and scheduling, the auto-regulation engine and push notifications.
  • No Tali AI. Her spot on Home shows what she does and a See plans button.
  • Add-ons (POS, Tali AI, Branding) come with a Solo plan, so they are not offered on Free.

Add-ons#

Paid Add-ons sit on top of a Solo plan. Add them from Settings > Manage Subscription. To change or cancel one later, tap Manage Billing on the same screen.

A 3.5% platform fee applies to POS and marketplace payments. It comes out of your payout and is never added to what your client pays. It is not a cut of your coaching income.

Trainers at a gym#

If you work at a gym on Totality, the gym's plan covers you. You don't pay anything and there's nothing to choose. Your Subscription screen shows Gym Partner, the gym's plan and how many of the gym's member spots are used.

  • Your client limit is the gym's limit, shared across every trainer there.
  • Tali credits are shared across the gym. Your gym admin can add more.
  • POS payments go to the gym's Stripe account, not yours.

Questions#

What happens to my clients when the trial ends?

Nothing. You keep your 3 clients, their programmes and all their history on Free. Tali switches off. Pick a Solo plan any time to get her back and add more clients.

I had more than 3 clients and moved to Free. Do I lose them?

No one is deleted. You just can't add a new client while you're at or over 3. Archive clients you no longer coach to get under the limit, or pick a Solo plan.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. Open Settings > Manage Subscription > Change Plan, pick Monthly or Yearly · Save 15%, then Switch to this plan.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. You only enter a card when you subscribe to a Solo plan.

What does "Up to 5 clients" count?

Active clients on your list. Archived clients are not counted.
