Your Discover listing

List you free on Totality Discover so people nearby find you, see your intro offer and prices, and send an enquiry or pay by card.

4 min read · Updated 27 November 2026

On this page

  1. Set up your listing
  2. Your intro offer
  3. Services and prices
  4. Card payments
  5. Enquiries
  6. What people see

Totality Discover is a free directory of gyms, trainers, studios and clinics on Totality. People search by suburb, postcode or distance at totalityapp.com.au/discover, see your intro offer and prices, and send you an enquiry or pay by card. They don't need an account to look or to enquire.

Listing is free, with no lock in. Switch it off any time.

Set up your listing#

  1. Open Settings and, under DISCOVER, tap Your Discover listing.
  2. Add a PHOTO OR LOGO (tap Add a photo).
  3. Check your BUSINESS NAME and pick WHAT YOU DO. That's the category people filter by.
  4. Write ABOUT YOU: what you do, who it suits, what to expect on day one.
  5. Under WHERE YOU ARE, start typing your address and pick it from the list. Leave Show my street address off to show your suburb only. Good for home studios and mobile trainers.
  6. Add your WEBSITE (OPTIONAL).
  7. Fill in the INTRO OFFER FOR NEW PEOPLE (see below) and your SERVICES AND PRICES.
  8. Turn on Show my listing on Discover and tap Save listing.

You see "Saved. Your listing is live on Discover." With the switch off, it saves but nobody can see it.

Your intro offer#

The intro offer is the first thing people see on your listing, for example "First class free".

  • Intro offer: the headline.
  • Offer details: the small print, for example "New members only. Book any reformer class."
  • Intro offer price: leave it empty for a free offer. People then send an enquiry to claim it. With a price, they can pay by card on your listing (see Card payments).

Discover has an Intro offers filter, so a clear offer helps you show up.

Services and prices#

Tap Add a price for each thing you sell. Give it a Price name (for example Online coaching), a Price, how often it's charged (A session, A week, A fortnight, A month or One off), an optional Length in minutes and an optional Note, like "1 session a week". They show on your listing under SERVICES AND PRICES.

Card payments#

People can pay for your intro offer and your services on your listing. The money goes to your own Stripe account, the same one you use in Totality.

Card payments need the POS add-on (A$9.99 a month) and your Stripe account. Tap the button in CARD PAYMENTS and Totality switches the add-on on, then you connect Stripe. If you already take payments in Totality, it uses the same account. See Getting paid.

Totality's fee is 1.25% plus Stripe's fee (1.65% + 30c for cards, or 1% + 30c up to A$3.50 for bank payments) on each payment. Payments made on your listing show under PAYMENTS in the same screen, where you can tap Refund on one.

Enquiries#

When someone taps Send enquiry (or Ask about the intro offer), they leave their name, an email or phone, and a message. Enquiries show under ENQUIRIES on your listing screen. Each enquiry is emailed to you as well, so nothing gets missed.

What people see#

Your listing shows your photo, name, category, intro offer, ABOUT, SERVICES AND PRICES, WHERE and WEBSITE, with a Book button for anything with a price and Send an enquiry instead under it. At the bottom it says Listed on Totality Discover and links to others near you.

People find you from the Discover page by searching Name, suburb or postcode, tapping Use my location (it shows businesses within 25 km), and filtering by Intro offers or a category: Gyms, Personal trainers, Pilates, Yoga, Group fitness, Physio and rehab, Massage, Recovery and Health clinics. Totality Nutrition and Totality Training users can open Discover from Find a coach near you.

Tali says

A real photo and an intro offer with the small print spelled out do most of the work. Keep "About" to what someone actually wants to know before their first visit.

Questions#

Does it cost anything to list?

No. Listing on Discover is free. You only pay the usual fees on card payments people make on your listing.

Can people see my home address?

Only if you turn on Show my street address. With it off, Discover shows your suburb only.

How do I take my listing down?

Turn off Show my listing on Discover and tap Save listing. It disappears straight away, and your details are kept for when you switch it back on.

Someone paid for my intro offer. What now?

They paid you through your Stripe account and the payment shows under PAYMENTS. Get in touch to book them in. If it can't go ahead, tap Refund.
