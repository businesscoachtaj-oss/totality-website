Signing up

Create your trainer account, confirm your email, pick Solo Trainer and add a profile photo.

On this page

  • Create your account
  • Tell Totality how you'll use it
  • Add a profile photo
  • Logging in next time
  • Questions

Signing up takes about two minutes. You need an email address you can open, and that's it. No card.

Create your account#

  1. Open Totality on your phone, or go to totalityapp.com.au, and tap Get Started: It's Free.
  2. Fill in First name, Email address, Password and Confirm password. Last name is optional.
  3. Got a referral code from another trainer or gym? Type it in Referral code. If you opened a referral link, the code is already filled in.
  4. Tap Create Account.
  5. Check your email for the confirmation link and tap it, then come back and log in.

Your password needs at least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, and a number or symbol.

Tell Totality how you'll use it#

The first time you log in you land on Welcome to Totality, which confirms your 7 day free trial has started.

  1. Under How will you use Totality? pick Solo Trainer ("Just me and my clients").
  2. Add a Business name if you have one. It's optional.
  3. Tap Let's Go →.

Pick Solo Trainer

Gym / Studio is for gym owners who manage a team. It creates a gym and makes you its admin. If you're a trainer who works at someone else's gym, pick Solo Trainer and accept the gym's invite when it arrives. See Joining a gym.

Add a profile photo#

Let's set up your account asks for a profile photo. Tap the circle to pick one, or tap Skip for now. Clients see your photo in messages. Tap Let's Go → and Totality takes you to Home and starts the tour.

Logging in next time#

Open the app and tap Log In. Use the same email and password on your phone and on the web.

Forgot your password? Tap Forgot Password? on the login screen, enter your email, and Totality emails you a reset link.

Questions#

I never got the confirmation email.

Check your spam or promotions folder. The email comes from Totality. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, email admintotality@gmail.com with the address you used.

It says an account with this email already exists.

You (or a gym) already made an account with that email. Tap Log In instead. If you don't know the password, reset it from the login screen.

My gym told me to sign up. Should I pick Gym / Studio?

No. Pick Solo Trainer. The gym sends you an invite, and when you accept you become a trainer at that gym.

Can I change my business name later?

The name your clients see can be set in Settings > Branding with the Branding add-on. See Branding. Your own first and last name are in Settings > Profile.
