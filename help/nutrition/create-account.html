Create your account

Sign up, log in, reset a forgotten password and accept the terms.

On this page

  • Sign up
  • Accept the terms
  • Log in
  • Forgot your password
  • Questions

You can sign up in the app or on the web. Both give you the same account.

Sign up#

  1. Open the app, or go to totalitynutrition.com.au, and tap Sign up at the bottom of the log in screen.
  2. Enter your First name (required) and Last name (optional).
  3. Enter your Email address.
  4. Choose a Password: at least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, and a number or symbol. Type it again in Confirm password. The eye icon shows or hides what you typed.
  5. If a friend gave you a code, type it into Referral code. If you opened a friend's share link, the code is already filled in and shows "Referral code applied". Tap Change to use a different one.
  6. Tap Create Account.

You go straight into onboarding, and a welcome email is sent to you.

Good to know

The Referral code box doesn't show in the iPhone app, because referral discounts apply to web and Android billing only. Use the web or Android app to sign up with a code.

Accept the terms#

The first time you sign in, the Terms & Conditions sheet appears. Read the key points, tick the box to confirm you've read them and that you are 18 or older, then tap Accept & Continue. Tap Read the full Terms & Conditions to see the full version. The sheet keeps coming back until you accept, even if you close the app.

Log in#

Enter your Email address and Password, then tap Log In. If you see "Please confirm your email address before logging in", open the confirmation email we sent and tap the link, then try again.

Forgot your password#

  1. On the log in screen, tap Forgot Password?
  2. Enter your email address and tap Send Reset Link.
  3. Open the email and tap the link. It opens a page where you choose a new password.
  4. Log in with the new password.

Questions#

It says an account with this email already exists.

You've already signed up, possibly in Totality. Tap Log in instead. If you've forgotten the password, use Forgot Password?

Why does it say my password needs a mix of characters?

Passwords need at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and a number or symbol.

Can I add a referral code after I've signed up?

The box is only on the sign up screen. If you missed it, email admintotality@gmail.com.

The reset email hasn't arrived.

Check your junk folder, and check you typed the same email you signed up with. You can send another link from Forgot Password?
