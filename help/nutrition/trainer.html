Using Nutrition with a Totality trainer

Log in with your Totality login, what's shared between the two apps, and how your Nutrition trial and plan work alongside Totality.

On this page

  • Log in with your Totality login
  • What's shared both ways
  • Your Nutrition trial and plan
  • Questions

If your gym or personal trainer uses Totality, Totality Nutrition is a separate app that uses the same account. What you log in one shows in the other.

Log in with your Totality login#

  1. Download Totality Nutrition, or open totalitynutrition.com.au.
  2. Tap Log In with the same email and password you use for Totality. Don't sign up again.
  3. Accept the terms if asked.
  4. Do the five onboarding steps. If you've already confirmed your units in Totality, they're filled in for you.
  5. Tap Get Started.

If you've forgotten your password, use Forgot Password? on the log in screen. It resets the password for both apps.

What's shared both ways#

Shared Notes
Food log Food you log in Nutrition shows in your Totality food log, and your trainer sees it in your client profile. Food logged in Totality shows in Nutrition.
Targets Targets your trainer sets in Totality show in Nutrition. Targets you save in Nutrition (onboarding, Calculator, Custom Targets) become your current targets in both. The most recent targets always win.
Saved meals The same saved meals in both apps.
Water Same entries and same daily target.
Weight, measurements, progress photos Same history in both apps. Your trainer can see them in Totality.
Wearable One connection for both apps.

Heads up

Finishing Nutrition onboarding saves a fresh set of targets dated today, and those become your current targets. If your trainer set your targets, check with them afterwards. They can set them again from Totality, or you can type theirs into Settings, Custom Targets.

Your Nutrition trial and plan#

  • Your Nutrition 7 day trial starts at your first Nutrition onboarding, not when you joined Totality.
  • Your gym or trainer's Totality plan doesn't include the Nutrition app's paid features. After the trial you move to Free unless you pick a Nutrition plan.
  • Tali's limits in the Nutrition app come from your Nutrition plan. See Tali AI.
  • The Totality member price (A$4.49 a month for Nutrition) is for people who pay for their own Totality plan, like trainers and gym owners. Clients of a Totality gym or trainer pay the normal price, and get the welcome offer instead.

Questions#

Do I need both apps?

No. Many clients log food in Nutrition and use Totality for training and check-ins. Use whichever suits you; the data is the same.

Can my trainer see what I eat?

Yes. Food you log in Nutrition shows in your Totality food log, which your trainer can see.

My targets changed after I did onboarding.

Onboarding saves its own targets. Ask your trainer to set yours again in Totality, or type them into Custom Targets.

I'm a trainer. Do I get a discount?

If you pay for a Totality plan through our website, the plans screen shows the Totality member price automatically on the web and Android.
