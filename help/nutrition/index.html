Totality Nutrition guide

Start here

What Totality Nutrition does, what Free and the paid plans include, and the quickest way to get set up.

On this page

  • The two tiers
  • Get set up in five minutes
  • Where things live
  • Questions

Totality Nutrition is a calorie and macro tracker built on Australian food data. You log what you eat, it shows calories, protein, carbs and fat against your own targets, and it keeps your history. It runs on Android and in your web browser at totalitynutrition.com.au, and the iPhone app is with Apple for review (until it's out, use the web app in Safari). Your account is the same everywhere.

The two tiers#

Every account is one of these:

  • Free A$0, forever. Food logging by search with your full history, calories and macros against your targets, water, weight and Progress, the calculator, up to 10 saved meals, 7 barcode scans a week and Tell Tali what you ate 3 times a day. No ads.
  • Paid Nutrition (A$8.99 a month) or Nutrition + AI (A$11.99 a month). Paid adds Insights, Breakdown, Meal Plan, Grocery List, Recipes, unlimited saved meals, unlimited barcode scans and Apple Health or Health Connect. Nutrition + AI also adds Tali's AI features with 100 credits a month.

Every new account starts with a 7 day trial Trial of the full Nutrition plan. No card is needed. When the 7 days are up you move to Free, unless you pick a plan. Nothing is deleted. See Plans compared for the full table.

Throughout this guide, badges show who gets what: Free Trial Paid.

Get set up in five minutes#

  1. Create your account with your name, email and a password. See Create your account.
  2. Answer five quick onboarding questions: units, goal, training days, body stats and date of birth. See Onboarding.
  3. Check the targets it works out for you and tap Get Started.
  4. Take Tali's short tour of Home. It takes under a minute. See Home and the tour.
  5. Log your first meal. Tap Log Food, then search, scan a barcode or tell Tali what you ate. See Your food log.

Tali says

G'day, I'm Tali. Best first step is logging what you've eaten so far today. Search it, scan it, or just tell me. No need to be perfect on day one.

Where things live#

The bar along the bottom has three tabs:

Tab What it is
Home (house) Today's calories and macros, your streak and Quick access tiles
Nutrition (apple) Your food log for the day, meal by meal
Calculator Work out or redo your calorie and macro targets

Everything else opens from Home: Breakdown, Meal Plan, Grocery List, Hydration, Insights and Progress are the Quick access tiles, and the gear icon at the top opens Settings.

The purple ? button that floats on most screens opens Report an Issue. See Troubleshooting.

Questions#

Is Totality Nutrition really free?

Yes. The Free plan is A$0 with no time limit and no ads. You keep food logging, your full history, targets, water, weight, Progress, 7 barcode scans a week, up to 10 saved meals and Tell Tali 3 times a day.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The 7 day trial needs no card. If you don't pick a plan, you move to Free when it ends.

Can I use it on my computer?

Yes. Open totalitynutrition.com.au in any browser and log in with the same email and password. Barcode scanning by camera and wearables need the phone app; on the web you can type a barcode number instead.

I already use Totality with my trainer. Do I need a new account?

No. Log in with your Totality email and password. See Using Nutrition with a Totality trainer.
