Trial and offers#

  • 7 day free trial. Every new account gets everything in Training + AI for 7 days, no card. Then you move to Free unless you pick a plan.
  • 50% off your first 2 months on a monthly plan, the first time you pay for Training.
  • 40% off every Training plan while you have Totality Nutrition, on your own plan or a team seat. See With Totality Nutrition. The offers do not combine. If your Nutrition plan ends, the 40% comes off from your next bill and we email you first.
  • Black Friday: until Monday 30 November 2026 the first 2 months offer is 60% off, not 50%.
  • Referred by a friend: sign up with a friend's referral code and your first 2 months on a monthly plan are 70% off (the 50% offer plus 20% from the referral).
  • Refer a friend: open Settings, tap Refer a friend and share your code. Once a friend subscribes you get 20% off your own plan, and it stacks for up to 5 friends.

Covered by a team#

If a business, club or group gave you a team code, your plan is covered and you pay nothing. Buying for your own people? Seats are A$6.99 a month each for Training and A$8.99 for Training + AI, 3 seats or more. See Teams.

Choose, change or cancel#

  1. Open Settings and tap Your plan, or tap Plans on the trial banner on Home.
  2. Pick Monthly or Yearly, then tap Choose Training or Choose Training + AI. You pay with Stripe.
  3. To change plan, tap Change to the other plan. Stripe works out the difference on your next bill.
  4. To change your card or cancel, tap Manage plan, card or cancel.

Plans renew until you cancel. When a paid plan ends you go back to Free, and everything you logged stays.

Questions#

What happens to my programme on Free?

Your logging, history and 3 routines stay. Paid programmes, Progress and the automatic changes stop until you pick a plan.
