Totality Training guide

Tali

Ask Tali about your training, have her build a programme, and read her write-up of your week.

On this page

  • Chat with Tali
  • Have Tali build a programme
  • Weekly review
  • Tali and your data
  • Questions

Tali is the AI coach in Totality Training. She is optional: everything else works without her. Tali can make mistakes and is not a doctor or physio.

Chat with Tali#

Tap Tali's face in the middle of the bar. She can see your goal, your programme and your recent workouts, so ask about technique, a plateau, warming up, recovery or planning your week. Tap a suggested question or type your own.

  • Free 3 questions a day.
  • Training + AI 1 credit a question, from 100 credits a month. They top up at the start of each month.

Have Tali build a programme#

Training + AI

  1. On Train, under Make your own, tap Have Tali build one.
  2. Check your goal, days a week, minutes a session and where you train.
  3. Under Anything else, tell her about an injury, a target lift or what you want more of.
  4. Tap Build my programme. It uses 5 of your Tali credits.
  5. Read her draft. Tap Use this programme, or add a note and build it again.

Nothing changes until you say yes. Her programme is saved under My programmes, marked Built by Tali, and you can edit it like any other.

Weekly review#

Open Weekly review from Quick access on Home. Training shows your workouts this week, personal bests, and the weight and set changes. Training + AI adds Tali's write-up: what went well and one thing to focus on next.

Tali and your data#

The first time Tali needs your training data, she asks for your yes. To build programmes and write her replies, Totality Training sends your first name, goal, level, training days, equipment, the workouts you log, what you type to her and, if Totality Nutrition is switched on, your daily protein and calories to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude model Tali runs on. Anthropic does not use it to train its models. Your email and payment details are never sent. Turn Tali off any time in Settings, Tali and your data.

Questions#

I ran out of credits.

They top up at the start of next month. On the free trial, the trial credits are used first.

Can Tali see my food?

Only your daily protein and calories, and only if Totality Nutrition is switched on in Training.
