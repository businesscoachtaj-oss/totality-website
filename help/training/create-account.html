Create your account

Sign up, answer four questions and start the programme matched to you.

On this page

  • Sign up
  • The four questions
  • Your programme is ready
  • Questions

Signing up takes about a minute. Four questions pick a programme that fits your goal, your level, your week and your kit.

Sign up#

  1. Tap Start free.
  2. Enter your first name, your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Tick the box to agree to the terms and privacy policy, then tap Create account.

Already use Totality or Totality Nutrition? Tap Log in instead and use the same email and password. If you sign up with an email that already has an account, the app tells you to log in.

The four questions#

  1. What's your main goal? Build muscle, Get stronger, Lose fat or Get fitter.
  2. How long have you been lifting? New to it, Under a year, 1 to 3 years, or 3 years or more. This sets the level.
  3. What does your week look like? Days a week (2 to 6), time per session (30, 45, 60 or 75 min +) and the days you usually train. Pick as many days as you train, or the app spreads them out for you.
  4. Where do you train? Full gym, Home with dumbbells, No equipment or A mix. Programmes only use what you have.

Your programme is ready#

The last screen shows the programme matched to you, with its workouts. Your 7 day free trial starts here. If you have Totality Nutrition on the same account, you can choose to show today's food on your Training Home screen.

  • Tap Start this programme to put it on your week straight away.
  • Tap Look around first to skip it. You can pick a programme later from Train.

You can change any of these answers later in Settings, Your goal and week.

Questions#

I forgot my password.

On the log in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. A link to set a new one is sent to that email.

Does picking a programme here lock me in?

No. You can switch to any other programme from Train at any time. Your history and personal bests are kept.
