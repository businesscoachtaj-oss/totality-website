Settings

Your profile, plan, goal and week, Tali, units, Sleep sounds, and deleting your account.

2 min read · Updated 25 November 2026

On this page

  1. What is in Settings
  2. Units and region
  3. Sleep sounds
  4. Delete your account

Tap the gear at the top of Home.

What is in Settings#

  • Your profile. Tap your initials or Add a profile picture to add a photo.
  • Your plan. Opens Plans. See Plans and billing.
  • Teams. Join with a team code, or cover Training for your people. See Teams.
  • Refer a friend. Get your referral code and share it. Your friend gets 70% off their first 2 months on a monthly plan, and you get 20% off your own plan once they subscribe. See Plans and billing.
  • Totality Nutrition. What is shared between the two apps. See With Totality Nutrition.
  • Your goal and week. Change your goal, level, days, time and kit.
  • Tali and your data. What Tali uses, and turning her on or off. See Tali.
  • Units and region. kg or lb, cm or ft in, kcal or kJ, and more.
  • Find a coach near you. Opens Totality Discover: trainers, gyms, studios and clinics near you, with their intro offers and prices. Search by suburb or postcode, or use your location, and send an enquiry.
  • Insurance with BizCover, under FOR COACHES AND TRAINERS. If you coach people, it opens Totality's insurance page: public liability and professional indemnity, with $20 off a policy using code TOTALITY.
  • Privacy policy, Terms and Sign out.
  • Delete my account.

Units and region#

Choose your country and language, and the units for body weight, measurements, energy, water and lifting weights. Changing these never changes your saved data.

Sleep sounds#

Open Sleep sounds from Quick access on Home. Tap a scene, like Rain on a tin roof or Brown noise, and it fades out so it doesn't wake you. Save a mix you like and it shows under Your mixes. Free on every plan.

Delete your account#

Delete my account deletes your account and everything in it, in Totality Training, Totality Nutrition and Totality, and cancels any paid plan. It cannot be undone.

Questions#

Does changing my goal change my programme?

It changes which programmes are matched to you. To switch, pick one from Choose from a template.
