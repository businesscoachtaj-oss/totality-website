With Totality Nutrition

One account for both apps, what is shared, and 40% off when you pay for one of them.

On this page

  • What is shared
  • 40% off when you have both
  • Questions

Totality Training and Totality Nutrition use the same account. Log in to both with the same email and they line up.

What is shared#

  • Nutrition in Training: today's food on Home (calories and protein against your targets), and your weekly protein in the weekly review.
  • Training in Nutrition: the day's workout shows on Totality Nutrition's home screen.
  • The same in both: body weight, measurements and progress photos. Log them in either app.

Open Settings, Totality Nutrition to turn any of these off.

40% off when you have both#

  • Pay for Totality Nutrition and every Training plan is 40% off: A$5.39 a month, or A$7.19 with AI.
  • Pay for Totality Training and Nutrition is 40% off when you join on the web or Android.
  • So both apps together are A$14.38 a month.

The 40% off does not combine with the welcome offer of 50% off your first 2 months.

Questions#

Do I need two accounts?

No. One email and password works in Totality, Totality Nutrition and Totality Training.
