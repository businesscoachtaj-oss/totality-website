Bookkeeping pack

A quarter or a financial year on one page for your accountant, with money taken outside the app, GST, expenses, payroll, payouts and Xero. Share it, email it or send it to Xero.

On this page

  • Pick a period
  • What is in it
  • GST
  • Send it
  • Xero
  • Questions

The Bookkeeping pack puts a quarter or a financial year on one page: what you took, in the app and outside it, where it came from, what is still owed, what went out and what Stripe paid into your bank. It is made from what is already in Totality. It is for your records and your accountant, and it is not tax advice.

It comes with POS, which is included on every gym plan. Find it with Find a screen on your dashboard.

Pick a period#

Under Which period, pick a quarter (for example Oct to Dec 2026) or a whole financial year (July to June).

What is in it#

Four figures sit at the top: Taken in the app, Outside the app, GST collected and Expenses. Underneath:

  • Taken in the app: every member payment through Totality, grouped by type, such as memberships, PT packages, one-off payments, POS and classes. Memberships and packs count every payment.
  • Taken outside the app: cash, bank transfers or PayID, and other apps, from the amounts your trainers put in when they tap Add sessions on a client's page.
  • Invoices paid in the period and what is still owed to you.
  • Payroll paid, from pay periods marked paid.
  • Fees taken by Stripe and Totality, read from Stripe for the period.
  • Expenses: tap Add an expense to record one here, with the date, what it was for, the supplier and the amount including any GST. Tap the bin to remove one.
  • Paid into your bank: what Stripe paid out to you in the period, so your accountant can match it to your bank statement.

Payments are what was taken, before card and platform fees.

GST#

If your gym is registered for GST, switch on Registered for GST in your business details (open any member's page, Invoices, the settings button). The pack then shows GST collected: one eleventh of everything you took that includes GST, after refunds, plus the GST recorded on your paid invoices.

An invoice paid in the app

If you also made an invoice for money a member paid in the app, that money is in the pack twice: once as an app payment and once on the invoice. The spreadsheet shows how each invoice was paid so your accountant can spot it.

Send it#

  • Share the spreadsheet saves one file with every payment, money taken outside the app, invoice, expense, payroll line and payout for the period.
  • Make a PDF summary makes a short PDF of the figures. Tap Open the PDF summary to view or save it.
  • Email it to my accountant sends the spreadsheet and the PDF summary as attachments. Replies come back to your own email. You can send up to 5 a day.

Xero#

When Xero is connected, the Membership income card sits in the pack too, so you can see what has gone to Xero next to the figures. See Xero.

Heads up

Check the figures with your accountant before lodging anything.

Questions#

Why doesn't Taken in the app match what landed in my bank?

Taken in the app is before fees. Fees taken shows what Stripe and Totality took, and Paid into your bank shows what Stripe actually paid you.

Where does cash for PT sessions show?

Only if it's recorded. When a member pays their trainer cash for sessions, the trainer taps Add sessions on that client's page, picks Cash and puts in the amount. It then shows under Taken outside the app, and in Credit checks with who added it.

Can I see last financial year?

Yes. Pick it from Which period. The drop down has this financial year and the two before it.

Do expenses I added on Revenue show here?

Yes. It's the same list of expenses, so one added in either place shows in both.
