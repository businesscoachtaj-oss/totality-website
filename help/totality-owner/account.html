Account, privacy and deleting

Sign out, keep your data, understand privacy, and delete your account safely.

On this page

  • Sign out
  • Your data
  • Privacy
  • Delete your account
  • Questions

Sign out#

Tap Sign out at the bottom of Settings, or at the bottom of the dashboard. On a shared front desk computer, always sign out at the end of the day.

Your data#

Your gym's data belongs to you. You can download it at any time:

  • Transactions and revenue summary: Insights, then Exports and revenue breakdown, then Summary CSV or All transactions CSV. See Revenue and insights.
  • Revenue transactions (last 90 days): the download button on Revenue.
  • Expenses: Reports, then Expenses, then CSV.
  • Pay periods: Payroll, then a trainer's period, then Export CSV.

If you need anything else, such as a full export of your members, email admintotality@gmail.com.

Privacy#

Totality 360 Pty Ltd handles personal information under the Australian Privacy Principles. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions, also linked at the bottom of your dashboard.

  • Members' health information from intake (PAR-Q, injuries, conditions) is only visible to your gym.
  • Card details are held by Stripe, never by Totality.
  • Admin notes and compliance documents are never shown to members.
  • Someone who already has a Totality account only joins your gym, and you only see their details, after they accept your invite.

Delete your account#

Before you delete

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Before you do it:

  1. If you are closing the gym, cancel your members' memberships and PT packages in Memberships and PT packages, so they stop being charged.
  2. Download anything you want to keep (see Your data above). Deleting your account also cancels your gym's Totality plan in Stripe, so you are not billed again. You do not need to cancel it first.
  1. Open More, then Settings, and scroll to the bottom.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Read "This will permanently delete your account and all your data. This cannot be undone."
  4. Tap Delete My Account.

You see Account deleted and are signed out.

If you cannot sign in, email admintotality@gmail.com from your account's email address and ask us to delete it.

Questions#

If I delete my account, are my trainers and members deleted too?

Their own logins are separate and are not deleted. Talk to us at admintotality@gmail.com before deleting if you are closing or selling the gym, so we can help with your trainers' and members' accounts.

Can I get my account back after deleting it?

No. Deleting is permanent.

I'm selling my gym. Can I hand the account over?

Email admintotality@gmail.com before you hand anything over, and we will talk you through the options.
