Signing up and your first login

Create your business account, pick what you do, set up the basics and take the quick tour.

4 min read · Updated 27 November 2026

  2. Tell Totality what you run
  3. Set up your gym

  5. The quick tour

  7. Signing in later

You can create your gym's Totality account yourself in a few minutes, on your phone or at totalityapp.com.au. Your 7-day free trial starts as soon as the account is made, with no card needed.

Create your account#

  1. Get the Totality app from the App Store or Google Play, or go to totalityapp.com.au on a computer.
  2. Tap Get Started: It's Free.
  3. Fill in First name, Last name (optional), Email address, Password (at least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, and a number or symbol) and Confirm password.
  4. If another gym or trainer gave you a referral code, type it in Referral code. If you opened their share link, it is filled in for you.
  5. Tap Create Account.

Tell Totality what you run#

Next you see Welcome to Totality with the line "Your 7-day free trial has started."

  1. Under ARE YOU SOLO OR A STUDIO OWNER? pick Studio owner ("I run a business with a team"). Solo is for one trainer or practitioner on their own, and does not give you the gym tools.
  2. Under WHAT DO YOU DO? tap the closest match. It sets up your layout: what your people are called, what the schedule tab is called, and which tools are switched on.
  3. Under YOUR BUSINESS, type your Business name, for example Iron Works. You can change it later in Settings.
  4. Read the GETTING STARTED tips and tap Let's Go →.

What changes with the type you pick:

  • What your people are called. Members for a gym or studio, Clients for personal training and some practices, Patients for physio, chiro and osteo.
  • The schedule tab's name. A class studio (Pilates, Yoga, Dance and so on) sees Timetable. A clinic sees Bookings. Everyone else sees Schedule.
  • Which tools are on. For example Pilates starts with classes and memberships, a gym with memberships, classes, session packs and programmes, and a physio with services, intake forms, treatment notes and funding.
  • Your setup card. A studio's first job is Add your timetable, a clinic's is Set your service prices. A gym keeps Add your trainers, Add your members and Connect payments.

The closest one is fine

You can change the type, and switch any tool on or off, any time in Settings under What kind of business is this? Switching a tool off only hides it. Nothing already saved is deleted. Each account can create one business. If you picked Solo by mistake, email admintotality@gmail.com and we will fix it.

Set up your gym#

The Set up your gym screen asks for your gym's Address, Phone and Gym email, so clients and trainers can find you. All three are optional. Fill them in and tap Let's Go →, or tap Skip and add them later in Settings.

Accept the terms#

The first time you reach the dashboard you are asked to read and accept the Totality Terms & Conditions. Tap Read the full Terms & Conditions to read them, then Accept & Continue.

The quick tour#

Tali, the assistant inside Totality, then walks you round the dashboard in about a minute. She points out:

  • Your morning brief: a short summary of today
  • Needs attention today: the list of things to act on
  • Key numbers: revenue, members, trainers, sessions and leads
  • Sections: Members, Sales and billing, Schedule, Staff and Insights
  • each tab along the bottom

There are 10 short steps. Tap Next to move on, Back to go back, or Skip tour at the top right to close it. The tour only shows once.

What to do next#

The dashboard shows a Set up your gym card with three steps: Add your trainers, Add your members and Connect payments. Each one ticks itself off when it is done. See Your dashboard.

Signing in later#

Open the app or totalityapp.com.au, type your email and password and tap Log In. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot Password?, type your email and tap Send Reset Link. The email has a link to set a new one.

Questions#

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

No. The trial starts without a card. You add your card when you pick a plan.

Can someone else at the gym also be an admin?

Each gym has one owner account that signed up. Your trainers get their own logins with the trainer tools. If you need a second admin login, email admintotality@gmail.com.

I signed up as Solo by mistake. What now?

Email admintotality@gmail.com from your account email and we will move you to a gym account.

I have been sent an invite to join a gym. Is this guide for me?

No. If a gym invited you as a trainer, see the trainer guide. If you are a member, see the member guide.

Can I see the tour again?

Not at the moment. Everything it covers is in this guide, starting with Your dashboard.
