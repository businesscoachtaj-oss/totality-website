On autopilot

Rules that work out who is due a follow up and line up the message for your yes. Welcome, win back, failed payment, new lead, review and birthday.

On this page

  • The rules for a gym
  • Send, skip or rewrite
  • Change a rule

On autopilot is for the follow ups you mean to send and forget. Switch on a rule and Totality works out who is due, then shows you the message ready to go. Nothing goes out until you tap Send.

Find it with Find a screen on your dashboard, or in Everything under Growing.

The rules for a gym#

Rule When it lines up a message
Welcome someone new Someone joined in the last 7 days
Check in when they go quiet No logged session for 14 days (you can pick 7, 14, 21 or 30)
Chase a failed payment A payment has not gone through
Follow up a new lead A lead is still marked New after 1 day (or 2 or 3)
Ask for a review Someone has been with you 21 days and is still training (or 42, 56 or 90)
Happy birthday On their birthday, when a date of birth is saved

Send, skip or rewrite#

Under Waiting for your yes you see three messages at a time, with Show all for the rest. For each one:

  • Send sends it as a message in the app, with a notification on their phone.
  • Skip leaves that person alone for this round.
  • Ask Tali to write it has Tali write a personal version for 1 Tali credit. You can still edit it before you send.
  • On a phone, Text from my phone opens the message in your own Messages app when the person has a mobile number saved.

A lead does not have the app yet, so a lead follow up goes by text or email from you. Sending it moves the lead from New to Contacted in Leads.

Change a rule#

Tap a rule to open it.

  • Change the number of days.
  • Edit What it says. Write {name} where their first name should go.
  • See how many were sent and skipped in the last 30 days.
  • Turn this off to stop the rule.

Nobody gets the same nudge twice in 30 days.

Good to know

Texts sent for you from a business number are coming soon. They are not available yet.

Does anything go out without me?

No. Every message waits on this screen for your tap.

Does it cost Tali credits?

Only when you tap Ask Tali to write it, which uses 1 credit. Your own template with the first name filled in is free.
