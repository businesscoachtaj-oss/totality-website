Plans, credits and billing

What each plan includes, what a credit is, what happens when you run out, and how to upgrade, change or cancel.

Tali HQ has five plans: Free, Tali HQ, Tali HQ Pro, Tali HQ Max and Tali HQ Business. Every new account starts with a 7 day free trial of Tali HQ. Prices are in Australian dollars, and you can pay monthly or yearly (15% less). Tali HQ Business is monthly only.

The free trial Trial#

Every new account gets 7 days of Tali HQ, with 1,000 credits. No card needed. Today shows how many days are left, and Me shows "Trial ends" and the date.

When the trial ends, you move to Free automatically. Nothing is deleted. Pro features are not part of the trial.

See how many you have left#

Open Me. Your plan card shows how many credits you have left this month, with a bar, plus extras you've bought. Underneath are images left, web searches left, and the date your credits top up. On Free it shows how many of your 250 messages are left, your web searches and your Deep think for the month.

When you run out#

Credits and messages reset on the 1st of each month, in your time zone. That's true on yearly plans too. Unused credits don't carry over.

  • Free: when your 250 messages are used, Talk says "That's your 250 free messages for this month. Upgrade to Tali HQ to keep chatting."
  • Paid plans: Talk says "You've used this month's Tali credits. They top up on the 1st, or you can move up a plan." Scheduled jobs (brief, Night school and so on) skip until the 1st. New emails still come in, without a summary or draft.

Top up#

On a paid plan or the trial, open Me and tap Top up on your plan card (Talk also shows a Top up button when you run out) to buy a one-off pack of extra credits, extra images, or both. Extras never expire and are used after your monthly allowance.

Choose a plan#

  1. Open Me and tap Plans on your plan card.
  2. Choose Monthly or Yearly, save 15% at the top.
  3. On the trial, tap Keep Tali HQ after my trial on Tali HQ, or Choose Tali HQ Pro or Choose Tali HQ Max. On Free, tap Choose on the plan you want.
  4. Stripe's secure checkout opens. Enter your card (and a promotion code if you have one) and pay.
  5. You come back to Tali HQ. Your new plan shows on Me.

Your plan starts straight away and your card is charged at checkout. If you're on a phone app, checkout opens in your browser and brings you back to the web app; your plan shows in the app the next time you open Plans.

Change or cancel#

Once you're paying, tap Manage billing on Plans. It opens Stripe's billing page, where you can:

  • update your card
  • see and download your invoices
  • switch plans. On Tali HQ, the Pro card says "Switching to Pro happens in Manage billing, where your card already is." Tap Switch to Pro in Manage billing.
  • cancel

When a paid plan ends, you drop back to Free. Your chats, journal, clients and everything Tali knows stay.

Founding members#

Founder pricing closed to new sign-ups on 10 November 2026. If you already have it, Plans says "You are a founding member: 42.5% off every paid plan for as long as you stay", shows your price with the full price crossed out, and the discount is applied at checkout.

Questions#

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The 7 day trial starts when you make your account, with no card.

What happens to my data if I go back to Free?

It all stays. Features that need a paid plan are locked until you upgrade again.

Can I buy extra credits?

Yes, on a paid plan or the trial. Open Me and tap Top up. Extras never expire. If you keep running out, the next plan up has about three times as many credits.

Do unused credits roll over?

No. Each month starts fresh on the 1st.

If I pay yearly, do I get all my credits up front?

No. Credits reset each month on yearly plans too.

How do I cancel?

Me, Plans, Manage billing, then cancel in Stripe.

I paid but Me still shows my old plan.

Close the Plans screen and open Me again, or pull down to refresh. It can take a moment for Stripe to tell Tali HQ. Still wrong after a few minutes? Email admintotality@gmail.com.
