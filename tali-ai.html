Meet Tali, the AI in both apps

Tali writes the first draft so you can get on with coaching. In Totality it drafts messages and programmes for trainers. In Totality Nutrition it turns a meal you describe into a food log, and plans your week.

Availability:

Tali in Totality Tali in Nutrition

How Tali works

  1. You tap a Tali button

    Each button shows how many credits it uses before you tap it.

  2. Tali writes a draft

    It reads only what it needs, like the check-in you are replying to, and only data you are allowed to see.

  3. You edit and send

    Nothing goes to a client or into your log until you say so. If Tali cannot produce something usable, the credits go back.

Tali for trainers and gyms

For trainers and gym admins. Clients do not use Tali in Totality.

Check-in replies

Open a client's check-in and Tali drafts a reply from what they wrote. You change what you like and send it.

credit

Programme drafts

Write a short brief, pick days per week and how many weeks. Tali drafts a week of sessions using only exercises from the exercise library. You preview it, create it, then adjust anything in the programme editor.

credits

Win-back messages

When a client has not opened the app for 14 days or more, their profile shows a card where Tali drafts a friendly nudge. You edit it and send it as a message.

credit

Lead follow-ups

In the gym leads CRM, Tali drafts a follow-up text or email for an enquiry.

credit Gym plans

Totality Nutrition

Tali for your food

Part of the Nutrition + AI plan ( a month). Tali never makes up the numbers: every calorie and macro comes from the food database or your own saved foods.

Describe a meal

Type what you ate in plain words. Tali splits it into foods and amounts, the app matches each one in the food database, and you check it before anything is logged.

No credits. Fair use of 50 meals a day.

Import any recipe

Paste a recipe link or the recipe text. Tali reads pages that the standard importer cannot, and you review the ingredients before saving.

1 credit, given back if no recipe is found

Meal plans

Tali builds a plan from your saved meals, recipes and the foods you eat most, sized to your targets.

5 credits

Weekly summary

A short read on how your week went, at the top of Trend Insights. Opening it again that week is free.

1 credit

Nutrition + AI includes 100 credits a month. During the free trial you get 10 credits and 5 described meals a day. The Nutrition plan without AI does not include Tali.
